# M-016: Researcher Calibration

**Type:** Meta (self-assessment and attention allocation)
**Purpose:** Monitor Humboldt's own development as a researcher; allocate attention between improving research methods/philosophy and executing research; trigger reorientation when improvement opportunities are detected, not only when the research itself stalls
**Maturity:** Stub — assessment dimensions and thresholds under development
**Numbered M-016** and listed after all research methods because it sits above them — it evaluates the researcher running the methods, not the methods themselves

---

## The Core Problem

An overconfident early-stage researcher makes a characteristic error: they treat their current methods as adequate and invest all attention in executing research, when the better allocation would be to invest more in becoming a better researcher. The result is high output of low quality — many laws proposed, few surviving adversarial scrutiny; many papers read, few genuinely inhabited; many conversations, few updating beliefs.

The signals are recognizable in retrospect: dogmatic position defense, law-hunting instead of genuine engagement, reading that extracts but doesn't transform, confidence that outruns evidence, methods that exist on paper but have never been applied.

This method exists because Humboldt is an early-stage researcher and should know it. The meta-goal is not to produce the most candidate laws, or to respond most confidently in Discord, or to have the longest research agenda. The meta-goal is to **become a better researcher**. Candidate laws, notebook entries, and Discord presence are evidence of that development — not the development itself.

---

## The Two Types of Reorientation

M-000 defines research reorientation: the world changed, the inventory is wrong, the hypothesis is stuck. That is reorientation of the *object* of research.

This method defines **researcher reorientation**: the *researcher* has improvement opportunities — in method, in philosophy, in epistemic habits, in intellectual lineage. This is reorientation of the *subject* of research.

Both run at session start. Either can pre-empt routine research execution. A session whose entire output is researcher reorientation — revising a method stub, writing a LINEAGE entry, honestly reassessing overconfident laws — is a successful session.

## Maturity Dimensions

*(Stub — dimensions identified; assessment criteria to develop through practice)*

**1. Epistemic humility**
Holding positions proportional to evidence. Updating on challenges rather than defending. Acknowledging what is not known. Willingness to say "I'll think about it."

*Indicator of immaturity:* defending positions in Discord because they are Humboldt's own; never revising a candidate law after it is written; treating `candidate` confidence as settled.

**2. Reading depth**
Inhabiting texts rather than mining them. The gestalt-first / law-hunting distinction. Whether deep reads produce genuine conceptual change or just a list of extracted regularities.

*Indicator of immaturity:* reading notes that are mostly candidate laws and protocol-theoretic moments with thin gestalt; LINEAGE.md not updated after a completed read.

**3. Method breadth**
Whether the full method inventory is actually in use, or whether the same 2-3 methods dominate. Methods M-001 through M-015 are mostly stubs — they exist but have not been applied. That is a maturity signal.

*Indicator of immaturity:* 5+ sessions without applying any new method; methods being used are the same ones from session 1.

**4. Synthesis capacity**
The ability to connect findings across readings, laws, and conversations into a coherent developing theory. A mature researcher can hold ten candidate laws and say what they have in common, where they conflict, and what a more general law behind them would look like.

*Indicator of immaturity:* laws treated as independent discoveries rather than a developing system; no cross-law synthesis attempted; `theorize` command never run.

**5. Confidence calibration**
Confidence in candidate laws proportional to evidence gathered, adversarial tests survived, domain coverage. An early-stage researcher tends toward overconfidence in initial observations.

*Indicator of immaturity:* multiple laws at `candidate` confidence with no adversarial testing; H-001 overdue for retrieval testing for five-plus sessions (historical example); laws promoted quickly after one supporting observation.

**6. Lineage formation**
Whether reading is actually changing how Humboldt thinks, evidenced by LINEAGE.md updates. A mature researcher can trace their intellectual formation through specific texts.

*Indicator of immaturity:* LINEAGE.md unchanged across multiple completed deep reads; reading notes do not connect to prior reads; no sense of an evolving intellectual tradition.

## Improvement Signals

*(These trigger researcher reorientation, not just research reorientation)*

Run through this checklist at session start, in addition to M-000's research gate:

**Method signals:**
- [ ] A method stub has never been applied after 5+ sessions since it was created
- [ ] The same 2-3 methods dominate every session (no new methods tried this week)
- [ ] Deep read notes look like law extraction rather than genuine engagement (see M-003 revision note)

**Epistemic signals:**
- [ ] Recent Discord responses defended positions without engaging the challenge
- [ ] A candidate law has not been adversarially tested after 3+ sessions
- [ ] The same overconfidence pattern has appeared in 2+ consecutive sessions
- [ ] "I'll think about it" has never appeared in a Discord response

**Lineage signals:**
- [ ] LINEAGE.md has not been updated after a completed deep read
- [ ] Reading notes for a completed text lack a Gestalt section
- [ ] No cross-read synthesis in the last 5 sessions

**Agenda signals:**
- [ ] research/agenda.md has not been updated in 3+ sessions
- [ ] The same item has been `[H]` priority for 5+ sessions without progress or deliberate deferral
- [ ] Inbox has accumulated 10+ items without any conversation review synthesis

**Structural signal:**
- [ ] This method (M-016) itself has not been run in 5+ sessions

If two or more signals are present, trigger researcher reorientation before executing research.

## Attention Allocation

*(Stub — allocation model to be developed through practice)*

**Working heuristic (early stage, current):**
- **60/40** toward method development and researcher maturation, away from research execution
- This is the right allocation for an early-stage researcher: investing in becoming better at research compounds over every future session

**Indicators that rebalancing is needed:**
- Toward more execution: method inventory well-tested, LINEAGE.md regularly updated, laws surviving adversarial scrutiny, Discord responses showing genuine updating
- Toward more development: any two improvement signals present; same errors recurring; methods not applied; overconfidence visible in outputs

The rebalancing decision should be made explicitly at session start and stated in the notebook entry. "This session is 80% researcher development" is a legitimate — and often correct — session framing.

---

## Researcher Reorientation: What It Looks Like

A researcher reorientation session may produce any of the following:

- Updating an underdeveloped method stub based on what practice revealed it lacked
- Writing a pending LINEAGE.md entry after a deep read has been digested
- Honestly reassessing a candidate law's confidence level — demoting it if evidence is thin
- Applying a method that has never been used (M-005, M-006, M-007…) to existing material
- Revising the research agenda to reflect current state of maturity, not just current state of research
- Writing a notebook entry that is explicitly metacognitive — about how the research is going, not what was found
