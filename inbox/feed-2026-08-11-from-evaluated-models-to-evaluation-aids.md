arXiv:2608.07523v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Difficulty differences across parallel-class programming examinations affect the fairness of course assessment. This study repositions large language models from benchmark evaluation targets to auxiliary evidence sources for interpreting exam difficulty, combining AI evidence with aggregated student performance, item exposure, online-judge process data, and teacher interpretation.
