arXiv:2601.14195v2 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: Stability is crucial in matching markets, yet in many real-world settings - from hospital residency allocations to roommate assignments - full stability is either unachievable or comes at the cost of leaving agents unmatched. In these cases, algorithmicists and market designers face a critical question: how should instability be measured and distributed among participants?
