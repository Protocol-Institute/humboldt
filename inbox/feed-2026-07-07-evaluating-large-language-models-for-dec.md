## Summary

arXiv:2607.02716v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Urban mobility modeling faces challenges in representing decision-making in dynamic environments. Although Multi-Agent Systems are widely used, rule-based approaches rely on fixed heuristics that limit adaptive behavior. This work investigates the integration of Large Language Models (LLMs) as decision-making components in multi-agent simulations. We propose a hybrid architecture that connects the GAMA platform to an external LLM-based module through an API, enabling agents to determine whether route replanning is necessary.
