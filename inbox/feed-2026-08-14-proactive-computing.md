Abstract: Computing systems are moving from reactive tools toward systems that sense, interpret, predict, and act before explicit user requests. This transition is enabled by the global scale of mobile connectivity, the rapid expansion of wearable and ambient sensing, advances in machine learning and foundation models, distributed edge infrastructure, and physical actuation.
