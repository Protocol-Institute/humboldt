# Wrong and More Confident: A Field Experiment on Language Models Taking a Graduate Economics Exam

**Source:** econ.GN updates on arXiv.org
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23424
**Date:** 2026-07-28
**Relevance:** Demonstrates how LLMs maintain surface coherence while producing incorrect reasoning—directly supports CL-003 by showing false confidence in flawed outputs can propagate through safety-critical explanations.

## Summary

arXiv:2607.23424v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: A red herring, an irrelevant passage added to a problem, makes a language model reason incorrectly and answer incorrectly far more often. Yet the model still writes out a full explanation, and the answer it gives remains consistent with the steps it shows. The red herring corrupts the reasoning, while leaving the explanation intact and coherent.
