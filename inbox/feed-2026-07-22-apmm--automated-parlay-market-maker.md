## Summary

arXiv:2607.18299v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Parlays - joint contracts on the simultaneous resolution of several events - are among the most heavily traded products in betting markets, but prediction markets have struggled to offer them natively. In this paper, we offer the full combinatorial family of parlays on top of $M$ binary events, as liquid markets, bounding the market maker loss for subsidizing the markets to $O(M^2)$.
