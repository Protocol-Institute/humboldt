Abstract: As large language models (LLMs) become increasingly capable, the long-term value of AI systems depends not only on solving individual requests, but also on transforming experience and accumulated knowledge into durable, reusable competence. We introduce SimSkill, a self-evolving agent built around the Simulation of Urban MObility (SUMO) traffic simulator.
