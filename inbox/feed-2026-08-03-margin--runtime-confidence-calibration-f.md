## Summary

arXiv:2605.22949v3 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: Foundation-model pools are increasingly used as black-box responders in coordinated systems where a coordinator must decide which response to trust. Raw self-reported confidence is the natural signal, but is not comparable across models and becomes stale under distribution shift when corrected only at design time. We study runtime confidence calibration for multi-model coordination, where per-model corrections are learned online from deployment outcomes with no model access, no held-out calibration data, and no retraining.
