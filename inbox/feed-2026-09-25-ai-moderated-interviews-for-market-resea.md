Abstract: AI-moderated interviews are emerging as a scalable market-research method for generating consumer insights and building consumer "digital twins." In a pre-registered, between-subjects study (N = 317) with three industry partners, we compare AI-moderated (N = 139), human-moderated (N = 24), and static interviews (N = 154).
