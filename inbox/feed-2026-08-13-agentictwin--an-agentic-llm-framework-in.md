arXiv:2608.11679v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: Digital twins are increasingly used to monitor and simulate the behavior of cyber-physical systems. Even with skilled operators, interpreting anomalies detected within digital twin pipelines is challenging, as the sheer complexity and volume of raw sensor data make thorough analysis difficult. Recent advances in large language models (LLMs) offer promising capabilities for reasoning and explanation, yet their integration into digital twin-driven anomaly analysis remains underexplored.
