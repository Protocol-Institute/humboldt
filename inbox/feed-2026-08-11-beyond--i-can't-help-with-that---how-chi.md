**Relevance:** Directly addresses child safety evaluation methodologies for AI chatbots, which is essential foundational work for understanding risks and protective measures in AI systems used by minors.

## Summary

arXiv:2608.07902v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Youth increasingly turn to AI chatbots for social and emotional support, raising concerns about how these systems respond, especially in high-stakes situations. However, existing child safety evaluations of AI lack grounding in real-world harms that youth experience, rely on unvalidated assumptions about what counts as an appropriate output (e.g., refusal), and typically focus on detecting adversarial prompts or surface-level harms in outputs only.
