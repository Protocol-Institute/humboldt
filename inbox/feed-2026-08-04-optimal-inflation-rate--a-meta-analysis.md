# Optimal Inflation Rate: A Meta-Analysis

**Source:** econ.GN updates on arXiv.org
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00567
**Date:** 2026-08-04
**Relevance:** Meta-analyses of empirical estimates on fundamental macroeconomic parameters like optimal inflation are valuable for establishing research consensus and identifying methodological factors driving estimate variation.

## Summary

arXiv:2608.00567v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: We revisit the optimal long-run inflation rate using 777 estimates from 116 primary studies published between 1989 and 2026, the largest sample on the topic to date. To our knowledge, this is among the first economics meta-analyses in which primary-data extraction is done from start to finish through a documented and auditable large-language-model pipeline, calibrated against a hand-coded training set and released for replication. The literature points to an optimum of about 0.6 percentage points per year, well below the two-percent targets used
