Abstract: This rapid evidence review examines 89 articles about artificial intelligence (AI) and book publishing published from November 1, 2025 through August 1, 2026. The purposive corpus spans English-, Chinese-, German-, French-, Spanish-, Portuguese-, Italian-, and Japanese-language publishing coverage; major-newspaper book coverage; and specialist technology commentators.
