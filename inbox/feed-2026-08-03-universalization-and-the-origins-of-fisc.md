Abstract: This paper proposes a model of tax compliance and fiscal capacity grounded in universalization reasoning. Citizens partially internalize the consequences of concealment by imagining a world in which everyone acted similarly, linking their compliance decisions to the perceived effectiveness of public spending. A selfish elite chooses between public goods and private rents, taking compliance as given.
