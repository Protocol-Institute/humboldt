Abstract: This paper studies policy learning for distributed task allocation in open multi-agent systems, where agents may join and leave in a time-varying fashion, with submodular stage team utilities. At each time, the active agents select actions from local categorical policies such that the feasible joint agent-action pairs form a partition matroid.
