## Summary

arXiv:2609.12220v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Aggregate performance in smart mobility systems depends heavily on the emergent behavior of selfish, resource-sharing agents that participate within the systems. As a result, recent work has focused on how a system designer can leverage incentives to influence behavior so that system cost (e.g., traffic congestion) is minimized. These results show that worst-case equilibria can be quite inefficient compared to system-optimal allocations.
