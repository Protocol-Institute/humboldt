Abstract: We study the facility location mechanism design problem where $n$ strategic agents report locations in Euclidean space and the mechanism outputs a single facility location. Each agent's cost is its distance from the facility, and our objective is to minimize the egalitarian cost, i.e., the maximum agent cost, in a strategyproof way.
