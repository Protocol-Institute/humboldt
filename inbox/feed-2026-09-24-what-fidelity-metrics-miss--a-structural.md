# What fidelity metrics miss: a structural check on synthetic educational data

**Source:** cs.CY updates on arXiv.org
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27265
**Date:** 2026-09-24
**Relevance:** Directly addresses fidelity metrics and validation of synthetic data quality, relevant to evaluating synthetic datasets used in secondary data analysis.

## Summary

arXiv:2609.27265v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Secondary use of educational records is increasingly mediated by platforms that share a differentially private synthetic version of a dataset and validate specific findings against the real data on request. The synthetic version is evaluated by comparing summary statistics of each variable, yet reported confirmation rates suggest that such comparisons do not predict which findings survive. We propose a structural check: the number of connected components of a weekly proximity graph over learners, tracked across a term. Across four annual cohorts
