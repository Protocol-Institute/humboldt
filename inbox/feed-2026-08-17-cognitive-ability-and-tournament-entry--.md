arXiv:2108.08097v3 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: We compare tournament entry among three Korean groups raised in different institutional environments: South Korea, North Korea, and China. Experiments with a random bonus show that North Korean (NK) refugees enter tournaments less often than South Korean (SK) participants and Korean-Chinese immigrants. The conditional NK-SK difference becomes statistically insignificant when Raven score is included.
