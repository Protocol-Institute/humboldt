## Summary

arXiv:2609.31007v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Large language models can produce fluent prose from short prompts, but direct prompt-to-text interaction gives writers limited access to the assumptions that shape a long narrative. We present Incipit, an implemented research prototype that introduces an explicit planning layer between a writer's intent and generated prose. This layer is grounded in literary axioms, defined as curated and reusable propositions about human experience and narrative craft.
