Abstract: We study a wireless jamming problem consisting of the competition between a legitimate receiver and a jammer, modeled as a zero-sum game where the value to maximize/minimize is the channel capacity at the receiver's side. Most approaches in the literature consider the two players to be stationary nodes. Instead, we investigate what happens when they can change location, specifically moving along a linear geometry.
