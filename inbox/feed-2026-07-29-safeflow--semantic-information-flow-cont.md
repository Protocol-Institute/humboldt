## Summary

arXiv:2607.25255v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Multi-agent systems improve capability through task decomposition and role specialization, but these same mechanisms introduce an important safety blind spot: a harmful objective can be fragmented into locally plausible subtasks, allowing malicious intent to evade detection by any single agent. This is a growing social-impact challenge: systems handling sensitive information or consequential tools can turn routine delegation into unauthorized disclosure or unsafe action.
