Abstract: I study when science starts charging for what reputation used to provide. In the model, AI lowers the cost of producing a polished manuscript faster than it lowers the cost of judging the work behind it. Polish then stops telling good work from plausible work. Entry expands. The willingness to pay for credible certification rises, and once polish fails entirely as a signal of quality, it rises to the full reputational gap between certified and uncertified work.
