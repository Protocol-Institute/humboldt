Abstract: Envy-freeness is a fundamental notion of fairness in the allocation of indivisible goods. In this paper, we study envy-free allocation under Leontief preferences, which model perfect complements. Although Leontief preferences have been extensively studied in the context of allocating divisible goods and market equilibria, they have received comparatively little attention for the allocation of indivisible goods.
