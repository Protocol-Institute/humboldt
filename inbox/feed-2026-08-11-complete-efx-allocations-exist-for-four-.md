# Complete EFX Allocations Exist for Four Additive Agents and Up to Nine Goods

**Source:** cs.GT updates on arXiv.org
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08590
**Date:** 2026-08-11
**Relevance:** Directly advances the state of knowledge on EFX (envy-free up to any good) allocations, a central problem in computational fair division with theoretical and practical importance.

## Summary

arXiv:2608.08590v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: We prove that every fair-division instance with four agents, additive valuations over the non-negative reals, and at most nine indivisible goods admits a \emph{complete} allocation that is envy-free up to any good in the strong, zero-tolerant sense ($\EFXo$). The case $m=9=n+5$ lies beyond the previously known frontier for complete EFX with four agents ($m\le n+3$).
