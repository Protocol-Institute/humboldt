arXiv:2607.13798v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Generative AI tools are increasingly being piloted in public agencies, but limited evidence explains how employee acceptance changes after hands-on use. This study examines Microsoft 365 Copilot adoption during an eight-week pilot at a state Department of Transportation. A matched two-wave survey measured perceived usefulness, perceived ease of use, behavioral intention, and trust before and after participation.
