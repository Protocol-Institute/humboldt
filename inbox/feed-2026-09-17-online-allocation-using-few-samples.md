Abstract: We study online allocation problems where $n$ requests over $m$ resources arrive in an adversarial order and must be served immediately and irrevocably. This framework captures both Online Resource Allocation, where the goal is to maximize value subject to resource budgets, and Online Load Balancing, where the goal is to minimize the makespan. We seek $(1\pm\epsilon)$-competitive algorithms in the large-budget or large-makespan regime.
