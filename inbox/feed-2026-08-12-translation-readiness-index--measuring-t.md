Abstract: Universities, funders, and investors often need to spot research with translational potential early, long before downstream outcomes like licenses, startups, or patents emerge. We introduce the Translation Readiness Index (TRI), a scalable text-based metric that estimates a publication's semantic proximity to patent-linked science using only its title and abstract.
