# Do Humans Bargain Differently with AI? Evidence from Alternating-Offer Games

**Source:** econ.GN updates on arXiv.org
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01212
**Date:** 2026-08-04
**Relevance:** Directly investigates how human bargaining behavior changes when negotiating with AI versus other humans, providing empirical evidence on human-AI economic interaction dynamics.

## Summary

arXiv:2608.01212v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Artificial intelligence increasingly participates in economic interactions not only as a tool, but also as an autonomous bargaining counterpart negotiating on behalf of firms, platforms, and consumers. Yet little is known about how humans respond psychologically and strategically when bargaining with such agents in dynamic settings. We study this question in a laboratory experiment using a three-stage alternating-offer bargaining game in which participants negotiate in real time with either another human or a GPT-based AI agent.
