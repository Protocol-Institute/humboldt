Abstract: What a city makes reachable depends less on what it contains than on who can still afford to move when travel costs rise. We leverage the 2026 US-Iran oil shock as a natural experiment, applying a hierarchical panel regression discontinuity design to 1.7 trillion point-of-interest visits across 122,000 neighbourhoods in China and the United States.
