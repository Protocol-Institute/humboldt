Abstract: Surgical outcomes depend not only on patient factors and postoperative care but are also strongly influenced by the quality of the operation itself. Yet, for much of mod-ern surgery, intraoperative quality has been assessed indirectly through outcomes and operative reports. The increase in minimally invasive procedures inherently guided by endoscopic video, together with advances in artificial intelligence, creates an unprecedented opportunity to systematically observe, measure, and improve surgi-cal care.
