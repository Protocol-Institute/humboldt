Abstract: LLM-based multi-agent embodied planning remains impractical due to prohibitively high execution latency. We identify failed actions as the dominant bottleneck, stemming from two core challenges: inaccurate state tracking under partial observability and inefficient coordination that produces redundant or conflicting actions. We introduce Mosaic, a runtime-efficient multi-agent planning framework that addresses both challenges.
