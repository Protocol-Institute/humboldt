## Summary

arXiv:2608.00679v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: Safety interventions for large populations of network-coupled agents must protect shared constraints without unnecessarily overriding task-oriented policy decisions. We present HetGPS, a hybrid graph-control framework synergizing learned graph risk with physics-anchored correction by separating intervention magnitude from corrective direction. An action-conditioned graph residual model schedules state-dependent intervention authority, while a physics model determines its direction.
