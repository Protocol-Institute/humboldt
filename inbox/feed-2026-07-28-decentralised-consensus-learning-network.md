## Summary

arXiv:2607.24416v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Centralised reward signals dominate modern AI learning systems, but they impose a single external definition of correct or valuable knowledge. We present a decentralised, consensus-based multi-agent learning framework in which expertise emerges through peer validation rather than prescribed reward. Agents update beliefs via weighted social consensus, while trust is allocated according to competence inferred from peer consistency instead of ground truth.
