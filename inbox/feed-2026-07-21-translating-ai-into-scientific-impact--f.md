## Summary

arXiv:2607.16780v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Artificial intelligence (AI) is increasingly embedded in scientific research, but its scientific value is unlikely to be distributed evenly. This study examines how AI knowledge integration is associated with scientific impact and asks who benefits from AI-related knowledge in science. Using large-scale bibliographic data, we measure AI integration through references to papers in the OpenAlex Artificial intelligence subfield and link it to five-year citation impact.
