Abstract: Competing firms increasingly delegate market decisions to algorithms supplied by the same third-party providers. We study whether a shared algorithm leads competitors to internalise one another's profits, using data from the Australian National Electricity Market, where batteries' bids are observed at 5-minute frequency and can be linked to an autobidding provider.
