Abstract: Large multi-call LLM systems pose a scientific problem that query-level routing does not capture: the value of a model depends on where it enters a dependent computation and on the deployment that surrounds that call. Existing routers typically decide \emph{where} to spend a stronger model while treating the benefit of the substitution itself as known.
