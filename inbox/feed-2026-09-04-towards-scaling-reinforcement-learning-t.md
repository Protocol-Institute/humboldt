Abstract: Modern multi-agent systems are increasingly deployed at scale over large populations of agents in settings such as ad-auctions, traffic routing, and recommendation systems. The dominant approach in such settings is to optimize each agent's policy independently, treating the other agents as part of a fixed single-agent environment rather than modeling the population dynamics.
