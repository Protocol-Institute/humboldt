Abstract: We study the best-of-both-worlds fairness in approval-based multi-winner elections, asking whether ex-ante guarantees for a fractional outcome can be implemented while every realized committee satisfies an ex-post representation axiom. Recent work has shown that several ex-ante proportionality guarantees can be achieved together with strong ex-post representation axioms.
