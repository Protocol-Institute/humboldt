Abstract: Lifelong Multi-Agent Path Finding (LMAPF) requires generating collision-free paths for large agent fleets under strict real-time constraints. Reactive frameworks such as PIBT and Enhanced PIBT (EPIBT) scale effortlessly to thousands of agents through rule-based, step-by-step coordination but suffer from severe temporal myopia, making them ineffective in scenarios where long-horizon reasoning is essential.
