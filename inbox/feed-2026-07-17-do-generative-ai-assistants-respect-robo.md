## Summary

arXiv:2607.14447v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: AI assistants increasingly retrieve web content at inference time to provide fresh and grounded answers, yet it remains unclear whether these search-augmented capabilities respect website-owner restrictions expressed through robots.txt. We present a controlled empirical study of ten widely used AI assistants with advertised web-search capabilities. For each assistant, we first identify a configuration that actually produces observable web-browsing behavior and record the user-agent exposed during retrieval.
