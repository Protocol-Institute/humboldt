Abstract: Right to be Forgotten (RTBF) in one of the oldest and prominent of the legal data rights. While its legal intention is straight forward (for example, the GDPR describes it in just 417 words), the computing community has found it challenging to implement this in practice. For example, regulators have issued 205 RTBF violations in the first five years of GDPR i.e., an RTBF failure once every 9 days, on average.
