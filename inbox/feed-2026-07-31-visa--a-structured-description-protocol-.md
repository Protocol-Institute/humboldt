## Summary

arXiv:2607.28027v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Agent-based models (ABMs) are difficult to reproduce: their behavior is spread across prose narratives, platform-specific code, and implicit assumptions, so that two readers routinely reconstruct different models from the same documentation. We present VISA, a structured, symbol-based description protocol that specifies a model in eight interconnected tables---four at the agent level (Agent, Variable, Sensing, Internal Function) and four at the model level (Associated Data, Input/Output, Schedule, Validation)---under the principle of minimality
