## Summary

arXiv:2607.19670v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Large language model (LLM) agents increasingly operate in strategic settings where outcomes depend on the actions of other agents. This raises a reliability question: will a model choose consistently when the same incentives are presented through different narratives? We introduce Same Game, Different Story, a benchmark that defines strategic robustness as invariance of model-induced action distributions under payoff-preserving changes in framing.
