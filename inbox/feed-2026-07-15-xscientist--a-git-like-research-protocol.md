Abstract: Autonomous research systems are often evaluated as one-shot paper generators: given a topic, they produce a manuscript and a small set of experiment logs. This framing hides the operational problem that makes such systems difficult to trust: research is long-running, branching, failure-prone, and dependent on auditable handoffs between agents and humans.
