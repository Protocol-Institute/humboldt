## Summary

arXiv:2607.15381v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: This paper studies what happens when AI gets cheaper, with emphasis on the labor market outcomes, whether it creates formal jobs or whether it pushes workers into informality. We argue that the answer depends on the elasticity of substitution between imported AI capital and formal labor. We build a small open economy DSGE model with a dual labor market, imported AI capital, and country risk, calibrated to an economy where informality is pervasive.
