## Summary

Abstract: Current Large Language Models (LLMs) excel at solving complex mathematical problems, yet this proficiency does not inherently translate into effective tutoring. While advanced LLM tutors may leverage multi-agent frameworks or fine-tuning, most still lack a mechanism to systematically accumulate and reuse pedagogical experience over time, limiting their adaptability to diverse student needs during fluid, multi-turn interactions.
