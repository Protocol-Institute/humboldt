## Summary

arXiv:2607.04543v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Governments are important actors in frontier AI governance, but many facts about their adoption and use of AI systems are difficult to observe directly. Procurement disclosures and official statements are useful, but can also be delayed, selective, and better suited to measuring formal adoption than actual day-to-day use. We propose a complementary monitoring primitive: measuring traces of language-model assistance in public government documents.
