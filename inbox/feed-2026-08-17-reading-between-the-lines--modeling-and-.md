Abstract: Deposition training requires attorneys to manage dynamic witness behavior, yet legal-AI evaluations largely focus on factual accuracy, reasoning, or response-level plausibility. We introduce WitnessSim, a deposition simulator driven by controllable legal personas. We use an evaluation framework separating behavioral realism from pedagogical usefulness.
