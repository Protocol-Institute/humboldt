Abstract: We introduce the problem of designing mechanisms that incentivize strategic agents to form self-funded marketplaces. In our model, if agent $i$ exerts effort $x_i\in [0,1]$, they incur a cost of $x_i\cdot c_i$ (where $c_i$ is unknown to the mechanism designer) and they generate revenue $x_i\cdot r_i$; crucially, $c_i$ can be greater or smaller than $r_i$.
