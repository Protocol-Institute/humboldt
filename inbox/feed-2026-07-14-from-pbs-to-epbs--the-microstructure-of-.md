Abstract: Ethereum's Glamsterdam upgrade introduces enshrined proposer-builder separation (ePBS), replacing relay-centric PBS with direct builder bids to proposers. We study how this shift changes the block-building microstructure through a general imperfect-information two-stage auction with verifiable messages, where an early bid serves as both a price offer and a signal.
