Abstract: We develop and test a model of agentic delegation in software production. Developers face language-specific entry thresholds; conversational AI mainly augments work in languages they already know, while agentic AI adds delegated execution under developer specification and verification. The model predicts an activation band of unfamiliar languages that become feasible only with an agent, expanding the observed language-production frontier of the developer.
