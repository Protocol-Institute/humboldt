Abstract: The critical role of division of labor (DOL) in enhancing cooperation is well-recognized in real-world applications. Consequently, many cooperative multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL) methods have incorporated DOL mechanisms to improve cooperation among agents. However, the lack of benchmark tasks specifically designed to evaluate and promote DOL and cooperation has limited the effective development and deployment of such mechanisms in cooperative MARL.
