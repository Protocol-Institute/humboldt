# Do Female Directors Raise ESG Ratings? A Meta-Analysis

**Source:** econ.GN updates on arXiv.org
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28549
**Date:** 2026-09-25
**Relevance:** Directly addresses the empirical relationship between board gender diversity and ESG performance through systematic synthesis of existing evidence.

## Summary

arXiv:2609.28549v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Appointing more women to corporate boards is widely expected to also raise firms' environmental, social, and governance (ESG) performance. We provide the first meta-analysis of this relationship, drawing on 533 estimates from 106 studies that measure ESG performance with Bloomberg or LSEG ratings. The average reported effect of a one-percentage-point increase in board gender diversity is about 0.28 ESG points, but much of it does not survive scrutiny.
