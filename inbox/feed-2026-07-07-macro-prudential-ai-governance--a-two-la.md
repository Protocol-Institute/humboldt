Abstract: Frontier-AI governance today faces a problem structurally analogous to the one banking regulation faced pre-2008, and which post-2008 reforms (Basel III, Dodd-Frank) have since addressed. Two gaps recur: discovering a risk is not tantamount to acting on it, and individual-model review is unlike managing correlated build-up across the sector. Drawing on the Basel III framework and the U.S. financial-stability architecture, I propose a macro-prudential early warning and response system ("MEWRS") for internal frontier AI.
