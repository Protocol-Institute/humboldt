Abstract: Secondary private equity transactions require rapid synthesis of fragmented, unstructured financial disclosures, where critical metrics and their entity anchors are distributed across disjoint documents with limited lexical overlap. We present Aethel, a reproducible framework that combines bipartite Personalized PageRank graph retrieval with a coreference-aware Bipartite Coreference Teleportation layer and an orchestrated specialist-agent architecture.
