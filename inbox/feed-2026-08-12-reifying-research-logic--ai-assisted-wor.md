Abstract: Quantitative language research often depends on long chains of computational steps, yet the logic connecting those steps usually remains buried in scripts. This makes analyses harder to inspect, share, and revise than they need to be. Focusing on quantitative syntax, we present QLWF, a visual workflow platform that turns natural-language research descriptions into executable workflows through an AI assisted five-stage pipeline.
