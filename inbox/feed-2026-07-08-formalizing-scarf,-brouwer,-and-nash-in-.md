Abstract: We formalize in Lean 4 a complete combinatorial route from Scarf's theorem to Brouwer's fixed point theorem and to the existence of mixed Nash equilibria in finite games. The development follows Ivanov's indexed-order formulation of Scarf's theorem, formalizes the room--door incidence structure and parity argument, instantiates the theorem on finite grids of the standard simplex, and carries out the compactness and continuity argument needed to obtain a fixed point.
