Abstract: This paper introduces and analyzes \emph{defensive rebalancing}, a novel mechanism for protecting constant-function market makers (CFMMs) from value leakage due to arbitrage. A \emph{rebalancing} transfers assets directly from one CFMM's pool to another's, bypassing the CFMMs' standard trading protocols. In any \emph{arbitrage-prone} configuration, we prove there exists a rebalancing to an \textit{arbitrage-free} configuration that strictly increases some CFMMs' liquidities without reducing the liquidities of the others.
