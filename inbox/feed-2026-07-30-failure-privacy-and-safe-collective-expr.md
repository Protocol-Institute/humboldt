Abstract: Controversial views sometimes remain unspoken because they invite retaliation. However, a sufficiently large group could speak safely if only they spoke together. Speaking one-by-one may encourage others, but retaliation against early participants can stop the cascade before a protective group forms. A social assurance contract privately collects signed commitments and publishes them only when all signers will be safe.
