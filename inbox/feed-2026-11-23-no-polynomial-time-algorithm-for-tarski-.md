Abstract: We study the time complexity of finding a Tarski fixed point. We provide a subexponential-time reduction from the Unique End of Potential Line (UEOPL) problem to the problem of finding a Tarski fixed point. Using a range of known cryptographic lower bounds for UEOPL, our reduction implies a range of conditional lower bounds on the running time of any algorithm that finds a Tarski fixed point.
