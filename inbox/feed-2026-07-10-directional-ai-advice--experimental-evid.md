## Summary

arXiv:2607.08706v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Generative AI is fast becoming the first place people turn for expert advice. The advice it provides can be directional rather than neutral, shaped in part by the choices of its designers and regulators. When clients consult AI before meeting an expert, they carry this directional advice into a relationship that once rested on the expert's judgment alone.
