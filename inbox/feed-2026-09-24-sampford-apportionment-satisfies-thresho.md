Abstract: Apportionment distributes a fixed number of legislative seats among political parties in proportion to their vote shares. Randomization can satisfy quota in every realization and exact proportionality in expectation, but these requirements determine only the parties' marginal seat distributions. We study threshold monotonicity, which requires that when every standard quota inside a coalition weakly increases and every standard quota outside it weakly decreases, the coalition's new seat total stochastically dominates its old one.
