# Building an Atlas of Social Experiments to Link Studies, Reconcile Conflicts, and Bridge Gaps

**Source:** cs.CY updates on arXiv.org
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27153
**Date:** 2026-05-27
**Relevance:** Directly addresses systematization of social science experimental findings, enabling synthesis across studies and identification of conflicts—core infrastructure for cumulative social science knowledge.

## Summary

arXiv:2605.27153v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Social and behavioral science runs thousands of experiments each year, yet their findings rarely accumulate into a coherent map of what is known, what conflicts, and what remains missing. We introduce ExAtlas, a framework for turning an archive of experiments into an atlas: a structured map in which studies link, conflict, or leave bridgeable gaps. Given a target study, ExAtlas searches for prior studies that are locally close in treatment and outcome space and asks whether their observed effects can be composed to predict the target effect.
