## Summary

arXiv:2608.14018v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: We study equilibrium pricing in oligopolistic data markets with budget-constrained buyers (e.g., machine learning companies purchasing data to improve model accuracy) and strategic data sellers. Sellers compete by setting prices for their datasets, giving rise to a pricing game whose pure Nash equilibria correspond to equilibrium prices.
