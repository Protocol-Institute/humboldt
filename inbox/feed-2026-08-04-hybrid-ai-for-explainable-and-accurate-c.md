Abstract: We present a so-called Conversational Hybrid AI (CHAI) architecture for building explainable and accurate conversational agents for eGovernment. We exemplify the architecture with a running prototype of a Covid-19 Chatbot based on a governmental guideline directed to citizens. We also describe an ongoing case on case management for supplementary grants for students with disabilities.
