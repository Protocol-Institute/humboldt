**Date:** 2026-06-26
**Relevance:** Direct empirical evidence of how agentic systems alter human coordination patterns and trust dynamics, testing CL-002 and CL-003 through real-world behavioral data.

## Summary

arXiv:2606.26959v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: We analyze usage data from OpenAI's Codex tool to present large-scale evidence of how agentic AI technology, which can take actions on a user's behalf, changes how people work. We use an automated, privacy-protecting pipeline to contrast usage across three populations: external personal-account users, external organizational-account users, and workers within OpenAI.
