arXiv:2607.25019v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: This paper studies the long-run alignment of interactive agents, including AI systems, teams, firms, and governments, with human welfare. It develops a farming game in which a population of agents makes planting, trading, and expansion decisions. Agents must allocate final output between transfers to humans and investment in their own expansion. Because transfers to humans reduce the resources available for expansion, evolutionary forces tend to select against aligned behavior.
