Abstract: Tipping-point dynamics describe the critical conditions under which a committed minority drives a population to abandon an established convention in favor of a new one. We present a transparent agent-based model of this process, in which agents hold one of two behavioral states and a mobile committed minority attempts to overturn the incumbent convention.
