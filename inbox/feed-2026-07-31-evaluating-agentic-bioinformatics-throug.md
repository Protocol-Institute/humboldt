## Summary

arXiv:2607.27556v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: Large language model agents increasingly plan, execute, and interpret biological analyses, yet fluent responses, successful tool calls, and benchmark performance alone do not establish scientific credibility. Existing reviews primarily organize biological agents by application, architecture, and agentic capability, but do not jointly operationalize the accountability of agent-generated workflows. We address this gap by treating the inspectable workflow trajectory, rather than architecture or final output alone, as the primary unit of analysis.
