Abstract: Multi-robot coordination methods often score a joint plan from singleton and pairwise terms, leaving out the terms that involve three or more robots. We measure the plan-selection regret of two pairwise approximations to delivered coverage using frozen multi-robot trajectories. For each four-robot plan on an indoor exploration benchmark, replaying all 16 robot subsets gives the exact delivered-coverage set function $F$.
