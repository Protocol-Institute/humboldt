arXiv:2606.25909v2 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: We construct an analytical general equilibrium model of an economy with carbon offsets, and show that increasing the carbon offset price has an ambiguous effect on aggregate emissions and welfare. Using two carbon accounting metrics, we demonstrate that offsets are over-credited under many parameterizations; however, offset under-crediting can also occur.
