Abstract: We study the fine-grained complexity of computing approximate Nash equilibria and approximating the value of free games in the regime where the approximation error vanishes. Under the PCP for PPAD and ETH for PPAD conjectures, we show that computing $\varepsilon$-approximate Nash equilibria in 2-player $N$-action normal-form games requires time $N^{(\log(N)/\varepsilon^2)^{1-o(1)}}$, thus showing that the classical Lipton-Markakis-Mehta algorithm (2003) is optimal through all regimes of $\varepsilon = \omega(1/\sqrt{N})$.
