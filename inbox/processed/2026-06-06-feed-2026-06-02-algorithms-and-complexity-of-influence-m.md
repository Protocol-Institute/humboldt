Abstract: This paper investigates the influence maximization problem under the Independent Cascade(IC) and Linear Threshold (LT) models. While this problem is known to be APX-hard on general graphs, we explore its computational limits by focusing on Directed Acyclic Graphs (DAGs) and more restricted tree structures. Our primary result demonstrates that influence maximization remains APX-hard on DAGs under the LT model, suggesting that the absence of cycles is insufficient to achieve a polynomial-time approximation scheme (PTAS).
