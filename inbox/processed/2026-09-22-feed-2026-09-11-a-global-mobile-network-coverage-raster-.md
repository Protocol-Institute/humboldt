# A global mobile network coverage raster product at 1km resolution, 1999--2030

**Source:** cs.CY updates on arXiv.org
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11320
**Date:** 2026-09-11
**Relevance:** Provides spatiotemporal global mobile network coverage data at fine resolution, enabling research on digital inequality, economic access, and infrastructure development.

## Summary

arXiv:2609.11320v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Where a mobile signal is available shapes who can work, learn, bank, seek health care and respond to crises in the digital age, yet no globally consistent, sub-national record of mobile network coverage exists. We present such a record: annual 1km maps of the probability of 2G, 3G and 4G coverage for 214 countries and territories for the years 1999 to 2030.
