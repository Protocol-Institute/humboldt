Abstract: This study provides a method to quantify the benefits of shifting passenger traffic from air to high-speed rail from the perspective of flight-delay cost reduction. We first estimate the number of flight reductions for airport origin-destination pairs based on the high-speed rail ridership forecasts provided in the California High-Speed Rail 2020 Business Plan, and then distribute these flight reductions to quarter-hour intervals.
