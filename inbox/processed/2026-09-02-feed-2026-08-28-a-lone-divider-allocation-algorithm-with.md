# A lone divider allocation algorithm with subjective divisibility

**Source:** cs.GT updates on arXiv.org
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26801
**Date:** 2026-08-28
**Relevance:** Directly addresses fair allocation algorithms and divisibility models, which is foundational research for resource distribution problems.

## Summary

arXiv:2608.26801v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: In the subjective divisibility allocation model, all goods are divisible, every agent $i$ has a non-negative additive valuation function $v_i$, where for some goods $v_i$ is additive over fractions of the good, and for some goods, any fraction smaller than~1 has value~0. Previous work established the existence of $\frac{5}{9}$-MMS allocations in this setting. We show that $\frac{3}{5}$-MMS allocations exist. Our proof is based on an adaptation of the lone divider framework.
