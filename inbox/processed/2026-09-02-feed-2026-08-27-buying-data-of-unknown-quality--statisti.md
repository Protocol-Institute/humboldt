Abstract: We study statistical parameter estimation in the setting of data markets when providers differ in both provision costs and estimation-relevant data quality. We define a cost-per-information score that summarizes each provider's provision cost per unit of information about the buyer's estimation objective. When quality is known ex ante, we describe a second-score procurement mechanism that ranks providers by this score, and endogenously chooses both a provider and a sample size while making truthful cost reports optimal.
