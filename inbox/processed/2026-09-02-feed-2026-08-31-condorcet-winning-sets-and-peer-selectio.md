## Summary

arXiv:2608.27653v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: In ranked-choice voting, a Condorcet-winning set is a group of candidates for which no outside candidate is preferred to every member of the group by a majority of voters. We study Condorcet-winning sets in planar metric elections, where voters rank candidates according to their distance under a given norm. We formulate general metric elections, peer selection, and the one-round Voronoi game as instances of a two-player Stackelberg game and place these problems in a common hierarchy.
