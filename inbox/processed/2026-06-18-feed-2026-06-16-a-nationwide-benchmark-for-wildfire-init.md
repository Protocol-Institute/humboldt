Abstract: Initial attack (IA) is the first wildfire suppression phase, when agencies must quickly decide which fires may escape early control. Existing IA failure prediction studies often use non-public response records or regional settings, so it remains unclear how well public data available at fire discovery time can support IA failure prediction at national scale.
