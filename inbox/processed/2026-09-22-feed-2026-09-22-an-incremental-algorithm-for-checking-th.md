Abstract: Braess paradox is a well-known phenomenon that originates when latency at Wardrop equilibrium in traffic networks decreases because of removing edges. The possibility of having the paradox was called vulnerability by Roughgarden in 2006 and was characterized later on by graph-theoretical notions, both for undirected and for directed graphs. In this paper we provide an algorithm for the incremental case of checking vulnerability for dynamically evolving graphs.
