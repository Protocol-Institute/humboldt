Abstract: The communication demands of distributed model prediction control (DMPC) can overwhelm even advanced wireless communication technologies as agents must exchange a significant amount of information at least once per time step. To semantically reduce communication demands, this work employs encoder-decoder networks built around long-short term memory (LSTM) cells in a distributed optimization algorithm.
