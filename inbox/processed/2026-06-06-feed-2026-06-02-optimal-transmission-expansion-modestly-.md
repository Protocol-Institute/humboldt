## Summary

arXiv:2402.14189v4 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: Major government studies and policy reports project that substantial expansion of interregional transmission will be needed to integrate clean energy and ensure reliability in decarbonized power systems. Using the open-source Switch capacity expansion model with detailed representation of existing U.S. generation and transmission infrastructure, solar, wind, and storage resources, and hourly operations, we evaluate the role of interregional transmission across least-cost, carbon-priced, and zero-emissions scenarios for 2050.
