## Summary

arXiv:2609.12929v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: Dementia affects an estimated 57 million people worldwide, and for most families the hardest part of care is not memory loss but the behavioral and psychological symptoms of dementia (BPSD): agitation, wandering, resistance to care, sundowning. Understanding these symptoms requires more than recognizing the behavior itself; it also requires knowing what happened beforehand.
