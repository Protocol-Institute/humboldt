**Relevance:** Directly evaluates cost-benefit tradeoffs of advanced LLM reasoning capabilities for a specific corporate disclosure task, providing empirical evidence on frontier model value proposition.

## Summary

arXiv:2606.13693v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Automated scoring of ESG narrative disclosures with large language models (LLMs) is gaining traction, yet whether reasoning-heavy frontier models add value commensurate with their cost remains empirically unsettled. We evaluate this question on a corpus of ten Japanese listed firms across three rubric axes -- quantitative targets, progress-tracking infrastructure, and external-standard alignment -- using a four-model consensus design that combines a reasoning-on frontier model with three reasoning-off contemporaries.
