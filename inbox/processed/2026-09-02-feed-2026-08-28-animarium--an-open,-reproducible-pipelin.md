# Animarium: an open, reproducible pipeline for synthetic populations of Italian cities, from ISTAT sources to open data (Tech Report v1)

**Source:** cs.CY updates on arXiv.org
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27111
**Date:** 2026-08-28
**Relevance:** This describes a reproducible pipeline for generating synthetic population data from Italian administrative sources, which is methodologically relevant for studies involving synthetic data generation, census data processing, or agent-based modeling applications.

## Summary

arXiv:2608.27111v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Synthetic populations of eleven Italian municipalities (1,814,317 individuals in 887,937 households) generated from published aggregates alone: ISTAT census and register tables, census-section counts, the national civic-address register, public-use survey microdata, and six municipal open-data portals, every source certified in a registry with licence, fingerprint and declared affordances.
