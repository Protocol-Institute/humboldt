## Summary

arXiv:2606.03697v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Integrated Circuits (ICs) are omnipresent, yet their globalized manufacturing process remains vulnerable to supply chain threats. Hardware Reverse Engineering (HRE) is essential for detecting such threats and re-establishing trust; however domain experts remain scarce due to a lack of educational programs. To contribute educational insights in this critical and rapidly evolving technology domain, we present our HRE course focusing on digital circuit analysis and digital circuit extraction from ICs. The course targets junior-level undergraduates
