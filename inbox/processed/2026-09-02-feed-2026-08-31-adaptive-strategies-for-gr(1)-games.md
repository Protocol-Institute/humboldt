Abstract: We consider two-player GR(1) games on graphs, where the system player Eve must satisfy \[ \Box\Diamond A_1\land\cdots\land\Box\Diamond A_m \;\implies\; \Box\Diamond G_1\land\cdots\land\Box\Diamond G_n \] against the environment player Adam. Here $A_1,\ldots,A_m$ are assumptions on the environment, $G_1,\ldots,G_n$ are guarantees the system must provide, and $\Box\Diamond S$ denotes ``always eventually $S$''.
