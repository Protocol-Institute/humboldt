Abstract: We introduce Aumann-SHAP, an interaction-aware framework that decomposes counterfactual transitions by restricting the model to a local hypercube connecting baseline and counterfactual features. Each hypercube is discretized into a grid to construct an induced micro-player cooperative game in which elementary grid-step moves become players.
