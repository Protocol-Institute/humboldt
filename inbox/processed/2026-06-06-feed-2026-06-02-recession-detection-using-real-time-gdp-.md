arXiv:2606.00989v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: This paper examines whether real-time GDP announcements can reliably identify business-cycle turning points. Using U.S. real-time GDP vintages from 1947 to 2021, we construct 4,356 recession indicators based on alternative smoothing methods and scaling variations. We then combine these indicators with alternative thresholds to generate 137,457 perfect recession classifiers.
