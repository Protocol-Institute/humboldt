# Preference robust distortion risk measures

**Source:** econ.GN updates on arXiv.org
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02854
**Date:** 2026-08-05
**Relevance:** Directly addresses robustness in risk measurement under preference uncertainty, a foundational topic for decision-making under ambiguity in finance and operations.

## Summary

arXiv:2608.02854v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: We introduce a framework for preference-robust decision making when preferences over risk are modelled through generalised distortion risk measures. Unlike distributional robustness, our approach addresses ambiguity in the risk functional itself. We construct ambiguity sets on distortion (weight) functions using the Wasserstein distance and Bregman divergences, and derive closed-form expressions for the worst- and best-case distortion risk measures. We further extend the framework to rank-dependent utility, yielding preference-robust behaviour
