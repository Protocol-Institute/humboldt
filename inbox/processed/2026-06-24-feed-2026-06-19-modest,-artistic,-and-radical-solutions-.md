Abstract: Machine learning is often touted to improve the efficiency of ICT, but that small gain is overwhelmed by the enormous carbon, water, and land footprints of data centers and ML-ready devices. We survey the electricity consumption of ML applications in training and inference, focusing on electricity-intensive image generation.
