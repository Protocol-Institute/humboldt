## Summary

arXiv:2608.04017v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: The rise of open and distance education has made it more important than ever to have communication tools that are simple, flexible, and good for helping students work together, talk to each other, and feel connected. Researchers have already looked at how instant messaging apps like WhatsApp and Telegram can be used for learning. Viber, on the other hand, has not been studied as much, especially when it comes to its use in higher education at a distance.
