## Summary

arXiv:2606.10226v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Value-at-risk (VaR) and conditional value-at-risk (CVaR) are widely used in risk-aware optimization and equilibrium models. When the loss depends on a decision variable, the induced distribution, the VaR threshold, and the CVaR tail set all change with the decision. This makes the regularity of the VaR and CVaR maps nontrivial. We give simple sufficient conditions under which the VaR map is continuous and the corresponding CVaR map is continuously differentiable.
