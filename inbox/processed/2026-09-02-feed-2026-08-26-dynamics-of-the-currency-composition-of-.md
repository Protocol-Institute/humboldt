## Summary

arXiv:2608.24468v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: We examine how macroeconomic and geopolitical developments in the United States, the euro area, and China affect the currency composition of central banks' foreign exchange reserves. Using an unbalanced panel of reserve shares for 53 countries over 1999-2023, we estimate a constrained system of equations that explicitly imposes the adding-up restriction on reserve shares.
