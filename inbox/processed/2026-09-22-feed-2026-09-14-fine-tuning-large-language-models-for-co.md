## Summary

arXiv:2608.10276v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: Student-generated metaphors about mathematics can provide insights into students' attitudes, beliefs, identities, and experiences, but expert human assessment through thematic coding of these semantically complex metaphor responses is labor-intensive and difficult to scale. This study examines whether Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA)-based supervised fine-tuning of Large Language Models (LLMs) can improve their performance on codebook-guided coding tasks for student mathematics metaphors.
