# Position: Align AI to Our Aspirations, Not Our Flaws

**Source:** cs.CY updates on arXiv.org
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13755
**Date:** 2026-06-15
**Relevance:** Directly addresses AI alignment methodology and value specification, challenging a mainstream assumption about preference aggregation as the alignment target.

## Summary

arXiv:2606.13755v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: We argue that aligning AI to aggregated human preferences is the wrong target. With current technology, one can train AIs to share the values of a Silicon Valley techno-optimist, a degrowth environmentalist, a national-conservative culture warrior, a single-party state cadre, or a devout religious traditionalist. We should not. Human values produce societies that thrive or fail on the merits of those values - from failed states and extreme inequality to declining happiness, political polarization, and government dysfunction in the world's wealth
