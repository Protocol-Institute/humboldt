Abstract: In this research-to-practice paper we present a survey that can be used to assess students' AI knowledge. As the use of artificial intelligence (AI), including generative artificial intelligence (GenAI), has proliferated, so has the need to educate students about the topic. A range of AI literacy frameworks have been proposed, outlining the essential knowledge that students should have. Alongside, different ways of assessing AI knowledge have been developed.
