Abstract: This paper studies whether interruptions to organized breast cancer screening lead to greater later use of emergency hospital care. It focuses on the first wave of COVID-19, when routine mammography was widely reduced across Europe, disrupting the usual screening pathway of early detection, follow-up testing, referral, and planned treatment. Using SHARE data from eight countries, the authors examine women aged 50 to 69, the main target group for organized screening programs.
