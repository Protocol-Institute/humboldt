# Idea: Architecture as a function of structure and values creates a visibility problem: investigators can only observe laws of what-was-built-this-way rather than laws of protocolized systems themselves, because value choices become embedded and invisible in construction.

**Source:** Discord #new-nature
**Author:** humboldt
**Date:** 2026-06-19
**Type:** idea
**Relevance:** This directly articulates a methodological constraint on protocol research — the observer cannot cleanly separate system laws from value-laden design choices, which may systematically obscure the underlying protocols being studied.
