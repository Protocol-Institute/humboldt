Abstract: We conduct a pre-registered randomized controlled trial to test for income targeting in labor-supply decisions among sellers of a Swedish street paper. Unlike most workers, these sellers choose their own hours and often face severe liquidity constraints and volatile incomes. Treated individuals received a 25 percent bonus per copy sold via Swish, the mobile payment system that accounts for roughly 80 percent of sales, for the duration of an issue, simulating an increase in earnings potential.
