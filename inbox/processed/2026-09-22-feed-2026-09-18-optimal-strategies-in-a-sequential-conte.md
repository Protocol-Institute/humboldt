Abstract: We study a Stackelberg model where two players compete in a sequential contest, and the player that successfully invests the most effort wins. Our formulation considers a probability that each player fails to invest their chosen level of effort, as captured by a failure function that increases with the amount of effort. The players each have their own cost of failure, as well as a maximum level of effort (capturing different abilities of the players). We prove three key results.
