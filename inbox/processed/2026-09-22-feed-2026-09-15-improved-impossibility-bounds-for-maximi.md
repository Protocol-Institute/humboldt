arXiv:2609.15085v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: The maximin share (MMS) is a central fairness benchmark for allocating indivisible items, but it need not be simultaneously attainable even under additive preferences. While extensive work has developed approximation guarantees, quantitative impossibility bounds have received comparatively little attention. We establish improved asymptotic and constant impossibility bounds for both goods and chores.
