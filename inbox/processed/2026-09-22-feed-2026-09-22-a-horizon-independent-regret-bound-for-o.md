## Summary

arXiv:2609.22839v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Can simple learning rules keep their regret bounded in self-play? Recent work achieves constant regret bounds through modified regularization and higher-order prediction. Yet for Optimistic Hedge, arguably the most canonical method in games, the best known individual regret bound remains logarithmic. In this work, we prove that plain Optimistic Hedge with a constant step size can attain $O_{n,d}(1)$ individual regret in general-sum games with $n$ players and $d=(d_1,\ldots,d_n)$ actions, under expected loss-vector feedback.
