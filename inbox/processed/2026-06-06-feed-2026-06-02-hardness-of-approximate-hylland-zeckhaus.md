# Hardness of Approximate Hylland-Zeckhauser Equilibria

**Source:** cs.GT updates on arXiv.org

**Date:** 2026-06-02
**Relevance:** Directly investigates computational complexity of CEEI mechanisms, a fundamental market equilibrium concept in algorithmic game theory and fair division.

## Summary

arXiv:2606.00951v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: In this paper, we investigate the computational hardness of finding fractional allocations to unit-demand players using competitive equilibria from equal incomes (CEEI), where we allow a small constant error in players' response to market prices (also known as an approximate Hylland-Zeckhauser equilibrium). We show that assuming the $\mathbf{(\varepsilon,\delta)}$-Generalized Circuits problem is PPAD-hard (the "PCP for PPAD" conjecture), finding an approximate HZ equilibrium is also PPAD-hard.
