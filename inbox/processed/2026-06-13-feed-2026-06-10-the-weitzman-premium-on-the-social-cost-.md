## Summary

arXiv:2502.01394v2 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: Preference heterogeneity massively increases the social cost of carbon. We call this the Weitzman premium. Uncertainty about an exponential discount rate implies a hyperbolic discount rate, which in the near term is equal to the average discount rate but in the long term falls to the minimum discount rate. We generalise Weitzman's (2001, AER) gamma discounting to zero-inflation and two dimensions but find that the analytical solution is a poor approximation of the non-parametric heterogeneity.
