Abstract: We consider fair allocation of indivisible items among agents with non-negative and additive valuations. The goal is to construct a lottery over deterministic allocations whose induced fractional allocation is envy-free, while every realised allocation is envy-free up to one item and Pareto optimal. We show that this is always possible for two agents.
