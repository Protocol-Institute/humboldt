Abstract: Alternating-time temporal logic and its extensions provide several ways of combining strategic and quantitative reasoning. We study a particular combination: whether a coalition has a single strategy that enforces a temporal objective while guaranteeing given long-run mean-payoff thresholds. We introduce ATL*_mp, an extension of ATL* over weighted concurrent game structures in which each strategic modality carries a conjunctive mean-payoff constraint.
