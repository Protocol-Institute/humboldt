## Summary

arXiv:2605.11180v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: We show that the total value of information to informed traders can be measured by the covariation between price changes and order flow. This covariation captures noise trader losses, which equal informed trader gains when market making is competitive. We estimate the value of information using high frequency data on US equities at $3.5 million per year.
