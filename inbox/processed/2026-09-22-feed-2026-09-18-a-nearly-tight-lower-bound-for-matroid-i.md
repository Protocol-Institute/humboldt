# A Nearly Tight Lower Bound for Matroid Intersection Prophet Inequalities

**Relevance:** Directly addresses prophet inequalities for constrained online selection problems, a core topic in algorithmic game theory and online optimization.

## Summary

arXiv:2609.20696v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: We study prophet inequalities under intersections of $q$ partition matroids, where an online algorithm irrevocably selects elements with independent nonnegative values drawn from known distributions and revealed in an adversarial order. We prove an $\Omega(q/\log q)$ lower bound on the competitive ratio.
