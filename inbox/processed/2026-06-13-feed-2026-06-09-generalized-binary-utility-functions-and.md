Abstract: The problem of finding envy-free allocations of indivisible goods can not always be solved; therefore, it is common to study some relaxations such as envy-free up to one good (EF1). Another property of interest for efficiency of an allocation is the Pareto Optimality (PO). Under additive utility functions, it is possible to find allocations EF1 and PO using Nash social welfare.
