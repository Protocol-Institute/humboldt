# Why Customer Choice Models Matter

**Source:** cs.GT updates on arXiv.org
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10557
**Date:** 2026-09-11
**Relevance:** Customer choice models directly impact revenue management policy effectiveness in attended home delivery, a core operational optimization problem.

## Summary

arXiv:2609.10557v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Customer choice models are central to revenue management in attended home delivery, yet the field usually picks one without much thought. Does the choice model matter? We believe it does, and this paper shows that revenue management policies judged by their own assumed model, or trusted under a single set of parameters, can be worse than they look.
