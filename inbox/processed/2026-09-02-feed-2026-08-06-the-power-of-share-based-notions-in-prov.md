Abstract: We study the problem of fairly allocating indivisible goods among agents with monotone valuations. We introduce a new share-based fairness notion, the residual maximin share (RMMS), and show that it provides a unified framework for several existing lone-divider style techniques in fair division. RMMS satisfies two key properties: feasibility and self-maximization.
