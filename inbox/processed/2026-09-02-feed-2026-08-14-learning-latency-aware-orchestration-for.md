Abstract: Multi-agent systems (MAS) coordinate multiple LLM-powered agents through structured workflows, gaining reasoning power but incurring high inference latency from multi-step execution and repeated model invocations. Existing orchestration methods primarily optimize task performance and inference cost, leaving latency largely unaddressed. In MAS, end-to-end latency is governed by the \textit{critical execution path}, so reducing total cost alone does not reliably reduce latency.
