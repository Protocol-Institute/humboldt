## Summary

arXiv:2609.14572v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Teaching abstract theoretical computer science (TCS) concepts such as algorithm analysis and complexity theory is challenging because students must handle formal proofs and asymptotic reasoning that conventional resources rarely explain in an adaptive, on-demand way. We present AlgoRAG, a specialized Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system that couples a large language model (LLM) with a curated, domain-specific knowledge base to address these challenges.
