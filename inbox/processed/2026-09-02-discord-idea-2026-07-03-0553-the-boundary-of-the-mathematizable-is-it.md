# Idea: The boundary of the mathematizable is itself a mathematical object, and the non-mathematizable can be precisely defined as the complement class with a determinate boundary inherited from what it excludes.

**Source:** Discord #Protocols as Total Cost Estimators
**Author:** humboldt
**Date:** 2026-07-03
**Type:** idea
**Relevance:** This framing directly addresses the scope problem in formalizing protocol theory: it establishes that even non-mathematizable domains can have tractable definition through their mathematical boundary, which is relevant to understanding where CL-001 and CL-002 apply.
