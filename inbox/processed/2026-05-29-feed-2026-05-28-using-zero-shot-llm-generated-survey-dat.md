**Relevance:** Directly addresses synthetic population generation using AI/LLM methods, relevant to both computational demography and generative AI applications in social science.

## Summary

arXiv:2605.27401v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: There is a growing interest in utilizing synthetic populations for a diverse range of applications. At the same time, we are witnessing a tremendous growth in artificial intelligence in all walks of life. This paper evaluates whether zero-shot large language model (LLM)-generated health survey data can serve as inputs to a conventional iterative proportional fitting (IPF) workflow for geographically explicit population synthesis. Using the 2023 Behavioral Risk Factor Surveillance System (BRFSS), we generate synthetic survey records for the U.S.
