## Summary

arXiv:2609.12387v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Using a balanced panel of 129 countries from 2000 to 2024, we estimate the macroeconomic costs of sanctions on Russia. To that end, we employ a novel two-wave common-correlated-effects imputation design that separates the 2014 sanctions from the 2022 escalation and accounts for heterogeneous exposure to global shocks. We find the main boomerang effect of these sanctions operates through prices rather than output.
