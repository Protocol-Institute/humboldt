## Summary

arXiv:2605.19302v2 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: We study strategic interaction in data-driven games where players face uncertainty about payoff distributions inferred from finite samples. To model calibrated attitudes toward such uncertainty, we formulate distributionally robust games with a special focus on coherent utility (risk) measures, including Mean-semideviation and Conditional Value-at-Risk.
