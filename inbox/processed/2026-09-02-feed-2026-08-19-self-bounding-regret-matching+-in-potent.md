Abstract: Regret matching+ (RM+) is parameter free, scale invariant, and central to large game solving, but its only general individual-regret guarantee grows as $\sqrt{T}$. A recent ICLR result used this envelope to prove that RM+ reaches an $\epsilon$-stationary point of a smooth objective over a product of simplices in $O(\epsilon^{-4})$ iterations, or $O(\epsilon^{-8})$ from the standard zero initialization.
