# Bad News for Couples: Bounds for Fair Division of Indivisible Items among Groups

## Summary

arXiv:2601.01012v2 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: We consider the problem of fairly allocating indivisible items to couples, where each couple consists of two agents with distinct additive valuations. We show that there exist binary instances with $n$ agents partitioned into $n/2$ couples for which envy-freeness up to $\Omega(\sqrt{n})$ items cannot be guaranteed. More generally, in the group-allocation model with $n$ agents partitioned into $k$ groups, we construct binary instances for which envy-freeness up to $\Omega(\sqrt{n-k})$ items cannot be guaranteed.
