arXiv:2609.22651v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: We study online allocation with convex costs (\OACC), a generalization of limited-supply models in which additional resources can be produced dynamically at convex cost. Prior work largely focuses on constructing a single algorithm that achieves strong competitive guarantees. In contrast, our main contribution is a structural characterization of the optimal design space of reserve functions (i.e., normalized pricing rules) for \OACC, yielding a family of optimal online algorithms.
