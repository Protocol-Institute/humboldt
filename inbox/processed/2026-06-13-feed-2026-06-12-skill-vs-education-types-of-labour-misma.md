Abstract: This paper analyses the distinction between educational and skill types of labour mismatch and their association with earnings. Drawing on cross-sectional data for 26 countries from the 1st Cycle of the OECD (2012) Survey of Adult Skills (PIAAC), I examine educational and skill mismatch using a comprehensive set of education- and skill-based indicators, explore heterogeneity across worker characteristics, and investigate the sources of conflicting country-level correlations with earnings through an error components model.
