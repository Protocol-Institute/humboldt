## Summary

arXiv:2608.16896v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: This paper presents the design and outcomes of a seven-weekend AI storytelling program developed for Black girls aged 10-12. Grounded in Afrofuturism and Black feminist thought, the program adopted AI-enabled counter-storytelling, supported the development of foundational AI literacies, and fostered future-oriented imagination. Activities included brainstorming AI-related topics, developing character and story plots, and delivering collaborative group presentations.
