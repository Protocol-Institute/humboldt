Abstract: Reinforcement learning (RL) theory fundamentally depends on probability theory through the Markov chain. There is a deep connection between probability theory and potential theory. This paper reviews that connection and explores the potential-theoretic viewpoint for core reinforcement learning representations and algorithms under a fixed-policy assumption.
