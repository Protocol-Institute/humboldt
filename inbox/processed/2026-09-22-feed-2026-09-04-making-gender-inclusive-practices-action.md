## Summary

arXiv:2609.03936v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: The persistent gender imbalance in computing remains a global concern, and universities offer a key part of the pipeline to address it. Although research has identified practices that support under-represented student groups, translating this evidence into actionable guidance remains challenging. This paper first presents a novel web- based toolkit (TechMate) designed to address this gap by helping computing educators implement gender- inclusive initiatives through practical research-informed guidance.
