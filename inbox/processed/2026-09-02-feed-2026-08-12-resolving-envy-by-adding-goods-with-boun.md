Abstract: We study envy elimination by adding goods (EEAG) when the additional pool has bounded supply and no separate budget bound. We establish a sharp type-count dichotomy for binary additive valuations. With one additional item type, EEAG is polynomial-time solvable for any number of agents. More generally, our algorithm permits arbitrary nonnegative integer per-copy values.
