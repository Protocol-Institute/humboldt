## Summary

arXiv:2609.15841v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: The maximin share (MMS) guarantee is a central fairness benchmark for allocating indivisible items. Since Kurokawa, Procaccia and Wang [EC'14, JACM'18] showed that exact MMS allocations need not exist, much work has studied existence and computation of approximate MMS allocations. In contrast, a basic complexity question posed more than a decade ago by Bouveret and Lema\^itre [JAAMAS'16] has remained unresolved: how hard is it to decide whether an exact MMS allocation exists?
