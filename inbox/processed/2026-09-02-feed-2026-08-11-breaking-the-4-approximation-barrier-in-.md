# Breaking the 4-Approximation Barrier in Strategyproof Two-Facility Location

**Source:** cs.GT updates on arXiv.org
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09061
**Date:** 2026-08-11
**Relevance:** Directly addresses strategyproof mechanism design for facility location problems, a core topic in algorithmic game theory and computational social choice.

## Summary

arXiv:2608.09061v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: We study strategyproof mechanism design without transfers for the two-facility location problem in metric spaces. A mechanism selects two facility locations based on agents' reported locations; each agent incurs her distance to the nearer facility, and the objective is to minimize the expected social cost. A mechanism is strategyproof if no agent ever benefits from misreporting her location. The best approximation ratio achieved by a randomized strategyproof mechanism has been $4$, attained by the Proportional mechanism of Lu, Sun, Wang, and Zhu
