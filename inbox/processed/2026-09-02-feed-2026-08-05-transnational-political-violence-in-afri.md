# Transnational political violence in African borderlands

**Source:** econ.GN updates on arXiv.org
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03596
**Date:** 2026-08-05
**Relevance:** Directly addresses spatial patterns of political violence in African regions, providing empirical data relevant to understanding transnational conflict dynamics and borderland instability.

## Summary

arXiv:2608.03596v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: This paper examines the relationship between borderlands and political violence in Africa. Using spatiotemporal data on conflict events from 1997 to 2024 alongside the innovative OECD Spatial Conflict Dynamics indicator (SCDi), it suggests that borderlands see more conflict than non-borderlands. Political violence tends to decrease very rapidly with the distance to borders in Africa.
