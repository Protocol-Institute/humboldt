# An Efficient Black-Box Reduction from Online Learning to Multicalibration, and a New Route to $\Phi$-Regret Minimization

**Date:** 2026-08-05

## Summary

arXiv:2604.19592v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: We give a Gordon-Greenwald-Marks (GGM) style black-box reduction from online learning to online multicalibration. Concretely, we show that to achieve high-dimensional multicalibration with respect to a class of functions $\mathcal{H}$, it suffices to combine any no-regret learner over $\mathcal {H} $ with an expected variational inequality (EVI) solver.
