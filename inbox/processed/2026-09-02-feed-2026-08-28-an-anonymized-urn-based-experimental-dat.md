# An Anonymized Urn-Based Experimental Dataset on Decision-Making under Risk and Ambiguity

**Source:** econ.GN updates on arXiv.org
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26358
**Date:** 2026-08-28
**Relevance:** Directly provides experimental data on decision-making under risk and ambiguity, which are fundamental topics for testing behavioral economics theories and preference models.

## Summary

arXiv:2608.26358v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: This data paper describes an anonymized release derived from controlled behavioral experiments on decision-making under risk (known probabilities) and ambiguous uncertainty (partially specified or unknown probabilities) in Ellsberg-type urn tasks. The release contains 4,486 decision records from 246 adult participant records across two implementations (laboratory and online).
