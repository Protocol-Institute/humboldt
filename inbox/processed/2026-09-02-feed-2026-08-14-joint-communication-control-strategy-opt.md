Abstract: In this paper, we formalize a joint communication-control strategy optimization (JCCO) problem in multi-agent linear systems with quadratic costs, under the common-information-based (CIB) framework from decentralized stochastic control. For computational tractability, we focus on such JCCO problems with partially nested (PN) information structures (ISs).
