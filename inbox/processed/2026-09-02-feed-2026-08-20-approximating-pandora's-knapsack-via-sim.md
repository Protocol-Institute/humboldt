Abstract: We introduce Pandora's Knapsack: a hybrid between the classic stochastic knapsack problem [Dean et al., 2008] and Pandora's box [Weitzman, 1979]. As in stochastic knapsack, items have sizes drawn from known distributions, and items that fit within a knapsack contribute to the total value. As in Pandora, every item $i$ comes in a box that costs $c_i$ to open. What distinguishes our problem is that the size is revealed only after opening the box and paying the cost.
  We study the power of simple decision-making policies to approximate Pandora
