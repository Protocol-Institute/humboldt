Abstract: This paper studies proportional risk sharing at claim occurrence time in community-based insurance. Each participant is modeled by an individual Cram\'er-Lundberg surplus process, and, whenever a claim is reported within the pool, its cost is redistributed according to a fixed allocation matrix. We compare the infinite-time ruin probability of each participant under stand-alone operation and under pool participation.
