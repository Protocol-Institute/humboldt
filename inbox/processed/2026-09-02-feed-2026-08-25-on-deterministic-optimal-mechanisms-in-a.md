Abstract: Consider the problem of designing a revenue-optimal auction mechanism when two heterogeneous items are sold to a single buyer having independent valuations over the items. The distributions of the buyer's valuation for the items are assumed to have densities that are positive, nondecreasing, and continuously differentiable on their support sets $[c_i,c_i+b_i]$ in the positive axis.
