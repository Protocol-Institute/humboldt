Abstract: Artificial intelligence (AI) is becoming an increasingly integral part of higher education, yet the factors shaping students' use of AI for learning remain insufficiently understood. This study examines how students' goal orientation and academic help-seeking behavior are associated with AI use in a computer science context, while also accounting for individual, behavioral, and contextual characteristics.
