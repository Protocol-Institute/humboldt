Abstract: We derive a closed-form bid-ask spread and welfare decomposition for the Glosten-Milgrom 1985 sequential-trading model when the market maker observes the trade direction perturbed by a binary flip channel of probability $\eta$ -- a natural information-theoretic model of privacy mechanisms acting on the direction signal. Under a committed Bayesian market-maker pricing rule, the equilibrium spread is $\mu(1-2\eta)\Delta$, where $\mu$ is the informed-trader fraction and $\Delta = v_H - v_L$ the value range.
