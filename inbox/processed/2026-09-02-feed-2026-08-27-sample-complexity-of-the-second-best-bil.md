# Sample Complexity of the Second-Best Bilateral Trade

**Source:** cs.GT updates on arXiv.org
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25303
**Date:** 2026-08-27
**Relevance:** Studies sample complexity of learning incentive-compatible mechanisms, a fundamental problem in algorithmic mechanism design and learning theory.

## Summary

arXiv:2608.25303v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: We study the sample complexity of learning near-optimal bilateral trade mechanisms. Unlike previous work on learning simple or fixed-price bilateral-trade mechanisms, we focus on mechanisms satisfying Bayesian incentive compatibility (BIC), interim individual rationality (IIR), and ex-ante weak budget balance (WBB). In other words, our target is to design a sample-based mechanism that achieves the second-best gains-from-trade benchmark.
