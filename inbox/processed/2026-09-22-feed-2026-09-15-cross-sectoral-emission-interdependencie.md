## Summary

arXiv:2609.13713v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: This study examines emission interdependencies within the waste sector and between waste and other economic domains, energy, transport, water, and communications, using Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) methodologies with emphasis on process-based and hybrid approaches. The analysis employs the EXIOBASE multi-regional input-output database. Statistical correlation is applied to identify commonalities and divergences in emission drivers.
