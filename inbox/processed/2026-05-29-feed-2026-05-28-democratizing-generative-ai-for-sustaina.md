Abstract: As generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) diffuses across industries and becomes broadly accessible, the locus of sustainable competitive advantage shifts from technology ownership toward the quality of employee-level adoption and use. This paper develops a cross-level conceptual framework linking firm-level GenAI investment and governance to individual-level AI democratization, defined as the extent to which employees meaningfully, responsibly, and effectively use GenAI in their daily work.
