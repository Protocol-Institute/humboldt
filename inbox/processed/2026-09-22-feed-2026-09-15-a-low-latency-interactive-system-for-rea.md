## Summary

arXiv:2609.13986v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: Vision-language models are extending video understanding from offline clip analysis to continuous interactive streaming, but most research still emphasizes model capability rather than deployable low-latency interaction. This paper presents a unified edge-cloud system for real-time video VLM applications. Lightweight phone, smart glasses, PC, and pseudo-replay clients publish video and speech to a server runtime that provides shared ASR/TTS, session orchestration, backend adaptation, response delivery, and archive-backed measurement.
