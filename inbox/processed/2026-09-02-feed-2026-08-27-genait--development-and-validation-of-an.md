**Relevance:** Directly addresses development of assessment tools for generative AI literacy in high school students, a key educational outcome in the GenAI era.

## Summary

arXiv:2608.25815v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: There is growing international interest in generative AI (GenAI) literacy and its assessment among high school students, but objective assessment in this population remains underdeveloped. This article reports the iterative development and validation of the GenAI Literacy Test (GenAIT), an 18-item multiple-choice test measuring high school students' conceptual knowledge about GenAI, with content spanning technical, practical, and human-impact domains.
