# AI worsens climate change, integrated assessment shows

**Source:** econ.GN updates on arXiv.org
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24670
**Date:** 2026-08-26
**Relevance:** Directly addresses the relationship between AI development and climate change outcomes, providing a quantitative integrated assessment framework relevant to understanding AI's environmental impact.

## Summary

arXiv:2608.24670v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Artificial intelligence (AI) interacts with climate in various ways, while a unified analytical framework of this intricate interplay is lacking. To align AI investment with climate policy, we propose such a framework integrating AI's impact on emissions, output, and climate damages into the DICE model. We distinguish between ICT-like and Industrial Revolution (IR)-like AI prospects. Calibrated to the best available evidence, we find that AI development is net polluting.
