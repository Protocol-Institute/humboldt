Abstract: Cough events during live spoken conversations carry clinically valuable respiratory signals, yet existing dialogue systems treat them as acoustic noise to be discarded. We present HealthCUES (Clinical Understanding from Embodied Sounds), a streaming pipeline for paralinguistic respiratory monitoring in real-time conversational agents, a capability that, to the best of our knowledge, is absent from all prior systems.
