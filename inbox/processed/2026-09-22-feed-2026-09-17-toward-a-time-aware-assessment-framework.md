Abstract: AI is increasingly used to support decarbonization decisions across the built environment, yet the development, training, and use of AI consume energy and induce CO2e emissions. However, existing assessments often report physical-system savings while omitting AI-side emissions. Moreover, they rarely account for the mismatch between when AI costs occur and when decarbonization benefits materialize, which may be substantial for infrastructure-scale projects.
