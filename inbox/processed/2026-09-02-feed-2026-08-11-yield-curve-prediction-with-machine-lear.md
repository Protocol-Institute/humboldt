# Yield Curve Prediction with Machine Learning: Forecasting Approaches and the Role of Macroeconomic Predictors

**Source:** econ.GN updates on arXiv.org
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07536
**Date:** 2026-08-11
**Relevance:** Directly addresses machine learning methods for yield curve forecasting, a core quantitative finance prediction problem with practical relevance for fixed income and macroeconomic analysis.

## Summary

arXiv:2608.07536v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: This paper compares direct-yield and factor-based approaches to U.S. Treasury yield curve forecasting using a common high-dimensional macroeconomic information set. Forecasts are evaluated on monthly zero-coupon yields over the 2015-2025 out-of-sample period. Gains over the random walk are concentrated at short maturities and in slope forecasts, and decline with the forecast horizon.
