## Summary

arXiv:2608.03973v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Generative Artificial Intelligence (GAI) is increasingly being integrated into marketing education and is reshaping the skillsets required in marketing careers. While research has highlighted the promise and perils of incorporating GAI into education, there remains a need for a comprehensive framework to guide its effective use. In this research, we conduct a multipronged analysis, including a review of marketing course syllabi, a survey of marketing educators, and follow-up qualitative interviews.
