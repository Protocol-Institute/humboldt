## Summary

arXiv:2608.27598v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: This study examines whether customer disclosures affect how supplier firms allocate capital across business segments. Customer disclosures can shape supplier investment decisions through two competing channels. They can improve suppliers' information about downstream demand, helping suppliers align capital with growth opportunities ("information channel"), or erode incumbent suppliers' private information advantage, inducing costly investments to defend customer relationships ("competitive-threat channel").
