## Summary

arXiv:2609.19438v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Solvency games are a gambling problem on infinite-state Markov decision processes in which the state $n \in \mathbb{N}$ represents an investor's fortune. In every round, the investor chooses an action from a finite action set, and every action yields a distribution over integer-valued gains in an interval $\{-\ell,\ldots,m\}$. The risk-averse investor wants to minimise the probability of eventual ruin (reaching a fortune $\le 0$).
