Abstract: Climate models often feature nonlinear responses to changes in model parameters or boundary conditions. Factor separation asks how much of the resulting change should be assigned to altered factors and their interactions. Lunt et al. (2010) showed that two factor attribution methods, the \emph{linear-sum} and \emph{shared-interaction} methods, coincide for $n\le 4$ factors and conjectured this holds for arbitrary $n$.
