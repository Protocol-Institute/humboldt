Abstract: In this research note we briefly connect two well-studied topics in computational social choice: metric distortion and the selection of undominated committees. In particular, we show that undominated committees are (in some sense) both necessary and sufficient to achieve bi-criteria metric distortion below $3$ (Banishashem et al., 2026). First, we show that any $\alpha$-undominated committee with $\alpha \le 0.5 - \Omega(1)$ has a bi-criteria metric distortion strictly of $3 - \Omega(1)$.
