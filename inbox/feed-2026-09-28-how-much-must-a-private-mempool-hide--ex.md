Abstract: Private and encrypted mempools hide pending transactions to stop sandwich attacks and other forms of maximal extractable value (MEV), but what they hide is rarely everything: a transaction's pair, direction, and a coarse range for its size can still leak. How much leakage makes sandwiching pay? We answer exactly for a fee-free constant-product automated market maker, the pricing rule behind Uniswap v2.
