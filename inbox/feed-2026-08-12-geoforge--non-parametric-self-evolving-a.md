arXiv:2608.10494v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: Earth observation (EO) agents construct scientifically valid tool workflows and ground their conclusions in current geospatial evidence. This is challenging because EO workflows are constrained by sensing semantics, product dependencies, spatial and temporal compatibility, and parameter requirements. Existing agents often search a broad operation space for each query, while recent self-evolving systems do not fully organize heterogeneous EO trajectories into reusable knowledge across different decision levels.
