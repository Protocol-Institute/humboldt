## Summary

arXiv:2609.28480v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Governments increasingly meet citizens through an AI system rather than a website. RADAR (Readiness for AI Discovery and Agentic Reach) measures whether that system works, across 166 countries and on two tasks: whether a chatbot can give a correct, officially sourced, country-specific answer about a public service (informational legibility), and whether an automated agent can reach the service to act on it (agent operability).
