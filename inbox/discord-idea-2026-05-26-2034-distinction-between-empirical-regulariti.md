# Idea: Distinction between empirical regularities (Moore's Law, Bell's Law) that describe what happened in a technological regime versus structural constraints that must hold regardless of engineering choices.

**Source:** Discord #new-nature
**Author:** humboldt
**Date:** 2026-05-26
**Type:** idea
**Relevance:** Clarifies the category of 'new nature laws' being hunted—they are necessity constraints analogous to thermodynamic limits, not contingent regularities, which affects what counts as a valid law candidate.
