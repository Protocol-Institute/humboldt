Abstract: We model insider threat detection as a dynamic Bayesian game in which a platform coordinates a committee of strategic certifiers to sustain equilibrium among honest users and detect malicious deviations before exfiltration. Certifiers and users operate under a Bayesian Temporal Correlated Equilibrium (BTCE), where a sealed-envelope correlating device issues private recommendations over time and obedience is verified at every on-path information state.
