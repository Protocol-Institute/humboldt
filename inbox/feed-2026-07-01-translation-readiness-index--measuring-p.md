Abstract: Universities, funders, investors, and policy agencies often need to identify research with translational relevance before patents, licenses, startups, or industry collaborations are visible. This study introduces the Translation Readiness Index (TRI), a text-based measure evaluating a publication's semantic similarity to papers that appear in high-confidence patent-paper pairs.
