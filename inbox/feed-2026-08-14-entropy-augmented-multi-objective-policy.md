Abstract: Autonomous agent teams deployed in settings such as marine and extraterrestrial outposts must coordinate actions to achieve optimal outcomes across multiple competing objectives. Multi-objective evolutionary algorithms such as NSGA-II optimize for diversity in the objective space, but neglect diversity in the behavior space, possibly leading to premature convergence and a collapse in behaviors that may differentiate policies in different external conditions.
