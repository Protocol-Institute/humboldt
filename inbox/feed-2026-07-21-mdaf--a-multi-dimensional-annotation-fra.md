## Summary

arXiv:2607.16219v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Government websites contain a vast but underexploited body of textual evidence on foreign policy. This article develops a scalable approach for extracting structured information from policy texts and converting it into standardized event data, with policy event defined broadly as a statement or action. It proposes MDAF integrating an LLM workflow to automate foreign policy text identification, information extraction, and event classification.
