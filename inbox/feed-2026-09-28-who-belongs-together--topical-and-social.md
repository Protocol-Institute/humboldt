arXiv:2609.31297v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: Bluesky starter packs are human curated collections of accounts and feeds aimed at helping users discover new communities, especially during onboarding. Prior research has examined their effects on platform growth and account visibility. However, whether these packs capture meaningful topical and social structure remains unanswered. We study 2718 active starter packs containing approximately 144K accounts, combining pack metadata, follow graphs, user post histories and inferred demographic attributes to answer this question.
