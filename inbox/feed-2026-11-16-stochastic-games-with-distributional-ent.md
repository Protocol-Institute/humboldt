Abstract: We introduce \emph{stochastic games with distributional entropy objectives}, two-player zero-sum games whose configurations are probability distributions over the states of a finite arena and whose stage reward is Shannon entropy. Over an infinite discounted horizon, MIN seeks to concentrate the distribution, while MAX seeks to disperse it. Because entropy is nonlinear, it matters whether strategic and transition randomness are averaged before entropy is evaluated or sampled first.
