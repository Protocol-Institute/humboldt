# Truncated Noisy Best-Response Algorithms: Toward Game Theoretic Learning with Safety Guarantees

## Summary

arXiv:2609.11863v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: We consider a game theoretic approach to solve multi-agent coordination problems with submodular maximization objectives. It is known for such problems that the Nash equilibria for the corresponding game are always within 50% of the optimal, but that the equilibria which achieve this worst-case bound are not stable. To exploit this instability, we propose a family of algorithms which we call Truncated Noisy Best-Response (TNBR) Algorithms. These algorithms are flexibly characterized by agents asynchronously and stochastically selecting actions
