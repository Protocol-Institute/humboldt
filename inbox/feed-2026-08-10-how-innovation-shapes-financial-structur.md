## Summary

arXiv:2512.14154v2 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: This paper studies how the stock market---relative to the banking sector---responds to innovation by using a panel of 75 countries from 1982 to 2021. Our baseline result is that innovation has a positive effect on stock market activity, efficiency and size relative to the banking sector. In addition, we uncover alternative funding channels by studying how institutional quality moderates the effect of innovation on financial structure.
