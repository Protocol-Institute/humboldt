Abstract: We study weighted fair division of indivisible mixed manna under additive valuations. First, we resolve the general existence open question for weighted envy-freeness up to one item (WEF1), and show that every instance with arbitrary positive entitlements admits a complete WEF1 allocation computable in polynomial time.
