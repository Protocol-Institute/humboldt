Abstract: House allocation is a fundamental problem in which each agent is assigned exactly one house. While the classical model assumes that all houses are available before the allocation is computed, many practical settings require decisions to be made as houses become available over time. We introduce the online house allocation problem, where houses arrive sequentially and the algorithm must maintain an allocation without knowledge of future arrivals.
