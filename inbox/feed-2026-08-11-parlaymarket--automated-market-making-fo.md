arXiv:2603.22596v3 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: Prediction markets are powerful mechanisms for information aggregation, but existing designs are optimized for single-event contracts. Traders frequently express beliefs about joint outcomes - sports parlays, conditional forecasts, multi-scenario financial bets. Current platforms either prohibit such trades or rely on ad hoc mechanisms that ignore correlation structure, resulting in inefficient prices and fragmented liquidity.
