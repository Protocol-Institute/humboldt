## Summary

arXiv:2608.07025v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: This paper studies ex-post equilibria (EPEs) in simultaneous-move games with parameter uncertainty. We first compare EPEs with existing notions of robust equilibrium and provide a general foundation for EPEs as a solution concept for games with parameter uncertainty, and we show that EPEs are fully characterized by two game-theoretic properties (monotonicity and set-consistency).
