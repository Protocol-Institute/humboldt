# Beyond the Margin: Targeted Conservation and Household Water Demand

**Source:** econ.GN updates on arXiv.org
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23347
**Date:** 2026-08-14
**Relevance:** Directly addresses whether targeted conservation interventions reduce aggregate household water demand, a core policy question for water resource management.

## Summary

arXiv:2606.23347v2 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: Non-price interventions targeting specific household water uses are increasingly central to conservation policy, but whether end-use savings translate into lower aggregate demand remains unclear. This paper reports evidence from a pre-registered field experiment in which 775 Finnish households were randomized to a shower timer, a water-saving shower head, or the same shower head with real-time feedback.
