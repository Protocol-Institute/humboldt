## Summary

arXiv:2606.30966v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: Formal specification is a powerful tool to guide the learning process and provides significant advantages over reward shaping: (1) mathematical rigor; (2) expressiveness to specify objectives and constraints, and (3) the ability to define tactics to achieve objectives. However, these benefits remain largely unexplored in the context of Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning (MARL). This paper introduces HyPOLE, a novel framework for MARL under partial observability, where learning is guided by the expressive power of the so-called hyperproperties
