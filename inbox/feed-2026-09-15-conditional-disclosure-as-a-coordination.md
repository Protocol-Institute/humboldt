## Summary

arXiv:2604.00874v4 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: People with controversial views may stay silent rather than speak out alone, though in a large enough group they could speak safely together. A social assurance contract makes disclosure conditional: signed commitments of support stay private until enough people have signed for it to be safe to publish. In contrast, a survey can measure hidden support but cannot protect anyone who acts on it. I derive the contract's exact advantage over speaking openly, and when that advantage disappears.
