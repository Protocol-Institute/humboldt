# Little Impact of ChatGPT on High School Test Scores

**Source:** econ.GN updates on arXiv.org
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.08812
**Date:** 2026-09-23
**Relevance:** Directly addresses empirical evidence on AI's impact on student learning outcomes, a key question for understanding educational AI integration.

## Summary

arXiv:2605.08812v3 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: In educational settings, AI can be used as a learning aid, but can also be used to avoid schoolwork, passing classes while learning little. Most existing evidence on AI's educational impact comes from trials of supervised instructor-encouraged AI usage, rather than on unsupervised usage which is of the most concern. This paper uses the dropoff in ChatGPT activity during non-school summer months to identify areas with heavy educational AI use, and then estimates the effect of that usage on aggregate United States high school test scores.
