**Source:** cs.MA updates on arXiv.org

**Relevance:** Directly examines how voting costs affect equilibrium outcomes in the canonical Hotelling-Downs spatial voting model, including breakdown of the median voter theorem.

## Summary

arXiv:2609.29869v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: We study a partial-participation variation of the Hotelling-Downs model. Voters each have a cost to vote, and only vote when the comparative gain from their preferred candidate exceeds the cost. Under this model the median voter theorem breaks, and we study the extent of polarization under equilibria in different voters and cost distributions.
