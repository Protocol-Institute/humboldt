**Relevance:** This dataset of 15 million AI interactions provides empirical evidence for understanding AI's economic impact and adoption patterns across sectors.

## Summary

arXiv:2608.00038v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: This paper introduces the AI & Economy ATLAS (Activity, Task, Landscape, and Adoption Study), an ongoing economic research initiative using Google AI usage data. The first iteration of ATLAS is built on 15 million de-identified interactions across the Gemini App, Google AI Mode, and Gemini API. Using privacy-preserving algorithms as well as established and bespoke classification methods, we map AI usage to over 800 occupations, 4000 tasks, 300 household activities, 150 countries, and 140 languages.
