Abstract: Validating Autonomous Driving Systems (ADS) in simulation requires testing architectures that can discover rare, safety-critical failures while generating scenarios that are executable, diverse, and useful for downstream failure analysis. We introduce Teach-to-Crash, a closed-loop testing framework that combines a constrained ego-centric scenario representation, stagnation-aware search control, and a dual-LLM architecture for adaptive failure discovery.
