## Summary

arXiv:2607.27574v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: Activation steering has emerged in large language models as a lightweight alternative for dynamically changing a model's behavior at inference time. However, we show that existing steering methods fail to steer even a simple policy in a two-route gridworld environment. To address this limitation, we propose Policy Gradient Steering (PGS), which formulates steering as a reinforcement learning problem. PGS accumulates gradients of a temporary behavioral objective over a small set of rollouts or demonstrations to construct a removable task vector
