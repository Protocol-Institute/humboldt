# SABRE: A Multi-Agent Approach for Selecting Out-of-Distribution Detectors Under a Budget

**Date:** 2026-08-05
**Relevance:** Addresses the practical problem of selecting OOD detectors for vision-language models across different domains, which is relevant to understanding model robustness and deployment reliability.

## Summary

arXiv:2608.02959v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Post-hoc out-of-distribution (OOD) detection for vision-language models assumes that a detector chosen on a benchmark stays reliable once deployed. We show this fails across domains: on a single frozen encoder, a detector that leads in one domain can invert in another, scoring in-distribution inputs as more anomalous than genuine outliers, and the best detector changes from domain to domain, so no fixed choice is reliable throughout.
