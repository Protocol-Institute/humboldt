Abstract: Personal AI agents face a deployment paradox on mobile: persistent background execution drains the battery and conflicts with platform background limits, yet purely reactive agents miss time-sensitive obligations until the user remembers to ask. We present Jagarin, a three-layer architecture that resolves this through structured hibernation and demand-driven wake.
