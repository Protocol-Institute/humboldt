## Summary

Abstract: How likely is it that advanced artificial intelligence (AI) will cause human extinction or a similarly permanent disempowerment of humanity? Humans, including AI experts, frequently disagree on this question. Yet one important perspective is missing from this debate: that of AI systems themselves. We put the question to 241 large language models (LLMs) at every available reasoning setting. The models converged on a median risk of 10.0%, with the middle 90% of their estimates between 3.5% and 15.8%, far narrower than the spread among human expert
