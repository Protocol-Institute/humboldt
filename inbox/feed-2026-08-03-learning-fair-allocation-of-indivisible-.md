Abstract: We study a setting in which an algorithm must output a fair allocation of indivisible items while "learning on the job". More specifically, the algorithm is to output an allocation satisfying EF1, PROP1, or similar fairness notions; however, the algorithm initially has no information about the agents' valuations, and can only learn about them by (repeatedly) proposing an allocation, and obtaining feedback about a fairness violation in the allocation.
