# Do people rely on ChatGPT more than their peers to detect deepfake news?

**Source:** econ.GN updates on arXiv.org
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01540
**Date:** 2026-08-04
**Relevance:** Directly examines human reliance on AI tools (ChatGPT) versus peer advice for detecting AI-generated misinformation, addressing a key question about comparative trust in different information sources.

## Summary

arXiv:2608.01540v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: This experimental study investigates how people rely on different sources of advice when detecting AI-generated fake news (deepfake news). In a laboratory deepfake detection task, student participants identified the proportion of human-written (non-AI-generated) content in synthetic deepfake news articles and received advice from ChatGPT (GPT-4), human peers, or linguistic experts.
