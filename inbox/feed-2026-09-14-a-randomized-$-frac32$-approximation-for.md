# A Randomized $\frac32$-Approximation for Strategic Facility Location on a Circle

## Summary

arXiv:2609.12792v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: We study strategyproof mechanisms for locating a single facility on a circle so as to serve a set of strategic agents under the utilitarian social cost objective. We analyze a simple parity-dependent mechanism that randomizes between the Random Dictator (RD) mechanism and the Proportional Circle Distance (PCD) mechanism. For an odd number \(n\) of agents, this mechanism mixes RD and PCD with equal probability, as originally proposed by Rogowski and Dziubi{\'n}ski (IJCAI 2025).
