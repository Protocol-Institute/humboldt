Abstract: In the Stable Roommates Problem (SR), a set of $2n$ agents rank one another in a linear order. The goal is to find a matching that is stable: one that has no pair of agents who mutually prefer each other over their assigned partners. We consider the problem of finding an optimal stable matching. Agents associate weights with each of their potential partners, and the goal is to find a stable matching that minimizes the sum of the associated weights.
