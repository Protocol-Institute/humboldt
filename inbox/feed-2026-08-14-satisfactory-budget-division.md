## Summary

arXiv:2502.00484v2 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: A divisible budget must be allocated to several projects, and agents are asked for their opinion on how much they would give to each project. We consider that an agent is satisfied by a division of the budget if, for at least a certain predefined number $\tau$ of projects, the part of the budget actually allocated to each project is at least as large as the amount the agent requested.
