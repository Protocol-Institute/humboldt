## Summary

arXiv:2609.25424v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: In the classical i.i.d. prophet-inequality problem, a single item is allocated to one of $n$ agents who arrive sequentially, with values drawn independently from a known distribution. When an agent arrives, their value is revealed, and the algorithm must immediately allocate the item or continue. The usual objective is utilitarian welfare: the expected value of the recipient.
