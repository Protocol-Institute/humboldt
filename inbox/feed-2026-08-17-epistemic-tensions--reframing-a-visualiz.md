Abstract: In this work, we present how employing the lens of entanglement helped us examine and reframe epistemic tensions arising in a visualization co-design project. Entanglement theory challenges traditional assumptions in the visualization research community by emphasizing that knowledge is not produced through linear, isolated processes, but is inherently entangled with phenomena and apparatuses.
