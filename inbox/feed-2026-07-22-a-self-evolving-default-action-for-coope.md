Abstract: Counterfactual credit assignment has proven effective in multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL) for discrete action spaces, yet its extension to continuous-action cooperative tasks remains challenging. Existing methods that approximate the counterfactual baseline via Monte Carlo sampling often introduce bias into policy gradients and fail to guarantee convergence to local optima, as the sampled actions may not have been sufficiently trained.
