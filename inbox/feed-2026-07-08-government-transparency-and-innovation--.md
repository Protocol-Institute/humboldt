## Summary

arXiv:2510.19377v3 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: We exploit a 1998 administrative quasi-experiment to analyze the effects of government transparency on follow-on innovation. Using the universe of U.S. wireless products, we find that the publication of technical details of new products online increased follow-on technology use from an average of 0.2 to 1.36 subsequent products within five years.
