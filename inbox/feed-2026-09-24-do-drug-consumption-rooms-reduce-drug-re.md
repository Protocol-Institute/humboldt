# Do Drug Consumption Rooms Reduce Drug-Related Hospitalizations? Evidence from Switzerland

**Source:** econ.GN updates on arXiv.org
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27732
**Date:** 2026-09-24
**Relevance:** Directly evaluates causal effects of a drug policy intervention on health outcomes using quasi-experimental methods, providing evidence relevant to harm reduction policy effectiveness.

## Summary

arXiv:2609.27732v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: This paper estimates the causal effect of drug consumption room (DCR) openings on drug-related hospitalizations in Switzerland. I exploit the staggered introduction of DCRs across Swiss cities between 1998 and 2022, using individual-level hospital records and a difference-in-differences estimator. DCR openings reduce drug-related hospitalization rates by roughly 35\% within 5 km of a facility.
