Abstract: Large language models (LLMs) are proposed as synthetic respondents for pilot testing, but their usefulness depends on whether they supply the evidence assessment development requires. We calibrated rating scale models on 14 digital-use skill items from 6,245 adults and used the human item parameters to evaluate responses generated for 1,300 demographically matched personas.
