Abstract: We study the agent-wise disjunction of two central fairness notions for indivisible items, where every agent must be either envy-free up to any item (EFX) or maximin-share (MMS) satisfied. One might expect that having a flexible fairness requirement for individual agents will restore existence, especially because the existence of EFX itself resisted resolution for nearly a decade.
