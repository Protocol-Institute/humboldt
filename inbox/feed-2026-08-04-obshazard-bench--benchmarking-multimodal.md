Abstract: Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) are increasingly used to interpret Earth observation data, yet their capability to support real-world disaster emergency response remains insufficiently evaluated. Existing remote sensing benchmarks largely rely on static, post-hoc, and expert-processed products, such as gridded reanalysis data, which are difficult to align with operational disaster scenarios where hazards evolve rapidly and decisions must be made under strict time constraints.
