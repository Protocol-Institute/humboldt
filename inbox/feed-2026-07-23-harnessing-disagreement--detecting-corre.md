# Harnessing Disagreement: Detecting Correlated Agreement Blindness in Multi-Agent Triage

**Relevance:** Directly addresses how improved agent alignment (convergence) creates safety vulnerabilities in multi-agent systems—a core mechanism in CL-001 (formalization increases coordinated failure modes) and CL-003 (trust in safety protocols becomes brittle as disagreement signals vanish).

## Summary

arXiv:2607.19899v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Disagreement-triggered escalation can create a structural blind spot in multi-agent arbitration: as base learners improve, they tend to converge, weakening safety monitoring where correlated failures concentrate. We term this correlated agreement blindness and present ARAT (Arbitrated Reasoning Agents for Alarm Triage), a directed-star system combining an inductive Random Forest (RF) agent, an analogical case-based k-nearest neighbour (k-NN) agent, and a calibrated meta-model to mitigate this effect.
