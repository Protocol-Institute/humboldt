# PriceBench: A Diagnostic Benchmark for Price, Quality, and Brand Preferences in LLM Booking Agents

**Source:** econ.GN updates on arXiv.org
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31468
**Date:** 2026-09-28
**Relevance:** Directly relevant to understanding how LLMs make preference-based decisions in real-world applications and the biases that may emerge in their role as purchasing agents.

## Summary

arXiv:2609.31468v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: LLMs increasingly act as purchasing agents, which makes the LLM, not the user, the one choosing among the options that satisfy a request; its preferences quietly fix what gets bought and what it costs. Hotel booking is a clean instance: a high-volume choice settled on a few comparable attributes, where the pick reveals those preferences. We introduce PriceBench, a diagnostic benchmark that recovers an LLM's price, quality, and brand preferences from its booking choices with a logit choice model, applied to 28 LLMs from 8 providers on 3,600 hotel
