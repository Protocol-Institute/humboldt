# LLM-Driven Autonomous Vehicles Inherit Human Driver Biases in Pedestrian Yielding: Results and Implications From A New Benchmark

**Date:** 2026-09-02
**Relevance:** Directly relevant to understanding how foundation models/LLMs affect AV safety and fairness, particularly regarding inherited biases in safety-critical decisions like pedestrian interactions.

## Summary

arXiv:2609.00192v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: Public trust in Autonomous Vehicles (AVs) may depend not only on technical success but also on the fairness of their decision making. While a recent trend in AV research involves using general purpose "common sense" models to guide AV decision making, the degree to which these inherit human biases in driving is still understudied. Given that psychology studies have shown human driver biases exist, such as lower pedestrian-yielding rates to Black pedestrians in the US, we argue that analyses of model bias should also be part of AV evaluation.
