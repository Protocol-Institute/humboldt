Abstract: We study security games on attack graphs, where an adaptive attacker seeks to reach a target by sequentially attempting stochastic controls along the current attack frontier, while a defender allocates limited resources across controls to delay compromise. The attacker may choose among exponentially many attack routes and freely pivot between them as successes and failures are observed, yielding an exponentially large space of contingent attack policies.
