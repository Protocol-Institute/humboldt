## Summary

arXiv:2609.02872v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: A central challenge in mechanism design is to develop truthful trade mechanisms that maximize the expected gains-from-trade (GFT) in two-sided markets. Because achieving the full GFT is generally impossible, the literature has focused on constant-factor approximations---a notoriously difficult problem even in simple settings. It was only recently that a breakthrough result by [DMSW22] achieved a constant-factor approximation for single-item bilateral trade.
