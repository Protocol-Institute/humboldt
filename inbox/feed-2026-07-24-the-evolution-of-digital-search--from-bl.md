## Summary

arXiv:2607.21459v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Digital search is undergoing a fundamental transformation from a human-driven process of discovery to an agent-mediated system of delegated decision-making. In the traditional model of digital search, users translate intent into keyword-based queries, evaluate ranked lists of links, and execute decisions outside the search interface. In an AI-native world, users express goals in natural language, agents interpret these intentions, and outcomes are returned as recommendations or executed decisions.
