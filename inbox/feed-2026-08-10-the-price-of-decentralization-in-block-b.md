## Summary

arXiv:2606.01874v2 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: Decentralized block building mechanisms replace the monopoly of a single proposer with multiple builders. However, their censorship-resistance and fair-access benefits depend not only on the number of builders, but also on where builders are geographically positioned to provide transaction coverage. We study this tension between builder location choice, user transaction coverage, and utility concentration by modeling decentralized block building as a stochastic coverage game.
