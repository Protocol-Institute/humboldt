## Summary

arXiv:2607.14550v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Logistics systems increasingly mix \emph{autonomous logistic equipment} (ALE) with non-autonomous machinery under a central control system (CS), where the best decision-maker depends on who holds the most current world model, yet authority is fixed at design time. When an ALE's local model and the CS global model diverge, both act on incompatible beliefs and produce deadlocks that resource-based handling neither explains nor prevents.
