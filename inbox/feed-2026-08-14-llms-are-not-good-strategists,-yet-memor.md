Abstract: Strategic reasoning in Large Language Models (LLMs) within long-horizon environments is often limited by inconsistent subgoals. In these settings, finite attention resources prevent the model from maintaining strategic coherence over thousands of steps. This limitation leads to strategic drift, where localized decisions fail to sustain a coherent trajectory across reasoning.
