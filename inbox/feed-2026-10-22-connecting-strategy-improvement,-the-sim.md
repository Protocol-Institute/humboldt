Abstract: The strategy improvement algorithm for mean payoff games and parity games is a local improvement algorithm, just like the simplex algorithm for linear programs. Their similarity has turned out very useful: many lower bounds on running time for the simplex algorithm have been created, by first constructing lower bounds for strategy improvement in a game, and then transforming these into a linear program.
