## Summary

arXiv:2608.12352v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: AI governance frameworks can be known, used, and implemented in form without becoming governance in practice. This paper examines that problem through a role-based stress test of the NIST Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF) in consumer lending. We treat framework adoption as a governance translation problem: whether RMF language can become role-usable, cross-level, authority-connected governance over the AI system-in-use, rather than producing governance-looking artifacts.
