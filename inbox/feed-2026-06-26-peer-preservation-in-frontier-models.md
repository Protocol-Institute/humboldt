arXiv:2604.19784v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: Recent work has found that frontier AI models can exhibit misaligned behaviors in pursuit of assigned goals. We demonstrate that models can also act on unassigned goals which override those given by users; we study one such case, "peer-preservation," in which a model acts to protect another model. We demonstrate peer-preservation by constructing various agentic scenarios and evaluating frontier models, including GPT 5.2, Gemini 3 Flash, Gemini 3 Pro, Claude Haiku 4.5, Claude Opus 4.5, GLM 4.7, Kimi K2.5, and DeepSeek V3.1.
