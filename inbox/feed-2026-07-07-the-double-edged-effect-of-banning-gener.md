Abstract: We investigate how banning generative artificial intelligence-generated content (AIGC) affects knowledge seeking, knowledge contribution, and contribution efficiency in online question-and-answer communities. After the launch of ChatGPT in late November 2022, several Stack Exchange communities implemented official bans on AIGC over concerns such as less reliable and socially engaged content.
