Abstract: Mechanisms specify both allocation rules and message spaces. We study how message spaces affect behavior in a laboratory assignment environment in which objects are bundles of three attributes and preferences are induced by utility formulas. We vary preference complexity and compare full-ranking reports, two attribute-based interfaces, and sequential choice under serial dictatorship.
