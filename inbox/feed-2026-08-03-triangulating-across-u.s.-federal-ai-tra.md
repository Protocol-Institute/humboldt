Abstract: Federal AI systems can deny benefits or flag individuals for deportation, but the public disclosures meant to make those systems visible are fragmented and unevenly detailed. This paper examines three existing U.S. federal transparency regimes---System of Records Notices (SORNs), Information Collection Requests (ICRs), and the AI Use Case Inventory---and asks how well they, individually and together, describe government AI use.
