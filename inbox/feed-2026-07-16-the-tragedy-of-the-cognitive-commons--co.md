Abstract: In a recent dynamic model by Acemoglu, Kong and Ozdaglar (2026a) agentic AI can cause a self-reinforcing deterioration of humanity's common knowledge base in what they call knowledge collapse. The model is based on a natural complementarity between the cumulative general knowledge of humans and locally generated context-specific knowledge, and on a learning externality that means that we all contribute to the private signal and the thin public signal that feeds the collective stock.
