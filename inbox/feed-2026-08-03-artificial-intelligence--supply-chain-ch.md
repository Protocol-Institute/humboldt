## Summary

arXiv:2607.29572v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Artificial intelligence depends on a stack of inputs, models on compute, compute on chips, and chips on electricity and refined minerals. This paper measures the concentration of each layer on one scale, the Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (HHI), from cited and reproducible data. Three findings follow. First, concentration forms a clear gradient. It is modest downstream, where public and regulatory attention is heaviest and model usage and cloud fall below the 1{,}800 mark United States agencies treat as highly concentrated. It rises steeply upstream
