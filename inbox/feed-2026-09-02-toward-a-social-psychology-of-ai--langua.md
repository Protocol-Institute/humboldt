# Toward a social psychology of AI: language-model agents reproduce human-like minimal-group bias

**Source:** cs.CY updates on arXiv.org

**Date:** 2026-09-02
**Relevance:** Demonstrates that AI systems exhibit human social biases in group dynamics, which is foundational for understanding AI safety and social behavior alignment.

## Summary

arXiv:2609.00009v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: Language-model agents now interact in groups, but evaluations that probe memorised stereotype content or use models to simulate people leave this social behaviour unmeasured. We adapt the minimal-group paradigm---social psychology's classic test of intergroup bias---into a controlled probe: an agent distributes points among anonymous peers bearing only an arbitrary group label.
