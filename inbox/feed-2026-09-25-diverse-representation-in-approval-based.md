Abstract: The study of approval-based committee (ABC) voting has so far focused predominantly on proportional representation. The canonical notion of diverse representation, based on the Chamberlin--Courant score, counts the number of voters with at least one representative in the committee, making it an individualistic notion. We develop a more comprehensive theory that instead requires the representation of many groups of voters, grounding it in the justified representation (JR) axiom, which we strengthen in two directions.
