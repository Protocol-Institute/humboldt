## Summary

arXiv:2608.07537v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: In this study, we propose a framework that incorporates subjective evaluations provided by a Vision-Language Model (VLM) into the fitness evaluation and selection processes of a genetic algorithm. As the target of evolution, we employ virtual soft robots with flexible morphologies and locomotion and present the VLM with sequence images representing the locomotion of two individuals.
