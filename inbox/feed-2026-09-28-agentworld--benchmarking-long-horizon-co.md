**Relevance:** Directly addresses multi-agent LLM collaboration evaluation, filling a gap in existing benchmarks by testing long-horizon cooperative tasks beyond competitive or short-interaction settings.

## Summary

arXiv:2609.31590v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Existing multi-agent benchmarks primarily test in competitive settings, short-horizon interactions under 20 steps, or simply aggregate individual performance, failing to isolate and highlight genuine collaboration capabilities of LLM-based agents. We introduce AgentWorld, a benchmark of 100 human-annotated tasks (with 100 augmented variants) for evaluating long-horizon, multi-agent collaboration.
