Abstract: We study fair allocations of indivisible items under general set valuations. We prove that every instance with three agents and arbitrary real-valued valuations admits a balanced allocation that is envy-free up to one good and one chore (EF$1^c_g$). This directly implies balanced EF$1$ when each valuation is either monotone nondecreasing or monotone nonincreasing.
