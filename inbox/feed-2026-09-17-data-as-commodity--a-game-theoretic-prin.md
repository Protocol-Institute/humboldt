Abstract: Data is the central commodity of the digital economy. Unlike physical goods, data exhibits properties that defy the standard theory of supply and demand: it is non-rival (the same dataset can be sold to multiple buyers without degradation), it is replicable at near-zero cost, and it is traded under heterogeneous licensing rules that restrict lawful use.
