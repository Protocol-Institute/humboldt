Abstract: The EU AI Act uses a risk-based approach to regulate AI systems, calibrating the intensity of regulation according to the risks they pose. While the AI Act's definition of 'risk' (as the combination of the probability and severity of harm) implies quantification, the AI Act focuses on risks to fundamental rights, thereby engaging a typically qualitative perspective.
