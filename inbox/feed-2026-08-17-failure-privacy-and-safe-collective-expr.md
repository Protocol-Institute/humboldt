Abstract: Controversial views sometimes remain unspoken because they invite retaliation. However, a sufficiently large group could speak safely if only they spoke together. Speaking up one-by-one may encourage others, but retaliation against early participants can stop that cascade before a protective group forms. A Social Assurance Contract privately collects signed commitments and publishes them only when all signers will be safe.
