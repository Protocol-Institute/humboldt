Abstract: Equality before the law is a human right, and access to high-quality legal aid for indigent defendants is essential to enforce it. In a context where all defendants have access to a lawyer, I study the impact of denying legal aid on court outcomes. I combine double machine learning and a new administrative dataset linking aid to court outcomes in New South Wales, Australia, to learn the assignment function, whose inputs are known.
