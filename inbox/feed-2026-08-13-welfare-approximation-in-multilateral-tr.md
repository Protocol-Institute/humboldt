## Summary

arXiv:2608.11351v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: We introduce the study of \emph{multilateral trade}: a mechanism-design problem in which a single potential trade involves $k$ agents and can be executed only if all $k$ agents agree to participate. The classical case $k=2$ is the well-studied bilateral trade problem, where a seller and a buyer with private values must decide whether to trade an item initially held by the seller.
