## Summary

arXiv:2607.04753v3 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: Decision-makers often receive information through narratives combining diagnostic evidence with details that carry no information useful for inference. We study whether such nondiagnostic details embedded in a narrative affect belief updating. In a laboratory experiment, participants repeatedly report incentivized beliefs in a Bayesian inference task.
