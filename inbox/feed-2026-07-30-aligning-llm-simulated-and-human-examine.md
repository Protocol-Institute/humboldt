# Aligning LLM-Simulated and Human Examinees for Psychometric Calibration: A Cognitive Diagnostic Profiling Approach

**Relevance:** Tests whether LLM simulation can substitute for human data in safety-critical educational assessment protocols—a case where formalization pressures (CL-001) and trust dependencies (CL-003) directly intersect.

## Summary

arXiv:2607.26317v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Psychometric calibration for educational tests typically requires costly human response data. Large language models (LLMs) simulated examinees offer a promising route to early calibration, but their responses are too accurate and too uniform. We propose Cognitive Diagnostic Profiling (CDP), a zero-shot framework that prompts LLMs to simulate plausible examinees with diverse cognitive profiles: binary attribute-mastery patterns are rendered as natural-language profiles and sampled under an uninformative or an informative distribution.
