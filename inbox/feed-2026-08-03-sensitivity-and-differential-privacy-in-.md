arXiv:2607.26388v2 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: Voting rules aggregate individual preferences into collective decisions, but the rankings they receive contain only ordinal information. The metric distortion framework studies ordinal voting rules in settings where voters and candidates are embedded in an unknown metric space. Deterministic rules have optimal worst-case distortion $3$, while recent randomized rules break the $3$ barrier.
