Abstract: Digital platforms increasingly rely on automated budget-management systems to regulate participation across allocation opportunities. A common tool is multiplicative pacing, which scales buyers' bids so that campaign-level budgets are spent gradually. Although pacing is operationally simple, its aggregate behavior depends on the underlying mechanism.
