Abstract: We propose a human bottleneck perspective for understanding how generative AI transforms the innovation process. The central premise is that many constraints traditionally plaguing the innovation process are cognitive and social in origin, rooted in how people generate ideas, evaluate novelty, and communicate through social systems. Generative AI does not act uniformly on these constraints.
