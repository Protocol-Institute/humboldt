Abstract: We study auctions where two positions are sold to unit-demand bidders with private heterogeneous order preferences: some are specialists who value only the first position, while others are generalists who are indifferent between the two. First, we consider a standard first-price rule which allocates the first and second items to the highest and second-highest bidders, respectively.
