Abstract: An AI research agent can improve the score it sees without finding a modelling change that works on new materials. We ask a stricter question. After repeated experiments, does the selected change survive on data that never entered the loop, and can its code be reused? We separate the search into changes to features, models, representations, and training data. Seven searches produce 701 evaluated changes across ten Matbench endpoints.
