Abstract: Algorithms in multi-agent systems such as federated learning, mobile robotic swarming, and consensus control can be designed and analyzed as distributed stochastic approximation algorithms. Such algorithms involve information exchanges between agents for various computations. The freshness of the information can be quantified using the Age of Information (AoI) metric.
