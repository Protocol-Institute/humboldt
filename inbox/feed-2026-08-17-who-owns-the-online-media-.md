# Who Owns the Online Media?

**Source:** econ.GN updates on arXiv.org
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14141
**Date:** 2026-08-17
**Relevance:** Directly examines media ownership concentration and control structures, which is foundational for understanding media economics, democratic accountability, and corporate influence on information distribution.

## Summary

arXiv:2608.14141v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Ownership matters for the media's watchdog role. We map the ownership networks behind thousands of online news outlets in the U.S., Canada, and Europe. The networks reveal who is ultimately responsible for the news: for over half of the outlets, a single entity. The rest sit behind multi-layered structures, making responsibility hard to trace. Market concentration, measured comparably across countries, is largely low to moderate.
