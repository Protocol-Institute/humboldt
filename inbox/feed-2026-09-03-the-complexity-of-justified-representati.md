# The Complexity of Justified Representation with Additive Utilities

**Source:** cs.GT updates on arXiv.org
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02030
**Date:** 2026-09-03
**Relevance:** Directly addresses computational complexity of justified representation concepts in voting/allocation settings, which is a core topic in social choice theory and computational social choice.

## Summary

arXiv:2609.02030v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: We study the computational complexity of satisfying proportional representation -- in particular proportional, extended, and fully justified representation (PJR, EJR, and FJR) -- in participatory budgeting and committee elections with additive utilities. First, we give a complete picture of the complexity of the axioms for a constant number of voters or voter types. Second, we show that even for committee elections with integer utilities bounded above by a small constant, satisfying FJR is intractable, giving the first strong NP-hardness result
