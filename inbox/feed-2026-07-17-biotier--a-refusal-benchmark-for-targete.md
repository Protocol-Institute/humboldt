Abstract: As large language models become increasingly capable, concerns about their potential to assist with biological misuse continue to grow. Prioritization of safety differs across the model ecosystem, with some models freely providing high-risk information that could be misused, and others refusing benign scientific content, potentially hindering legitimate research.
