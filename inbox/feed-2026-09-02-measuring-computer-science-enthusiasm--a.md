Abstract: This study examines how age and gender independently shape adolescents' interest in computer science (CS) education. Building on the Person-Object Theory of Interest (POI), we define enthusiasm as a short-term, activating response that combines positive affect, perceived relevance, and intention to re-engage. Because such enthusiasm can shift CS attitudes and engagement intentions even briefly, it offers a useful measure for short outreach activities.
