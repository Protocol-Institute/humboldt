Abstract: Restricting access to a dual-use AI model is precautionary only if it delays harmful actors more than defenders. That condition varies across actors: a state agency or organized criminal group may obtain a substitute through theft, distillation, intermediated access, independent development, or a foreign release, while a small utility or open-source maintainer may have no comparable route.
