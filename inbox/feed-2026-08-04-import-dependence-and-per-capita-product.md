arXiv:2608.01010v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Food supply shocks in major producing economies can propagate through trade networks and generate uneven impacts across the global food system. This study examines the robustness of economies' food supply under production shocks to major producers in the global staple food system. Using 2023 production, reserve, and bilateral trade data for wheat, rice, maize, and soybean, we construct a calorie-based global food supply network across economies.
