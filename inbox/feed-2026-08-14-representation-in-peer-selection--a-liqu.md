## Summary

arXiv:2608.13085v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: We study the peer selection problem in which a group of peers must select a fixed-size committee from among themselves. To this end, we introduce a class of approval-based preference profiles, inspired by delegation graphs in liquid democracy and the transitivity of trust therein, which we call ``liquid profiles''. Under this new domain restriction, each voter designates a single trusted agent and, in turn, approves all those trusted by that individual.
