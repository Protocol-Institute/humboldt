## Summary

arXiv:2606.18684v2 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: Firms differ in manufacturing and foreign commercialization capabilities. We study how these differences organize exporting through multi-product export platforms. Products and increasingly costly platform slots form a transferable-utility assignment market. Complementarity between manufacturing and commercial capability generates positive assortative matching, while convex organizational costs determine platform scope.
