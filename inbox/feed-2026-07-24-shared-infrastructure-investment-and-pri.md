Abstract: We study a shared infrastructure deployed by an Infrastructure Provider (InP) and used by multiple firms generating revenues through resource usage. We focus on a challenging setting where (i) infrastructure deployment requires substantial upfront investment, which the InP recovers via payments by firms that depend on their uncertain future revenues; (ii) firms' resource usage is jointly influenced by exogenous factors, infrastructure pricing, operational costs, and resource congestion; and (iii) firms exhibit heterogeneous risk aversion.
