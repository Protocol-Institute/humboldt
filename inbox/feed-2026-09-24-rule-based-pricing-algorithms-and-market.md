Abstract: Rule-based pricing tools are widespread in digital commerce, yet we know little about how their design shapes market outcomes. In a controlled market experiment, participants use dashboards to build pricing algorithms competing in a sequential Bertrand game over multiple periods. We vary design features commonly found in commercial repricing tools: warnings about price wars, pre-configured strategies, and advice from a large language model.
