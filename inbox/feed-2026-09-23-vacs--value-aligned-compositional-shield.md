arXiv:2609.26135v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: Multi-agent reasoning systems in high-stakes domains must be both accurate and safe, yet agents often follow heterogeneous value priorities (e.g., rigor, conciseness, safety), causing conflicting recommendations. Existing methods do not jointly provide: (i) principled inference of each agent's implicit values from behavior, (ii) compositional formal safety guarantees without full online communication, and (iii) value-aware conflict resolution with faithful explanations.
