Abstract: Rapid bespoke commissioning of the Cognitive Digital Twin (CDT) is a major challenge in reconfigurable manufacturing. Traditional digital twin (DT) construction methods primarily focus on geometric reconstruction, often neglecting the deep semantic integration and functional interoperability necessary for autonomous reasoning. This paper proposes an agent-based, AI-driven workflow to automate end-to-end CDT debugging.
