## Summary

arXiv:2607.27429v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: LLM-agent systems can solve complex tasks through dynamic self-organization and emergent cooperation. Auditing this process is essential because plausible intermediate or final outputs can conceal incomplete or unsupported work and poorly allocated responsibility, ultimately compromising response quality. While existing approaches may record messages, tool calls, provenance, or task dependencies, an auditability gap exists as they do not jointly represent what work remains, who is responsible for it, and what evidence justifies each work-state t
