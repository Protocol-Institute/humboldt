Abstract: As LLM-based agents are increasingly deployed to negotiate, delegate, or transact on a user's behalf, software pipelines need runtime mechanisms to verify that an agent's stated intentions match its actual behavior. We study whether a lightweight, real-time chain-of-thought (CoT) monitor can detect strategic deception during asymmetric negotiations, using a used-car sales scenario where a seller agent has private knowledge of an undisclosed defect and a buyer agent has only public market data.
