Abstract: Autonomous AI agents in social media present concrete risks to democratic discourse and platform governance, while also offering tools for pre-deployment recommender system testing. A central open question is whether persona-prompted LLMs can simulate individual-level social media reactions with sufficient accuracy to support either application, and how accuracy depends on profile completeness, model selection, and the generalization challenge posed by novel post content.
