## Summary

arXiv:2609.09332v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: A language model can resolve a writer's current organizing problem while the construction needed for her own resolution remains unfinished. I call this early epistemic settlement. The supplied organization meets every demand then governing the passage, yet proceeding from it can displace work through which the writer would have changed those demands or become able to form further organizations.
