## Summary

arXiv:2608.07520v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Mass religious gatherings such as the Kumbh Mela concentrate tens of millions of people into a single region over a few weeks, producing intense, repetitive, multilingual, and safety-critical demand for information. The default response, a conversational assistant that routes every query to a large language model (LLM), is poorly matched to this setting: it is costly at scale, slow on emergency paths, prone to hallucination on facts that can cause physical harm, and unusable when connectivity fails.
