Abstract: This paper explores how financial institutions pass interest rate risk through to product markets using the life insurance industry as a setting. We show theoretically that it is optimal for insurers to distort product issuance across maturities to offset duration gaps. We examine insurers exogenously exposed to interest rate risk through their variable annuity liabilities after the 2008 financial crisis.
