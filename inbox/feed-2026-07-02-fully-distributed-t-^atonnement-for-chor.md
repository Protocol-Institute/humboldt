Abstract: We study price-adjustment dynamics for computing competitive equilibria (CE) in Fisher markets with chores. Unlike in classical goods markets, prices in chores markets are payments for taking on undesirable tasks, and natural excess-demand dynamics can fail; even the na\"ive analogue of Walrasian t\^atonnement may diverge. Recent work of Chaudhury et al. [2025] overcomes this obstacle via relative t\^atonnement, which subtracts the average excess-demand signal from the excess demand vector.
