Abstract: Recent industry practice has seen the rapid emergence of agentic systems composed of heterogeneous, tool- and LLM-mediated agent components, raising practical questions about inter-agent coordination and protocol design. This paper presents an implementation-grounded comparison of the Model Context Protocol (MCP) and the Agent2Agent (A2A) protocol, from a multi-agent systems engineering perspective, using an inter-agent coordination scenario involving LLM-based agents.
