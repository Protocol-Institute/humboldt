Abstract: Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly used for financial decision-making, yet it remains unclear whether improvements in reasoning quality translate into better economic outcomes. We investigate this question using a multi-agent debate framework for portfolio allocation in historical market simulations, where specialized agents propose, critique, and revise investment decisions.
