# A Simple Polynomial-Time EFX Repair for Cancelable Valuations

**Source:** cs.GT updates on arXiv.org
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08864
**Date:** 2026-08-11
**Relevance:** This paper advances the computational complexity of finding EFX (envy-free up to any item) allocations, a central problem in fair division theory.

## Summary

arXiv:2608.08864v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: The leximin++ proof of Plaut and Roughgarden for agents with identical monotone valuations gives a natural EFX-repair procedure: starting from an arbitrary partition, repeatedly transfer an eligible item to a minimum-valued bundle. The procedure terminates, but the standard argument gives no polynomial bound on the number of transfers, even for additive valuations.
