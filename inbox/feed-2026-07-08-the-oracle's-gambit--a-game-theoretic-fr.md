Abstract: Responsible vulnerability disclosure can secure the defender's head start by controlling when a vulnerability becomes public. However, this status quo is now challenged by increases in capability of AI models, which benefits both defenders and adversaries. When both sides draw their capability from the same AI model, the defender's head start depends on the lab's decision to release the model, and the question becomes not whether to release but how.
