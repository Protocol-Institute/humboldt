## Summary

arXiv:2608.06325v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: We study the existence of fair and efficient allocations of indivisible goods under the balancedness constraint, which requires that any two agents' bundles differ in size by at most one. Our main result establishes the existence of balanced allocations that satisfy envy-freeness up to one good (EF1) and fractional Pareto optimality (fPO) for arbitrary additive valuations.
