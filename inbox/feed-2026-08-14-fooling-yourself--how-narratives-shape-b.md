arXiv:2607.04753v2 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: Decision-makers often receive information through narratives combining diagnostic evidence, which favors one state over another, with nondiagnostic details, which provide no information about the true state. We study whether such uninformative details embedded in a narrative affect belief updating. In a laboratory experiment, participants report incentivized beliefs in a Bayesian updating task.
