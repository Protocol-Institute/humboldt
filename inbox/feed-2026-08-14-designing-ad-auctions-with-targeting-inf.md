Abstract: Digital advertising publishers sell ad inventory that conveys targeting information, such as demographic, contextual, or behavioral audience segments, to advertisers. While revealing this information improves ad relevance, it can reduce competition and lower auction revenues. To address this trade-off, we develop the Information-Bundling Position Auction (IBPA), a general auction mechanism for search and display advertising that leverages targeting information while preserving competition.
