# Non-Existence of EFX Chore Allocations for Monotone Cost Functions with Binary Marginals

## Summary

arXiv:2608.10572v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: We study the existence of envy-free up to any item (EFX) allocations of indivisible chores when agents have monotone cost functions with binary marginals. For indivisible goods, the corresponding existence question is known to have an affirmative answer for general monotone functions with binary marginals. For chores, however, the existence of EFX allocation was previously known only for more restricted classes, while the general binary-marginal case remained unresolved.
