## Summary

arXiv:2607.01059v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: In the fair allocation problem under conflict constraints, the goal is to partition the vertices of a graph among agents in a fair manner, such that no two adjacent vertices are assigned to the same agent. We study this problem for agents with common preferences through the lens of three fairness criteria: stochastic-dominance envy-freeness up to one item for preference orders (SD-EF1), envy-freeness up to one item for monotone additive valuations (EF1), and envy-freeness up to one item from each side for general additive valuations (EF[1,1]).
