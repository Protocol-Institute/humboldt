Abstract: We study fundamental information-theoretic limits of robust stochastic optimization when the distribution is known only through its exact moment sequence. We develop a unified framework that produces families of distinct distributions sharing all moments yet inducing radically different optimal decisions, thereby establishing strong impossibility results for a range of decision problems under moment ambiguity.
