Abstract: Mean field equilibrium (MFE) has emerged as a computationally tractable solution concept for large dynamic games. However, computing MFE remains challenging due to nonlinearities and the absence of contraction properties, limiting its reliability for counterfactual analysis and comparative statics. This paper studies dynamic models in which agents interact through a small number of functions of the population distribution, with particular emphasis on the scalar case.
