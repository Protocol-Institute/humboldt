arXiv:2607.13002v2 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: Competing firms increasingly delegate pricing and bidding decisions to algorithms supplied by the same third-party providers. We study whether a shared algorithm leads competitors to internalise one another's profits, using data from the Australian National Electricity Market, where every battery's bids are observed at 5-minute frequency and can be linked to an identifiable autobidding provider.
