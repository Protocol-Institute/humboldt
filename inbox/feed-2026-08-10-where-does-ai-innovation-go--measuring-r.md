## Summary

arXiv:2608.06903v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: The rapid growth of artificial intelligence (AI) in music has expanded research from generation and information retrieval to education, health, and governance. Yet this growth does not necessarily imply balanced research attention. Where is research attention directed across diverse music tasks, and how can such imbalance be systematically measured? Existing studies examine AI music from separate technical, application-specific, or bibliometric perspectives, but lack a systematic framework for measuring field-level imbalance.
