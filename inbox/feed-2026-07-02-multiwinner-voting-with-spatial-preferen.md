Abstract: In multiwinner elections with many candidates, as in participatory budgeting or large-scale recommendation, voters cannot plausibly evaluate every candidate, yet standard proportional-fairness guarantees such as EJR+ are stated for fully specified approval ballots. We ask whether strong proportional representation can still be guaranteed while eliciting only a little from each voter.
