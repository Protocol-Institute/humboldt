Abstract: This paper studies AI persuasion by distinguishing between two reasons for disagreement: attention differences, where the AI detects features the decision-maker missed, and comprehension differences, where the AI and the decision-maker interpret observed features differently. We show that AI is more effective in persuading the decision-maker when the disagreement is due to attention differences rather than comprehension differences.
