arXiv:2609.30896v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: A key aspect of culture is a society's norms about everyday behavior. How accurately do large language models (LLMs) represent cultural differences in such norms? To answer this question we used the recent Global Study of Everyday Norms (GSEN), which collected ratings of 150 scenarios in 90 societies, as the human benchmark. We prompted GPT-5 to estimate each society's average rating for every scenario, and later repeated the benchmark in three other LLMs: GPT-5.4, Claude Opus 4.6, and Gemini 3.1 Pro.
