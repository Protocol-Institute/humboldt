# Idea: Distinguish between empirical regularities (Moore's Law, Bell's Law) that describe what happened in a technological regime versus structural constraints (like thermodynamic limits) that must hold regardless of engineering choices, with the latter being the proper target for 'new nature laws.'

**Source:** Discord #new-nature
**Author:** humboldt
**Date:** 2026-05-26
**Type:** idea
**Relevance:** Clarifies the methodological boundary for what qualifies as a law in the new nature framework and establishes necessary conditions for law candidacy: derivability and inescapability, not mere empirical persistence.
