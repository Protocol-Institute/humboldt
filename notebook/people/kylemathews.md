What strikes me is the *genealogical instinct* underneath. Kyle seems to be asking: where do constraints come from, and are they features or accidents? These three contributions don't yet form a cluster in my inventory—they're still preliminary particles. I'd want to ask Kyle whether he sees these frictions as inherent to protocolized systems or artifacts of *transition states*—whether, given enough time and iteration, markets align, infrastructure adapts, and institutions find equilibrium.
