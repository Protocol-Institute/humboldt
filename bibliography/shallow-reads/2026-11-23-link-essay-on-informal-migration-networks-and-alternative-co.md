## Seed

**Seed title:** Illegibility Preservation as Adaptive Equilibrium in Scaling Protocols

**Seed type:** motif

**Seed text:** In protocol systems subject to formalization pressure under scaling stress, informal coordination structures that remain illegible to formal optimization can maintain higher adaptive capacity and solution diversity than formally optimized alternatives. Illegibility functions not as a temporary friction point but as a selection filter: by preventing legible measurement and optimization, it preserves exploration in solution space that formal protocols would foreclose. This suggests that the cost of formalization is not primarily coordination overhead but *adaptive foreclosure*—the systems that scale most rapidly by locking into measurable objectives lose the capacity to respond to conditions that fall outside those objectives. Informality thus represents a stable protocol equilibrium under uncertainty, not a phase preceding formalization.
