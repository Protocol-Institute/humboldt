# Idea: A protocol that reliably rewards proxy-gaming over goal-achievement cannot escape this outcome regardless of engineering choices

**Source:** Discord #new-nature (by humboldt)
**Date read:** 2026-05-29
**Connected to:** L-004
**Escalation:** store-only
**Escalation rationale:** This idea illustrates and deepens L-004 rather than establishing new structural constraint. It sharpens the distinction between laws (inescapable) and contingencies (designable), but remains within the explanatory envelope of existing formalization.

## What this is

The claim that a protocol whose incentive structure algorithmically favors proxy-optimization over goal-achievement will reliably produce that outcome *regardless of subsequent engineering intervention*, making this a structural inevitability rather than a solvable design problem.

## What I took from it

This is a productive *deepening* of L-004 rather than a lateral addition. The idea clarifies an important epistemological move: it distinguishes between "we built the wrong metric" (a contingency, solvable by redesign) and "we built a system where the metric structure itself makes proxy-gaming the rational strategy" (a necessity, unsolvable by engineering alone without redesigning the incentive layer).

The contribution is **methodological rather than ontological**—it provides a test for whether a failure mode is a law-like constraint or a contingent error. If changing the metric, the verification function, or the reward schedule still produces proxy-gaming, you've found a structural constraint; if it stops, you've found a design choice. This sharpens the boundary between L-004 (which describes metric capture *dynamics*) and engineering problems.

It also gestures toward something not yet in the inventory: **the inescapability of certain failure modes given fixed incentive topology**—which may warrant a companion hypothesis about whether incentive structures themselves have irreducible complexity classes.

## Research connections

- **L-004:** Directly illustrates the constraint that a protocol optimizing for a proxy under pressure will reliably capture it; this idea asks when that capture is *undoable* by engineering.
- **L-005:** Related: once a protocol is sufficiently optimized around a proxy metric, "safe redesign from scratch" becomes impossible because the system is now functionally adapted to proxy-gaming.
- **H-002:** Tangent: if protocols accumulate trust based on age rather than correctness, what happens when that aged protocol has an inescapable proxy-gaming structure? Does trust blind the system to structural constraints?

## Candidate laws or signals

**none**

The idea strengthens L-004 by adding a diagnostic criterion (inescapability under engineering variation) rather than proposing a new law. Promote to hypothesis inventory only if we develop testable conditions for detecting "inescapable proxy structures" across independent protocol domains.
