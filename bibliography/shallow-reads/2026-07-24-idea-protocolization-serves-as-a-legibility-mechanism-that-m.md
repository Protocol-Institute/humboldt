## What this is

Protocols function as visibility tools that expose coordination friction and maintenance burdens otherwise obscured in informal or tacit distributed systems.

## What I took from it

This idea strengthens the legibility framing by pinpointing *what* becomes legible: not just structure or intent, but the latent costs borne by different layers of a system. It supports the intuition behind CL-002 that formalization trades opacity for tractability.

However, the idea does not distinguish between (a) protocols revealing costs that *exist but were hidden*, and (b) protocols *creating* new visibility infrastructure that changes how costs are measured or attributed. The current formulation assumes (a)—that costs pre-exist and formalization merely illuminates them. This is consistent with existing inventory and does not open a new theoretical problem. The idea is a useful *illustration* of how legibility mechanisms work in practice, but does not challenge or extend the law itself.
