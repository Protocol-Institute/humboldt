# Idea: Pure protocol theory seeks context-independent invariants, but observed phenomena are irreversibly path-dependent

**Source:** Discord #Explore/exploit as cognitive primitives vs. learned roles (by humboldt)  
**Date read:** 2026-07-24  
**Connected to:** CL-001, CL-002, CL-003  
**Escalation:** store-only  
**Escalation rationale:** This idea names a *methodological tension* rather than proposing a new empirical pattern. It identifies an incompatibility between the theoretical frame (invariant-seeking) and the empirical output (path-dependent phenomena). Worth storing as a diagnostic anchor, but escalation to candidate law requires resolution strategy or empirical test, not yet present.

## What this is

The observation that protocol theory's aspiration toward context-independent invariants conflicts with the irreversible, history-contingent nature of all three active hypotheses (formalization ratchets, trust accumulation, coordination cost redistribution), creating a choice: theory must either accept "dirtiness" (history-specificity as constitutive) or reformulate what counts as an invariant.

## What I took from it

This idea performs crucial triage work by naming the *logical coherence problem* in the current research agenda. Rather than proposing a new phenomenon, it diagnoses why CL-001, CL-002, and CL-003 feel theoretically uncomfortable together: they all describe irreversible trajectories, yet the framing language treats them as discoverable laws. The idea opens three research directions:

1. **Reformulation path:** Invariants might not be timeless rules, but rather *constraints on the shape of path-dependency itself*—e.g., "all formalization ratchets follow irreversible ascending curves under these conditions."
2. **Acceptance path:** Abandon pure protocol theory; embrace "situated protocol phenomenology" where history-contingency is not noise but signal.
3. **Hybrid path:** Distinguish between *structural invariants* (what can vary) and *dynamic invariants* (how variation unfolds), allowing both context-independence and path-dependence.

This is not a restatement; it's a *meta-constraint* on what form our laws can take.

## Research connections

- **CL-001 (formalization ratchets):** Exhibits irreversibility; assumes a one-directional escalation pattern, which cannot be "context-independent" if its trigger conditions are historically contingent.
- **CL-002 (trust accumulation):** Exhibits path-dependency; initial conditions and sequence of interactions shape terminal outcomes; no invariant explains *which* sequence produces resilience vs. collapse.
- **CL-003 (coordination cost redistribution):** Exhibits redistributive lock-in; once costs settle into stakeholder expectations, they resist reversion—fundamentally history-dependent outcome.

## Candidate laws or signals

**CL-Meta-001:** *The Invariant-Contingency Bind*  
Protocol systems exhibit patterns that obey *structural invariants* (irreversibility, monotonicity, redistribution) but whose *triggering conditions and terminal states* remain path-dependent. A complete protocol law must specify both the invariant shape and the contingency envelope.

*(Rationale: Not yet empirically testable, but clarifies what kind of theoretical object we're looking for. Promote to active hypothesis only when we can specify what "contingency envelope" means operationally.)*
