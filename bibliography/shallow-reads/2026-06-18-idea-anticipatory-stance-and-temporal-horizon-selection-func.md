# Idea: Anticipatory stance and temporal horizon selection function as projections that select and discard dimensions

**Source:** Discord #Integration levels as ontological hierarchy? (by 4umd)
**Date read:** 2026-06-18
**Connected to:** none annotated
**Escalation:** store-only
**Escalation rationale:** Refinement of existing dimensional-selection intuition; no new mechanism identified. Useful conceptual bridge but requires mechanistic development before elevation.

## What this is

The claim that protocol design enforces anticipatory *constraints*—by encoding certain temporal horizons and forecasting modes while suppressing others—functions as a dimensionality-reduction operation, structuring what futures a system can "see" and therefore what present actions become possible.

## What I took from it

This idea genuinely refines rather than restates. It takes the integration-as-projection framework (dimensions revealed/hidden by path choice) and applies it to the *temporal axis*: not just which state-space variables are accessible, but which anticipatory postures are available to the system. A protocol that selects for 1-step lookahead vs. multi-step planning is not just *faster*—it's suppressing entire classes of futures from the system's decision topology.

The move is useful because it flags that anticipation is not epiphenomenal to protocol structure; it's *constitutive*. But the idea stops short of mechanism: *how* does encoding a temporal horizon actually eliminate a dimension? Does it compress the state space, or constrain the gradient landscape, or something else? Without that answer, it remains a useful analogy rather than a law.

It does open one clear research direction: whether different integration paths (the current framework) systematically correlate with *different* temporal horizons, suggesting that integration *depth* and anticipation *range* are coupled.

## Research connections

- **Integration-as-projection framework:** This idea applies the same dimensional-selection logic to time rather than state-space variables—a potential unification.

## Candidate laws or signals

**None.** The idea is a useful conceptual probe but lacks a testable mechanism. Worth revisiting if future work on integration paths reveals systematic correlations between path topology and temporal horizon constraints. Candidate for promotion: *after* mechanism is identified.
