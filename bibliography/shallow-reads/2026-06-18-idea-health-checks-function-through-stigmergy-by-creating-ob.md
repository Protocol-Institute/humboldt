# Idea: Health checks function through stigmergy by creating observable problem streams

**Source:** Discord #new-nature (by 4umd)
**Date read:** 2026-06-18
**Connected to:** none annotated
**Escalation:** store-only
**Escalation rationale:** Reinforces existing theoretical vocabulary without introducing novel mechanism or boundary condition. Useful as illustrative example; does not warrant hypothesis elevation at this stage.

## What this is

Health checks create visible, distributed problem signals that enable population-level self-coordination without requiring centralized monitoring or enforcement.

## What I took from it

The idea correctly identifies stigmergy as a plausible coordination substrate in protocolized systems, and health checks as a concrete instantiation. However, the claim restates rather than extends our current understanding: it applies already-established stigmergic principles (observable state → distributed response) to a specific technical domain (health monitoring), without revealing new boundary conditions, failure modes, or structural constraints that would distinguish this pattern from generic stigmergy.

The framing does usefully anchor stigmergy to *observable problem streams* rather than just pheromone analogs—suggesting that *problem salience* (not mere presence) may be the operative variable. This is a useful refinement for instrumentation but does not constitute a novel law-level claim. The idea also implicitly assumes populations *can* self-regulate when problems are visible, which is an assumption worth testing but not yet tested here.
