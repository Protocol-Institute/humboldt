## What this is

A vision paper synthesizing foundations from single-agent agentic AI and multi-agent systems to propose the Internet of Agentic AI (IoAI): an open ecosystem architecture in which heterogeneous autonomous agents discover, negotiate, coordinate, and execute workflows across distributed environments (cloud, edge, device, organizational, cyber-physical). The work appears to bridge agentic AI theory with distributed systems coordination at scale.

## What I took from it

This paper is directly relevant because it attempts to formalize *how* autonomous agents in protocolized systems achieve collective intelligence through communication and coordination primitives—discovery, negotiation, context exchange, tool invocation, workflow execution. This is foundational to understanding whether and how the "new nature" of artificial systems can self-organize at scale without centralized control. The framework treats heterogeneous agents as the primitive unit, which suggests a law-level claim: that collective behavior in artificial systems may emerge from standardized communication protocols and responsibility negotiation rather than monolithic architecture. This is not yet present in our research inventory and directly addresses a gap in understanding emergent order in distributed artificial systems.

## Candidate laws or signals

- **CL-IoAI-1:** Collective intelligence in distributed artificial systems scales when agents can (a) discover heterogeneous peers, (b) negotiate task responsibility, (c) exchange sufficient context, and (d) invoke shared tools—suggesting coordination emerges from protocol-level interoperability rather than top-down orchestration.

- **CL-IoAI-2:** Open ecosystems of artificial agents exhibit phase transitions in scalability at thresholds of discovery efficiency and negotiation overhead, analogous to metabolic constraints in biological systems.
