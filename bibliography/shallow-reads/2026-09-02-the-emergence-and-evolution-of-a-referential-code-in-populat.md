# The emergence and evolution of a referential code in populations of bee-like agents

## What this is

Agent-based evolutionary model studying how populations of communicating agents transition between direct referential codes (pointing) and indirect ones (reference frames decoupled from immediate geometry). The work uses the honeybee waggle dance as biological motivation and shows emergent coordination pressures driving formalization of communication protocols under environmental constraint.

## What I took from it

The paper demonstrates a concrete instance of **formalization pressure under constraint** (L-003 adjacent): when the environment makes the "natural" communication code (direct pointing) impossible, populations undergo a coordinated shift to a formal, standardized reference frame (gravity + sun position). This is mechanically interesting as a micro-example of how protocol ossification can emerge bottom-up through selection, not just top-down imposition.

However, the paper does not appear to examine the **reversibility** of this transition, the **cost of coordination failure** during transition periods, or the **resistance to switching back** once the indirect code is established—all of which are central to L-003 and L-010. The work is domain-specific (agent simulation of bee communication) and does not generalize beyond the communicative system studied. It confirms that formalization occurs under pressure, but does not isolate the mechanism governing adoption nonmonotonicity or reveal why populations sometimes resist formal codes even when beneficial.

## Research connections

- **L-003 (Formalization Ratchet):** Shows formalization pressure emerging from environmental constraint, but does not examine stability, reversibility, or resistance to change once formalized.
- **L-010 (Coordination Adoption Nonmonotonicity):** The work models adoption but appears not to investigate non-monotonic phases, defection incentives during transition, or pooling resistance.
- **seed-062 (Formalization Opacity Collapse):** Indirect: the shift from direct to reference-frame code may involve a legibility inversion, but this is not analyzed.
