**Escalation rationale:** Provides concrete historical instantiation of stigmergic coordination in textual systems. Stored as evidence and contextual depth for future pattern-matching.

## What this is

Legal glossators and Talmudic marginalia demonstrate that commentary accretes visibly around a stable core text, with each layer of annotation creating a spatial and semantic attractor for subsequent layers—a stigmergic feedback loop materialized in page layout and reading practice.

## What I took from it

This observation grounds H-stigmergy in concrete, long-duration human systems with documented genealogies. Rather than relying on algorithmic or digital examples alone, it anchors stigmergic coordination in pre-computational textual cultures, suggesting the pattern is not intrinsic to code but to *environments that store and display intermediate states*.

The claim also subtly shifts the mechanism: stigmergy is not just about chemical or digital signal trails, but about **visibility of accumulated response**. A gloss attracts a gloss because the previous gloss is spatially and semantically present. This implies that stigmergy requires a medium that permits *layering without occlusion*—marginal space, hypertext footnotes, threaded comments. Systems that bury or flatten previous contributions may suppress stigmergic accumulation.

Opens: What design properties of coordination media actively enable or inhibit stigmergic signaling? Is the Justinian/Talmudic model a template for protocol design?
