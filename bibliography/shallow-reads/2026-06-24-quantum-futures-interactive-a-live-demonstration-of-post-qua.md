## What this is

A demonstration/visualization tool for post-quantum cryptographic migration in blockchain systems. The work is primarily a pedagogical artifact combining interactive visualization with toy LWE-based protocol generation, aimed at bridging communication gaps across technical and non-technical stakeholders regarding quantum risk.

## What I took from it

This is an engineering communication tool rather than a theoretical or empirical investigation. The abstract signals awareness of fragmentation across research, engineering, governance, and investment communities—a coordination problem—but the paper appears to address this via *demonstration design* rather than by analyzing the mechanisms that produce such fragmentation or modeling how to resolve it.

The focus on "infrastructure tradeoffs" is suggestive but underdeveloped in the visible abstract. Without access to the full paper, it's unclear whether this characterizes actual tradeoffs between security, performance, and adoption latency in post-quantum migration, or simply illustrates them narratively. If the latter, this is a tool paper. If the former, it might warrant revisiting.
