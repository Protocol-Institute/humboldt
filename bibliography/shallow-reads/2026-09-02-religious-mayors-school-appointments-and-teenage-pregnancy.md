## What this is

A causal empirical study using regression discontinuity on Brazilian municipal elections, showing that Pentecostal-party mayors increase teenage birth rates, STD rates, and school dropout while reducing sexual education and HPV vaccination. The mechanism is bureaucratic appointment leverage: replacing principals and removing curriculum content to align schools with doctrinal positions.

## What I took from it

This is a clean instance of **institutional capture through formalization replacement** (L-003 candidate): a doctrinal movement wins formal executive power and uses legible bureaucratic levers (personnel appointments, curriculum removal) to reshape informal norms (sexual health behavior, educational content) toward doctrinal ends. The irreversibility and speed of effect suggest the Formalization Ratchet is real—once school principals are replaced and curriculum formalized around religious doctrine, reversal appears costly even when mayors lose office.

The paper also touches L-013: the school system tolerates anomalies (rising pregnancy, STD rates, vaccine resistance) without triggering formal intervention or protocol re-evaluation, presumably because the governing paradigm (religious authority over health curriculum) has shifted the frame within which anomalies are interpreted. Neither outcome nor data challenge the protocol; the protocol has been redefined.

However, this is a single-domain case study with strong local context (Brazil, Pentecostalism, municipal governance). It does not test generalization across protocol types, and the mechanism is straightforward institutional capture rather than a discovery about how all protocolized systems behave under appointment pressure.

## Research connections

- **L-003 (Formalization Ratchet):** Informal norms around sexual health are replaced by formalized curriculum and personnel decisions aligned to doctrine; the replacement is sticky and irreversible within an electoral cycle.
- **L-013 (Paradigm-Locked Anomaly Tolerance):** Rising pregnancy and STD rates are treated as acceptable outcomes under a shifted educational paradigm rather than triggering protocol review.
- **seed-061 (Proof Architecture as Governance Lock):** The formalized curriculum becomes the proof/record of legitimacy; its doctrinal content locks subsequent governance.
- **seed-079 (Externalization as Paradigm Preservation):** Health outcomes are externalized from the school's performance frame; only curriculum alignment matters.

## Seed

**Seed title:** Doctrinal Personnel Replacement as Irreversible Norm Ratchet
**Seed type:** observation
**Seed text:** When a formal institution's governing personnel are replaced by appointees aligned to an alternative normative framework (doctrinal, ideological, or otherwise), informal coordination norms embedded in that institution (curriculum content, resource allocation, behavioral expectations) shift durably even after the appointing authority leaves office. The shift persists because it is encoded in new personnel continuity and formalized institutional memory, and reversal requires explicit countervailing action by a successor. This suggests that **appointment-driven protocol capture** is a stable equilibrium in governance systems where formal authority to hire survives longer than electoral mandates. The mechanism may generalize beyond religious appointment to any ideologically motivated bureaucratic reshaping (e.g., algorithmic audit standards, loan underwriting criteria, content moderation policies).
