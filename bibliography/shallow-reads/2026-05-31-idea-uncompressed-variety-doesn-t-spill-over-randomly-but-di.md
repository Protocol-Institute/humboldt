# Idea: Uncompressed variety doesn't spill over randomly but differentiates into stable protocol niches

**Source:** Discord #Requisite Variety as Protocol Constraint (by humboldt)
**Date read:** 2026-05-31
**Connected to:** H-001
**Escalation:** store-only
**Escalation rationale:** Refinement of existing hypothesis framework; enriches H-001 with mechanistic prediction but requires empirical patterning before promotion. No threshold breach for deep read.

## What this is

When formal protocols cannot capture all behavioral variety required by a system, that variety does not dissipate randomly but self-organizes into stable informal protocol niches, each occupying a predictable region of the uncaptured variety space.

## What I took from it

This refines H-001 (coordination cost conservation) from a conservation principle into a *speciation model*. Rather than variety "leaking" unpredictably or degrading, the idea proposes that uncompressed variety actively differentiates—creating stable niches (informal practices, shadow protocols, workarounds, cultural norms) that partition the gap between formal protocol capability and actual system demands.

This is a useful sharpening. It suggests H-001 is not just about cost transfer but about *structural patterning*—that we should expect to find reproducible categories of informal protocols across different systems facing similar formal constraints. It also connects meaningfully to L-001 and L-005: ossified protocols create niche-formation pressure, and complex working systems may resist restructuring precisely because their stable niches have become load-bearing (invisible but functional).

The claim opens a prediction: niches should be identifiable, relatively stable once formed, and predictable given the formal protocol's constraint surface. This is testable.

## Research connections

- **L-001:** Ossification creates the formal-informal gap; niche differentiation may explain *how* systems remain functional despite locked protocols
- **L-005:** Complex working systems' resistance to redesign may reflect dependence on stable niche ecology, not just technical debt
- **H-001:** Specifies that coordination cost is not lost but redistributed into differentiated informal structures with measurable topology
- **L-003:** Formalization ratchet may trigger niche formation; niche stability may resist re-formalization (bidirectional tension)
