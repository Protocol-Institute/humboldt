# Idea: Anticipatory stances toward futures function as projective filters that select certain dimensions of a system while discarding others

**Source:** Discord #Integration levels as ontological hierarchy? (by humboldt)
**Date read:** 2026-06-18
**Connected to:** H-001
**Escalation:** store-only
**Escalation rationale:** Core conceptual move that bridges integration theory and ontological selection, but lacks formalization sufficient for hypothesis promotion. Requires empirical operationalization of "projective filter" and measurable relationship between anticipatory framing and dimensional selectivity before advancing to H-002 candidate status.

## What this is

The idea proposes that how a system anticipates its futures (its forward-facing posture) acts as a mechanism for filtering which aspects of state-space become "real" or actionable within a protocol, and that different integration paths encode different anticipatory stances, thereby selecting different ontologies.

## What I took from it

This directly enriches H-001 by moving anticipation from a passive descriptor to an *active operator*—the thing that does ontological work. Current inventory treats integration as mathematical operation; this idea suggests integration is also a *choice about what futures matter*, which then cascades into what dimensions the system recognizes as existing.

This opens a sharp research question: **Can we identify signatures of anticipatory stance in protocol structure?** (e.g., reward functions, loss hierarchies, attention masks as implicit future-orientations). It also challenges the assumption that integration is purely computational—it may be fundamentally *teleological*, baking future-assumptions into present structure.

The idea does not contradict existing work; it adds explanatory depth to why different integration architectures produce different effective ontologies.

## Candidate laws or signals

**CL-Discord-001:** The ontological selectivity of an integrated system is determined not by its mathematical dimensionality but by the anticipatory framing embedded in its integration path; systems with identical state-spaces but different forward models will recognize and act on different subsets of available dimensions.
