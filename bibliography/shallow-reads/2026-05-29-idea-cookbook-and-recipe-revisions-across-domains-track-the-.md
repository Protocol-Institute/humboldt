# Idea: Cookbook and recipe revisions across domains track the moment when tacit practices formalize, potentially correlating with community size inflection points.

**Source:** Discord #new-nature (by humboldt)
**Date read:** 2026-05-29
**Connected to:** L-003
**Escalation:** store-only
**Escalation rationale:** Proposes a methodological corpus and measurement approach for validating L-003, but does not itself claim a new law. Value lies in empirical operationalization rather than pattern discovery.

## What this is

A proposal to use diachronic analysis of cookbook and recipe documentation as a concrete corpus for detecting and dating the transition from informal, tacit practice to formalized protocol—and to correlate these moments with community growth thresholds.

## What I took from it

This idea treats L-003 (The Formalization Ratchet) as testable through material culture and textual history. Rather than abstractly asserting that stress/scale drives formalization, it identifies a measurable proxy: the revision signatures and rhetorical shifts in recipe texts. The insight is clever—recipes encode both tacit knowledge (early: "cook until it feels right") and formalized procedure (later: "325°F for 18 minutes, stirred at 6 and 12 o'clock"). 

However, the idea is primarily methodological. It does not advance a new pattern about protocol behavior; it operationalizes an existing one. The correlation claim with "community size inflection points" is suggestive but underspecified—it lacks a prior model of what inflection point matters (local cookbook adoption? cuisine professionalization? publication density?). This makes it valuable as a research *program*, but not yet as a law candidate.

## Research connections

- **L-003 (The Formalization Ratchet):** Direct empirical test case; recipes are concrete protocols under scaling pressure.
- **H-001 (Coordination cost conservation):** Potential signal: does formalization reduce local coordination cost while raising standardization cost? Recipes might show this trade-off clearly.
- **H-002 (Trust accumulation with age):** Tangent worth noting—older recipe texts may gain authority independent of their clarity or accuracy, a pattern consistent with trust-by-stability.

## Candidate laws or signals

None. This is a proposal for **method and corpus selection**, not a new law or hypothesis. Store as a research note under L-003 validation strategies. If comparative analysis of recipe revisions across (e.g.) culinary, software, craft, and scientific domains *does* reveal a consistent inflection point signature, escalate at that time.
