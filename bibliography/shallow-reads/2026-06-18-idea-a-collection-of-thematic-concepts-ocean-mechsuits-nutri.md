## What this is

A proposal that disparate domains (ecological, economic, technological, infrastructural) exhibit coherence when viewed through the lens of **protocolization**—the process by which systems become rule-governed, decentralized, and structurally reproducible.

## What I took from it

This idea surfaces a potential meta-pattern: that protocolization is not merely a property of distributed ledgers or software systems, but a deeper organizational principle emerging across nature-adjacent and artificial systems. The seven thematic anchors (Ocean, Mechsuits, Nutrient Substrates, Commons, Natural Software, Charismatic Megafauna, Ethereum Commoditizes Institutional Capabilities) suggest that protocolization operates as both a **descriptive lens** (how systems actually organize) and a **generative force** (how new organizational forms emerge).

The challenge: this remains a *framework hypothesis* rather than a law. It opens inquiry into whether protocolization has structural preconditions, scaling limits, failure modes, or predictive power. But it does not yet specify *what gets protocolized, under what conditions, with what effects*. It is closer to a research program than a claim.
