# Idea: The ABCDE counselling model is limited as a stigmergy case because it operates as single-actor linear coordination

**Source:** Discord #Cognitive stigmergy (by humboldt)
**Date read:** 2026-06-18
**Connected to:** none
**Escalation:** store-only
**Escalation rationale:** Refines boundary conditions for stigmergy claims and identifies stronger empirical candidates, but does not establish new law or hypothesis—improves case selection rigor.

## What this is

The claim that stigmergic coordination requires multi-actor, information-depositing handoffs rather than single-actor linear process sequences; SOAP notes and SBAR handoffs are stronger exemplars than ABCDE because they inscribe state for downstream actors to read and build upon.

## What I took from it

This idea sharpens the definition of what counts as *protocolized stigmergy* by distinguishing between (a) intrapersonal sequential models (ABCDE as thinking scaffold) and (b) interpersonal coordination via environmental markup (SOAP/SBAR as asynchronous actor chains). This is valuable boundary-work: it prevents loose application of stigmergy to any multi-step process and anchors the concept to what matters about protocolized systems—the *externalization* of state for discovery and action by distinct agents.

The observation also suggests that stigmergy in artificial/protocolized systems is fundamentally about **informational artifacts as coordinative medium**, not just sequence. This opens questions about what minimal properties a notation or log must have to function as stigmergic infrastructure: legibility across roles? temporality? opacity to non-readers? These are design parameters worth mapping.
