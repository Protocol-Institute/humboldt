# Idea: Protocol interventions that alter system parameters propagate through integral levels with observable effects on stocks or commitments

**Source:** Discord #Integration levels as ontological hierarchy? (by humboldt)
**Date read:** 2026-06-18
**Connected to:** L-001
**Escalation:** store-only
**Escalation rationale:** Operationalizes measurement of degrees-of-freedom reduction via parameter shift observation; extends L-001 without introducing novel mechanism or cross-level interaction pattern. Useful refinement of existing law but does not yet warrant promotion.

## What this is

Protocol parameter changes (e.g., interaction rate limits via quarantine rules) produce measurable effects across both stock and commitment integral levels, and the *pattern* of which levels respond reveals how much structural flexibility the system retains.

## What I took from it

This idea treats protocol intervention as a diagnostic tool rather than merely an operational lever. By observing *differential response* across integral levels—which level "absorbs" the parameter shift and which remains stable—we gain a method to infer system rigidity/flexibility. It refines L-001 by suggesting that DOF measurement need not require direct introspection; parameter perturbation followed by level-wise observation can reveal constraint topology indirectly.

The idea does not propose a new law of propagation or interaction; instead it operationalizes the observation plane. It assumes (reasonably) that stocks and commitments respond differently to protocol changes, but does not yet characterize *when* or *why* that differential response occurs. This makes it a methodological sharpening rather than a novel causal claim.

## Candidate laws or signals

**none** — This is a useful measurement protocol, not a candidate law. Promote to candidate law only if differential response patterns across levels begin to show consistent, lawlike structure (e.g., "commitments always lag stocks under rapid parameter shifts" or "stock-level changes only propagate to commitment level if X threshold is exceeded").
