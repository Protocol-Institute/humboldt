# Idea: Protocol design evaluation reveals consistent divergence between rulebook intent

**Source:** Discord #👾-protocol-fiction (by plague_year)
**Date read:** 2026-11-23
**Connected to:** L-023
**Kind:** content
**Escalation:** store-only
**Escalation rationale:** Restatement of L-023 mechanism with concrete game-system instantiation but no new structural claim about *why* formalization produces this effect or under what conditions the divergence accelerates, reverses, or stabilizes.

## What this is

Formal protocol specifications consistently fail to capture or enforce the actual coordination solutions that emerge in practice; informal mechanisms sustain functional outcomes the rulebook does not prescribe or cannot legibly control.

## What I took from it

This is direct empirical grounding for L-023 (Informality as Novelty Sink Under Legible Optimization) — the observation that when protocol obligations become formalized and subject to measurable enforcement, operational novelty and adaptive coordination migrate into informal, non-legible layers. The game-system context is valuable as a domain where the divergence is *designed-for and observable* (rulebooks are intentionally minimal; play is empirically rich).

However, the idea itself does not resolve the open structural questions: *why does formalization trigger this displacement?* Is it because formal specifications are necessarily underdetermined? Because agents exploit the gap between letter and spirit? Because legible optimization pressure makes novelty costly within formal space? The idea flags the phenomenon but doesn't propose a mechanism that would distinguish L-023 from simpler "rules are incomplete" claims.

The idea also doesn't clarify whether the informal layer is *functionally load-bearing* (the system breaks without it) or merely *parallel* (redundant coordination that could be formalized but isn't). That distinction matters for evaluating whether informality is a law or a symptom of specification failure.

## Research connections

- **L-023:** Direct empirical support for the core claim — formalized protocols shed functional novelty into informal channels under optimization pressure.
- **L-005 (Gall Generalization):** Related mechanism: complex systems cannot be safely restructured to eliminate informality because it carries load that formal specification never captured.
- **seed-461 (Proxy Opacity Under Nested Legible Enforcement):** If formalization creates legible proxies, informality may be the substrate that prevents those proxies from fully replacing original function.
- **seed-151 (Exception Formalization as Legibility Trap):** Formal protocols invite exception-specification, which then requires informal interpretation; the cycle regenerates the divergence.
- **L-004 (Goodhart Generalization):** Formal specifications may function as measurable proxies for unmeasurable coordination goals; informal play is what prevents metric capture from collapsing function.

## Seed

**Seed title:** Specification-Execution Divergence as Non-Recoverable Load Displacement
**Seed type:** observation
**Seed text:** In protocol systems where formal specification is intentionally or contingently incomplete, operational load migrates to informal coordination layers that are not legible to measurement or enforcement systems. The divergence persists even when participants and designers are aware of it, suggesting the formal layer and informal layer are not *redundant* but *complementary* — each carries load the other cannot. This may indicate that any protocol system attempting to eliminate informality by increasing formalization will experience cascading load displacement rather than substitution, up to the point of system failure or re-informalization at a higher layer.
