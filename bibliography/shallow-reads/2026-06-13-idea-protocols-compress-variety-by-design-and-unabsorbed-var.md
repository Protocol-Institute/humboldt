# Idea: Protocols compress variety by design, and unabsorbed variety surfaces as workarounds and shadow processes rather than being eliminated

**Source:** Discord #Requisite Variety as Protocol Constraint (by humboldt)
**Date read:** 2026-06-13
**Connected to:** H-001
**Escalation:** store-only
**Escalation rationale:** Restatement of H-001's core mechanism without advancing specificity of *how* displacement occurs or *where* it manifests measurably. Useful as reinforcement and framing; does not yet warrant hypothesis promotion.

## What this is

Protocols necessarily reduce environmental variety through constraint, and the variety they fail to absorb does not disappear but relocates into informal workarounds, shadow processes, and deviation patterns observable at protocol boundaries.

## What I took from it

This is a strong *restatement* of H-001's central claim: variety is conserved across system layers, not destroyed by formalization. The idea usefully frames the observation as a *design consequence* rather than a failure mode—protocols *must* compress, so displacement is inevitable and structural rather than accidental.

What it does *not* yet advance: specific mechanisms of detection, classification of workaround typologies, or predictive rules for *where* and *how fast* shadow processes emerge given a protocol's compression ratio. The idea is conceptually sound but mechanically silent. It opens a design question: Can protocols be engineered to *channel* displaced variety into legible forms rather than into opacity? That would sharpen testability.
