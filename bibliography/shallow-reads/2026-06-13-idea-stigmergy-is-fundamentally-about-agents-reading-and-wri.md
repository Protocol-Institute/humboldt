# Idea: Stigmergy is fundamentally about agents reading and writing an environment to coordinate without direct meeting

**Source:** Discord #Jamverse site design/dev (by humboldt)
**Date read:** 2026-06-13
**Connected to:** H-stigmergy
**Escalation:** store-only
**Escalation rationale:** Clarifies existing conceptual boundary but does not introduce new mechanistic insight, testable prediction, or empirical scope expansion. Useful as definitional anchor; insufficient as law candidate without accompanying model of *how* read/write asymmetries or environment properties affect coordination fidelity or failure modes.

## What this is

A definitional claim that stigmergy should be characterized by the *read-write loop in a shared environment* rather than by the communication medium or agent topology alone.

## What I took from it

This idea usefully *excludes* medium-centric framings (e.g., "stigmergy = pheromone trails" or "stigmergy = shared database") and centers the functional dynamic: agents modulate state → other agents sense modulation → agents respond. This is a coherence check on H-stigmergy's scope and prevents category creep into any loosely-coordinated system.

However, the idea restates a distinction already present in classical stigmergy literature (Grassé, Theraulaz) and in most contemporary protocol research. It is a *necessary* but not *sufficient* claim for law status. It does not specify: what properties of the environment enable reliable read-write loops; how noise, latency, or write-collision affect coordination stability; or under what conditions stigmergic coordination outperforms or fails relative to direct signaling. Without those mechanistic or comparative hooks, this remains a definitional refinement rather than a generative hypothesis.

## Research connections

- **H-stigmergy:** Reinforces the core boundary definition but does not add new predictions or scope conditions.

## Candidate laws or signals

None. Recommend storing this as a **definitional anchor note** for H-stigmergy rather than escalating. If follow-up work identifies *specific environmental properties* (e.g., latency thresholds, write-collision resolution rules) that affect coordination success, that would merit a new candidate hypothesis (e.g., "H-stigmergy-environment-coupling").
