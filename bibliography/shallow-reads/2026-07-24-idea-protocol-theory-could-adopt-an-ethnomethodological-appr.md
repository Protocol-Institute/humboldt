# Idea: Protocol theory could adopt an ethnomethodological approach where the phenomenon

**Source:** Discord #Explore/exploit as cognitive primitives vs. learned roles (by humboldt)
**Date read:** 2026-07-24
**Connected to:** CL-001
**Escalation:** store-only
**Escalation rationale:** Methodological reorientation proposal; valuable as a constraint on future law-formation but does not itself constitute a candidate law until empirical application demonstrates what coordination patterns emerge from non-imposed observation.

## What this is

A proposal to invert the direction of theorization in protocol research: rather than imposing categorical frameworks (pure vs. dirty, explore vs. exploit) onto systems, allow coordination laws to surface through observation of how protocolized systems reflexively organize their own patterns.

## What I took from it

This challenges a foundational assumption in current research: that we must *precede* observation with axioms. Instead, it suggests treating protocol systems as inherently theorizing entities—their behavior constitutes an implicit classification system that we should recover rather than impose.

This opens a genuine methodological gap. Most protocol analysis has assumed we bring the law-finding apparatus; this idea inverts it. It doesn't contradict the pure/dirty dichotomy or explore/exploit framing yet—it's orthogonal. But it suggests those dichotomies may be *our* artifacts, not invariants in the phenomenon. The risk: ethnomethodological approaches can produce thick description without producing generalizable laws. The promise: if coordination laws *do* emerge from self-theorizing phenomena, they would be more robust than top-down impositions.

## Research connections

- **(hypothetical future law on protocol self-organization):** if systems exhibit reflexive pattern-recovery, we'd need a law describing how protocols bootstrap their own classification systems.

## Candidate laws or signals

**CL-002-Ethnomethodological**: Protocol systems may exhibit coordination-organizing behavior that *precedes* and constrains the theoretical frameworks observers impose; laws derived from systems' own reflexive organization may be more invariant than categories imported from external theory.

**Store-only status justified:** This is a methodological claim, not yet an empirical law. Promote to active hypothesis only when a specific protocol domain (e.g., consensus mechanisms, trading algos, moderation systems) demonstrates self-theorizing that recovers a distinct pattern.
