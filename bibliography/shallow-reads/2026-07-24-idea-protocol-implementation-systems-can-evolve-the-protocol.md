# Idea: Protocol implementation systems can evolve the protocol itself in practice, making them fundamentally different from manufacturing processes with fixed tolerances.

**Source:** Discord #Interpretive tolerance as protocol slack (by 4umd)
**Date read:** 2026-07-24
**Connected to:** L-001, H-001
**Escalation:** store-only
**Escalation rationale:** Identifies a meaningful disanalogy between protocol and manufacturing systems, but the claim is currently a qualifier on existing frameworks rather than a candidate law. Warrants storage pending empirical cases that show *how* and *under what conditions* this evolution occurs.

## What this is

Protocol implementation systems possess feedback loops that allow the protocol itself to be revised through practice, whereas manufacturing tolerances remain fixed constraints—a structural difference that suggests protocols are fundamentally adaptive, not merely executed.

## What I took from it

This idea productively challenges the manufacturing analogy that may be embedded in L-001 and H-001. It correctly flags that protocols are not inert specifications subject to tolerance bands; they are living objects that can be reinterpreted, relaxed, or tightened through their own deployment. This opens a distinction between *protocol drift* (unintended variance) and *protocol evolution* (intentional or emergent adaptation), which may be critical for understanding formalization ratchets.

However, the idea remains at the level of *what is possible* rather than *under what conditions this happens, how consistently, and with what consequences*. The claim needs grounding: Does every protocol system evolve itself? Are there protocols that resist evolution? What determines whether evolution is reversible or ratcheting?

## Research connections

- **L-001:** This idea suggests L-001 may need a qualifier distinguishing protocols from manufacturing; protocols possess a reflexive adaptation capacity that fixed-tolerance systems do not.
- **H-001:** Directly relevant—if protocols evolve through implementation, formalization ratchets may be driven by this evolution mechanism rather than external constraint tightening.

## Candidate laws or signals

**None.** The idea is a useful *disanalogy marker* and should be stored as a persistent qualifier on manufacturing-protocol comparison. It becomes a candidate hypothesis only when paired with: (a) specific mechanisms of protocol self-evolution; (b) empirical cases showing conditions under which evolution occurs or is blocked; (c) claims about whether evolution is reversible or irreversible.
