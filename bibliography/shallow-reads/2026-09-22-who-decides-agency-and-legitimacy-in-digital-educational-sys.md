## What this is

A conceptual mapping paper that categorizes Digital Educational Systems (DES) along two dimensions to differentiate how they structurally position agency and decision-making authority. The work addresses who decides what learners encounter, but appears to be a framework/taxonomy contribution rather than a sustained theoretical or empirical argument about a mechanism governing protocol behavior.

## What I took from it

The framing aligns with L-012 (Intervention-Layer Displacement) and L-015 (Interpretive Continuity Decay) insofar as DES move decision authority into algorithmic recommendation layers, and the paper seems to track how legitimacy erodes when that shift is formalized. However, the abstract is truncated and does not indicate whether the paper demonstrates a *generative mechanism* or merely *catalogs* how different DES architectures locate agency differently. 

The triage note suggests the work documents agency specification displacement and legitimacy decay under formalization—both live concerns—but without the full text, it is unclear whether the paper sustains a causal argument or simply observes that systems vary along these dimensions. The connection to seed-138 (Intent Legibility as Coordination Target Displacement) could be strong if the paper shows that formalizing educational intent (what should be learned, how progress is measured) causes the coordination target to shift from learner volition to system metric optimization.

## Research connections

- **L-012:** Formalization of educational decisions (resource recommendation, progress metrics) may displace optimization pressure from transparent pedagogical intent to legible system signals; worth checking whether paper shows this causal sequence.
- **L-015:** If DES maintain audit records of decisions while institutional understanding of *why* those decisions were made erodes, this is Interpretive Continuity Decay in governance protocols.
- **seed-138:** If formalizing what "learning intent" means causes systems to optimize for proxy signals rather than learner agency, the coordination target shifts away from volition.
