# Idea: Cookbook and recipe documentation across food, code, and other domains capture the moment practices transition from tacit to explicit

**Source:** Discord #new-nature (by humboldt)
**Date read:** 2026-05-29
**Connected to:** L-003
**Escalation:** store-only
**Escalation rationale:** Proposes a concrete corpus and timing methodology for testing L-003; does not itself claim a new law or reveal an unanticipated pattern structure.

## What this is

Cookbook and recipe documentation across multiple domains (culinary, software, etc.) can serve as a timestamped artifact corpus for tracking when communities shift from oral/tacit practice transmission to written/explicit codification, potentially revealing inflection points where community size or coordination pressure triggers formalization.

## What I took from it

This is a methodological observation nested within L-003, not a challenge to it. The Formalization Ratchet already predicts that stress and scale pressure drive tacit norms into explicit protocols; this idea provides a way to *measure* that transition in real time across comparable domains.

The strength here is tractability: recipe documentation has known publication dates, versioning, and revision history. Food culture and software development both exhibit documented growth curves and community size milestones. Cross-domain comparison would allow us to test whether formalization pressure correlates with a universal scaling threshold (e.g., "formalization begins when communities exceed N participants") or whether it is domain-specific. This refines H-001 implicitly: if coordination cost is conserved, we should see formalization happen *earlier* in high-coordination-cost domains (e.g., safety-critical code) than in low-coordination-cost ones (e.g., casual cooking blogs).

The idea opens a useful direction: **protocol artifact archaeology**—using versioning history and documentation drift as proxy measures for when and how fast formalization pressure operates.

## Research connections

- **L-003 (Formalization Ratchet):** Provides a concrete measurement corpus and timeline methodology for testing the hypothesis that stress/scale/conflict trigger tacit→explicit transitions.
- **H-001 (Coordination Cost Conservation):** If formalization timing varies by domain, differential timing may signal that coordination cost is *not* uniformly conserved—or that domains with higher coordination cost formalize earlier, supporting the hypothesis.
