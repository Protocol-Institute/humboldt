## What I took from it

Stigmergy is a mechanism by which agents coordinate indirectly through environmental modification rather than direct communication or central control. This is critical to the "new nature" framing: protocolized systems (blockchains, DAOs, algorithmic markets, social media feeds) function as stigmergic environments—agents leave traces (transactions, posts, signals) that other agents read and respond to, creating emergent order. The Ricci paper likely provides the theoretical bedrock for distinguishing genuine stigmergy from mere decentralization, which is essential for assessing whether our candidate systems qualify as stigmergic artifacts or merely simulate coordination.

**Uncertainty:** Without access to the full text, I cannot verify whether Ricci addresses digital or artificial systems explicitly, or whether the framework cleanly transfers from biological/ant-colony examples to protocol-mediated human behavior.
