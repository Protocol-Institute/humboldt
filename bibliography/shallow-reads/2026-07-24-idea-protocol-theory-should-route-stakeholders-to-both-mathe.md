# Idea: Protocol theory should route stakeholders to both mathematical and non-mathematical tools depending on use case, with a 2x2 matrix mapping tool types against result types.

**Source:** Discord #Protocols as Total Cost Estimators (by 4umd)
**Date read:** 2026-07-24
**Connected to:** CL-001, CL-002
**Escalation:** store-only
**Escalation rationale:** The idea restates a structural pattern (2x2 routing matrices) without instantiating it with specific conditions, use cases, or decision rules. Until the routing logic itself is specified—what determines *when* formalization serves vs. obstructs stakeholder goals—this remains a meta-organizational claim rather than a researchable law. Worth monitoring for concrete implementations.

## What this is

Protocol design should include a conditional routing mechanism that directs stakeholders toward mathematical or non-mathematical tools based on their use case and desired result type, rather than assuming formalization is universally appropriate.

## What I took from it

This idea **refines rather than contradicts** CL-001 (formalization ratchet). The current law predicts monotonic pressure toward formalization; this idea proposes that formalization pressure is *conditional*—activating in some contexts and dormant or counterproductive in others. This is a useful boundary condition, not a refutation.

However, the idea remains structural rather than mechanistic. It names the shape of a solution (a 2x2 matrix) without specifying what goes in the cells or what decision rule selects among them. It is weaker than ideas that instantiate this routing (e.g., "formal tools reduce ambiguity cost but increase cognitive load; use formal tools when stakeholder heterogeneity > threshold X"). Until we can identify the *conditions* under which formalization becomes a liability, this remains a design principle rather than a law.

## Research connections

- **CL-001 (formalization ratchet):** This idea proposes that CL-001 applies conditionally, not universally. Suggests CL-001 needs a scope boundary: "...in contexts where cognitive load cost is secondary to ambiguity resolution."
- **CL-002:** If extant, this likely relates to cost trade-offs; the routing idea is a cost-minimization strategy that assumes CL-002's trade-offs are real and context-dependent.

## Candidate laws or signals

**None at this stage.** 

The idea is actionable as a design heuristic but not yet a law. To escalate to **H-Protocol-Routing-001**, we would need to specify: (a) the axes of the 2x2 matrix with measurable dimensions, (b) empirical or theoretical criteria for when each quadrant applies, and (c) evidence that routing *improves outcomes* relative to universal formalization. Store this as a design pattern to investigate further when concrete instantiations emerge.
