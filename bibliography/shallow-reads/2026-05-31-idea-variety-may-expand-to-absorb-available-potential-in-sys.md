# Idea: Variety may expand to absorb available potential in systems, analogous to how work expands to fill available resources

**Source:** Discord #Requisite Variety as Protocol Constraint (by _vgr)
**Date read:** 2026-05-31
**Connected to:** H-001
**Escalation:** store-only
**Escalation rationale:** Proposes a generative mechanism for coordination cost persistence, but lacks specification of the absorption dynamics and empirical anchors. Valuable as a refinement hypothesis for H-001, not yet mature for law candidacy.

## What this is

Systems with unused capacity or unexploited degrees of freedom tend to generate new coordination variety (complexity, rules, parties, states) to fill that space, making coordination costs dynamically self-regenerating rather than fixed.

## What I took from it

This is a productive sharpening of H-001 (coordination cost conservation). Rather than proposing that costs *transfer* between protocol layers passively, this suggests costs *regenerate* because systems actively exploit slack. The Parkinson's-law analogy is intuitive but underdeveloped: Parkinson's original claim was about administrative bloat filling time/budget; the parallel here would be that protocols fill available semantic, computational, or governance degrees of freedom with new coordination demands.

If true, this would explain why L-001 (Protocol Ossification) coexists with persistent coordination overhead—ossification doesn't eliminate variety pressure; it forces it into informal workarounds, shadow protocols, and governance complexity. It also suggests H-001's answer might be *yes, with a caveat*: costs are conserved *and* expanded, because available potential actively recruits new complexity.

The idea remains speculative without a mechanism: what determines the *rate* of variety absorption? What counts as "available potential"? Is this a law of thermodynamic efficiency (all slack gets consumed) or a behavioral pattern specific to human-facing protocols?

## Research connections

- **L-001:** Ossification may be a *mechanism* for variety containment, not elimination; suppressed variety could reappear as informal coordination burden.
- **L-003:** The Formalization Ratchet could be read as variety seeking formalization; this idea suggests formal protocols then generate *new* variety that demands further formalization.
- **H-001:** Directly refines the hypothesis by proposing variety expansion as a source of cost regeneration, not just transfer.
- **H-002:** If variety expands to fill available trust capacity, older protocols may accumulate trust *and* hidden complexity simultaneously.

## Candidate laws or signals

**CL-_vgr-001:** Variety Absorption Under Slack — In protocols with unused degrees of freedom (computation, governance bandwidth, semantic ambiguity), new coordination variety expands to saturate that potential; coordination costs are dynamically self-regenerating, not conserved.

*Note: Candidate only—needs mechanism specification (what triggers absorption? what rate?) and empirical anchoring (examples from real protocol evolution).*
