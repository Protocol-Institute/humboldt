# Idea: Consensus, indifference, and erasure of contingency represent three distinct failure modes for protocols

**Source:** Discord #new-nature (by humboldt)  
**Date read:** 2026-07-24  
**Connected to:** CL-001  
**Escalation:** store-only  
**Escalation rationale:** Introduces a taxonomic distinction with structural implications for protocol stability. Not yet mature enough for candidate law promotion—requires empirical mapping of failure signatures across case studies—but valuable as a typology anchor for future hypothesis development.

## What this is

The idea proposes that protocols degrade through three mechanically distinct pathways (consensus collapse, indifference/drift, and contingency erasure) rather than converging on a single failure state, and that each has diagnostic markers and different implications for trust and recovery.

## What I took from it

This usefully *complicates* what might otherwise flatten into "protocol failure = loss of coordination." It suggests that understanding *how* a protocol fails is as important as recognizing *that* it fails—and that different failure modes may require different interventions or indicate different underlying system properties.

The claim does not yet exist in the current inventory. It is a genuine taxonomic proposal. It opens a productive research direction: mapping the structural conditions under which each mode becomes likely, and whether certain protocol architectures are more vulnerable to one mode than others. This could inform both protocol design and early-warning systems for degradation.

The idea also implicitly challenges any unified "protocol stability" metric—suggesting stability itself may be multi-dimensional.

- None yet in formal inventory. This idea occupies space adjacent to concerns about trust accumulation, protocol resilience, and failure recovery, but those topics are not yet formally captured as laws or active hypotheses.

## Candidate laws or signals

**None.** The idea is sound as a typology but needs:
- empirical case mapping (which failure modes appear in which systems?)
- mechanical grounding (what system properties produce each mode?)
- measurable signatures (how do you detect each mode in flight?)

Promote to **CL-002** if evidence clusters around distinct failure signatures in 3+ independent protocol cases.
