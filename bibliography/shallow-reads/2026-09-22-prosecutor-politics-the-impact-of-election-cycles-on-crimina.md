## What this is

Empirical economics paper studying how U.S. criminal sentencing (1986–2006) varied with district attorney election cycles across 40 states. Shows 3% increase in prison admissions during election years, concentrated in Southern and Republican counties, declining as public sentiment shifted — a behavioral response to electoral incentives within an established institutional protocol.

## What I took from it

This is a clean observational confirmation of metric capture (L-004) within a safety-critical protocol domain: prosecutors optimize for a legible proxy (tough-on-crime signaling at election time) rather than the unmeasurable goal (justice/proportionality), and the system tolerates this distortion across a 20-year period. The effect persists despite stable underlying criminal behavior, evidence of paradigm-locked anomaly tolerance (L-013) — the criminal justice protocol accumulates operational pathology (systematic over-sentencing correlated with electoral cycles, not crime) without institutional corrective mechanisms triggering.

The paper does not theorize the mechanism or generalize beyond criminal justice. It treats election-cycle sentencing variation as a straightforward behavioral economics finding: rational agents (prosecutors) respond to incentives (electoral success). It does not investigate whether the protocol itself *enables* or *forces* this capture, nor does it ask why the anomaly persists so long within a system that ostensibly claims to prioritize consistency and fairness.

## Research connections

- **L-004 [Goodhart Generalization]:** Prosecutors optimize for "tough on crime" visibility (measurable proxy) rather than appropriate punishment severity (unmeasurable goal); under election pressure, the proxy dominates behavior, distorting outcomes.
- **L-013 [Paradigm-Locked Anomaly Tolerance]:** The criminal justice system accumulates evidence of systematic, predictable sentencing bias (election-correlated, not crime-correlated) across 20 years without triggering structural reform or formal acknowledgment of the pathology.
- **seed-128 [Legibility-Driven Agent Convergence Under Computable Audit]:** Election results and sentencing records are both legible; prosecutors converge on election-aligned behavior because the signal is crisp and the audit trail transparent.

## Seed

**Seed title:** Audit Trail Legibility as Perverse Synchronization Force in Safety-Critical Governance

**Seed type:** observation

**Seed text:** In safety-critical protocols (criminal justice, medical licensing, environmental enforcement) where agent behavior is audited via legible records (case outcomes, sentencing length, approval rates), agents may synchronize on externally-driven signals (electoral cycles, media attention, regulatory fashion) rather than protocol-internal goals, precisely *because* the audit trail makes deviation visible. Legibility intended to enforce consistency instead creates a coordination surface for collineation with salient external incentives. The protocol tolerates this because the anomaly is distributed (all prosecutors shift together) and rationalized post-hoc as within-protocol variance.
