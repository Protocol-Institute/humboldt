## What this is

An agent-based model comparing legislative passage rates across four institutional designs (parliamentary, presidential, premier-presidential, mixed) under varying party-system fragmentation. The work isolates three competing micro-mechanisms—coalition formation, party discipline, and committee gatekeeping—to explain why passage collapses under fragmentation, primarily by manipulating agent behavior rules in silico.

## What I took from it

The paper demonstrates empirically that formalization pressures (party discipline rules, gatekeeping protocols, coalition thresholds) interact nonlinearly with fragmentation in ways that existing informal norms cannot stabilize. This is consistent with L-003 (Formalization Ratchet) insofar as institutional stress (fragmentation) forces legibility of previously informal coordination (vote-buying, side-deals, implicit party structures). However, the work remains domain-specific: it models *one* class of coordination failure (legislative passage) under *one* stress condition (party proliferation), without claiming or testing generalization to other protocol systems.

The framing also resonates with L-013 (Paradigm-Locked Anomaly Tolerance): legislatures continue operating under institutional designs that demonstrably fail to produce output under foreseeable conditions, yet resist structural reform. But again, this is observation-level only—no mechanism for why the anomaly persists is offered.

## Research connections

- **L-003:** Confirms that fragmentation drives formalization of coordination rules (discipline enforcement, coalition criteria), but does not explain *why* formalized rules fail to restore passage where informal ones do.
- **L-013:** Observes that legislatures tolerate persistent passage collapse under fragmentation without triggering institutional redesign; offers no explanation for anomaly persistence.
- **seed-070:** Gestural connection to coordination-as-infrastructure-constraint; suggests that as parliamentary protocols become more legible (party discipline, explicit coalition thresholds), they become more brittle under structural stress.
