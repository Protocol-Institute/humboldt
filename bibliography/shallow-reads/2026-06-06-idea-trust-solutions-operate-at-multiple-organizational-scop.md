## What this is

Trust accumulation functions may not be universal across organizational boundaries; instead, intra-organizational and inter-organizational trust may follow distinct temporal and accumulation patterns.

## What I took from it

This directly pressures H-002 (age/stability as universal trust driver) by introducing a boundary condition: the hypothesis may hold *within* a single organization but fail *across* organizations, or vice versa. Rather than invalidating H-002, this suggests the need for scope-stratified modeling—trust drivers may bifurcate at organizational perimeters.

The idea is a genuine addition because it identifies a structural variable (scope membership) that the current hypothesis inventory treats as homogeneous. It opens a question about whether trust is a single phenomenon or a family of related phenomena, and whether protocol design should target scope-specific trust solutions rather than universal ones.
