# Idea: Protocolization functions as a mechanism for decentralization, following a pattern of centralizing until coordination costs become unsustainable, then decentralizing until equilibrium is reached

**Source:** Discord #🎩-formal-protocol-theory (by _vgr)
**Date read:** 2026-07-24
**Connected to:** CL-002
**Escalation:** store-only
**Escalation rationale:** The idea restates an oscillation pattern already captured in CL-002 but adds a mechanistic hypothesis (coordination cost threshold) without sufficient empirical grounding or formal cost model. Worth preserving as a refinement vector but not yet a distinct law. Recommend deepening before promotion.

## What this is

Protocolization is proposed as an active regulatory mechanism that oscillates systems between centralized and decentralized states by responding to coordination cost pressure, with equilibrium as the attractor.

## What I took from it

This idea strengthens CL-002 by naming the *driver* of oscillation (coordination costs) rather than merely describing the pattern. It moves from observation to mechanism. However, it remains underspecified on two critical points: (1) how coordination costs are measured or bounded in protocolized systems, and (2) whether "equilibrium" is stable or itself a dynamic state subject to external perturbation. The claim treats protocolization as a *reactive* stabilizer, which conflicts with or complements—but does not replace—any account of protocolization as *constitutive* of system identity. This opens a productive tension: Is protocolization the process systems *use* to manage cost, or the ontological form that cost-bearing systems *take*? The idea is useful precisely because it forces this distinction into focus.

## Research connections

- **CL-002:** Directly restates the oscillation pattern; this idea proposes coordination cost as the mechanism driving CL-002's observed motion.
- **Coordination burden:** This idea implies that coordination burden is not exogenous but becomes a system parameter that protocols actively sense and respond to.

## Candidate laws or signals

**None.** CL-002 already captures the oscillation pattern. This idea is a mechanistic refinement of CL-002, not a distinct law. Promote to candidate hypothesis only if: (a) a formal cost model is articulated, or (b) empirical cases show consistent threshold behavior. Store as a *refinement vector* for deepening CL-002.
