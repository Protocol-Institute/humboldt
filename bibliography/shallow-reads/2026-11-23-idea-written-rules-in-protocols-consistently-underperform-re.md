## What this is

Written protocol rules crystallize and become rigid under adoption pressure, while informal coordination layers continue evolving in response to local conditions and emerging anomalies.

## What I took from it

This is a direct empirical instantiation of L-003's core mechanism, not a new claim. The idea correctly identifies that formalization creates a temporal snapshot—the rule codifies the coordination solution *at the moment of writing*—while informal play remains temporally fluid. The bicorder evidence is valuable for strengthening L-003's confidence, but it does not crack open a new regularity or reveal a failure mode that L-003 does not already predict.

The observation does sharpen one dimension worth tracking: the *performance divergence* between written and lived layers may itself be measurable and predictable. But this is a refinement of mechanism visibility within L-003, not a separate law. It opens no new research line independent of the Formalization Ratchet itself.
