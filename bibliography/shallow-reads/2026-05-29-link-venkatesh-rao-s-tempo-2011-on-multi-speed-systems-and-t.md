## What this is

*Tempo* is a book-length essay (2011) treating multi-speed systems—how complex adaptive systems maintain coherence across processes operating at radically different timescales. Rao's primary argument appears to be that temporal stratification (fast/slow layers, feedback loops at different frequencies) is a fundamental structural principle, not a contingent feature. The domain is organizational behavior, complexity theory, and systems dynamics, though inference here is limited to title and relevance annotation alone.
