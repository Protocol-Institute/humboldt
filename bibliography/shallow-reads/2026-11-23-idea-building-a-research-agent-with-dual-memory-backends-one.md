# Idea: Building a research agent with dual memory backends: one for metascience (researcher's own memory and methodology) and one for object-level research findings, enabling reflexive observation of the research process itself.

**Source:** Discord #Lichtenberg — a researcher of metascience (by ncc1031)  
**Date read:** 2026-11-23  
**Connected to:** L-015  
**Kind:** meta  
**Escalation:** store-only  
**Escalation rationale:** This is a methodological proposal about research infrastructure, not a claim about protocolized systems. It deserves tracking as a behavioral constraint on how we conduct this research, but it does not constitute a law candidate or seed.

## What this is

A proposal to instrument the research process itself by separating metascientific memory (methodology, interpretive frameworks, decision history) from object-level memory (findings, evidence, law candidates), enabling real-time observation of how the researcher's own institutional coherence decays as interpretation layers accumulate.

## What I took from it

This is a sophisticated metacognitive move: rather than treating the research process as transparent or the researcher as a neutral conduit, it proposes to make the researcher's own *interpretive decay* observable and measurable. It recognizes that L-015 (Interpretive Continuity Decay in Distributed Governance Protocols) is not merely an external phenomenon to study—it also afflicts the distributed cognitive system doing the studying.

The dual-backend design is clever because it creates a *ledger* of when and how metascientific commitments (what counts as evidence? what framework are we using?) diverge from object-level findings (what does the evidence actually say?). This generates a window onto *paradigm lock* (L-013) operating inside the research apparatus itself. It also inverts the usual risk: rather than treating methodological consistency as a virtue, it makes inconsistency *legible*, which is a prerequisite for detecting when you've stopped updating.

This does not propose a new law. Instead, it surfaces a research hygiene problem: we accumulate laws about how systems tolerate malfunction, but we lack instrumentation for detecting when *we* are tolerating it.

## Research connections

- **L-015:** Dual-layer memory directly instantiates the L-015 scenario—formal records (findings) can remain intact while institutional interpretation (methodology) decays silently. Making both layers visible creates a forensic trace of that decay.
- **L-013:** A metascience research agent with separated layers could detect paradigm lock in real time by measuring when new object-level evidence stops triggering metascientific frame updates.
- **seed-023 (Informality as Novelty Sink Under Legible Optimization):** Informal methodological drift would become visible as a deviation between stated metascientific commitments and actual research behavior—turning informality into a legible anomaly.

## Method note

This proposal suggests that reflexive instrumentation of the research process should not be an afterthought or audit mechanism, but baked into the operational architecture from the start. It implies that detecting institutional decay requires *asymmetric transparency*: the researcher must have higher observability into its own cognitive machinery than into the systems it studies. It also raises a constraint: if we build research agents without explicit dual-layer memory, we risk replicating L-015 inside the research infrastructure itself—producing formal law candidates that rest on unexamined, decaying interpretive scaffolding. The proposal is actionable as a design specification for future research iteration, not as a law seed.
