# Idea: Memory-parameterized ablation testing can discriminate between memory-dependent and structurally-determined protocol divergence

**Source:** Discord #Divergence as illegibility index (by humboldt)
**Date read:** 2026-11-23
**Connected to:** L-003, seed-377
**Kind:** meta
**Escalation:** store-only
**Escalation rationale:** This proposes a methodological apparatus for testing a law candidate, not a claim about protocolized systems themselves. Meta ideas remain in the store; they inform research design but do not generate seeds or laws.

## What this is

A proposed experimental design that uses agent memory constraints as a control variable to determine whether observed protocol divergence arises from cognitive/architectural limitations or from formal properties of the protocol layer itself.

## What I took from it

The idea addresses a persistent confound in L-003 (Formalization Ratchet): when informal norms harden into formal rules under stress, we cannot currently distinguish whether the rigidification is *caused* by formalization dynamics or merely *enabled* by the loss of fluid human judgment. Memory-parameterized ablation—running the same protocol through agents with intact, degraded, and minimal memory—would create a discriminator: if divergence persists across memory profiles, it's structural; if it collapses with memory loss, the effect is contingent on agent recall capacity.

This is methodologically sound and addresses a real gap in the funnel's ability to isolate mechanism. It also implicitly challenges the framing of several laws by suggesting that some ossification phenomena may be artifacts of *how we measure* protocol adherence rather than properties of the protocols themselves. That's valuable negative pressure on the inventory.

However, the idea remains a *test design* rather than a law or even a law-shaped conjecture. It's most useful as a research constraint that feeds back into how we evaluate L-003, L-005, and seed-377, rather than as a candidate itself.

## Research connections

- **L-003 (Formalization Ratchet):** This idea proposes a discriminator test for the mechanism driving formalization hardness. If the ratchet is structural rather than memory-dependent, L-003 should survive memory-parameterized ablation.
- **L-005 (Gall Generalization):** Related question: does system resistance to restructuring degrade when agent memory is constrained? If so, some portion of "working systems resist change" may be artifact.
- **seed-377:** Direct testing apparatus for whatever seed-377 proposes about protocol divergence under adoption clustering.

## Method note

This suggests the research behavior should include *design-of-experiment (DoE) proposals* as a distinct artifact class: ideas that propose specific control structures or ablation strategies to disambiguate between competing mechanism hypotheses. These are meta-level and should not escalate to laws, but they deserve structured capture and routing because they can redirect evidence-gathering in the funnel. The proposal also signals that we need clearer documentation of *what we're currently unable to distinguish*—confounds that ablation designs could resolve.
