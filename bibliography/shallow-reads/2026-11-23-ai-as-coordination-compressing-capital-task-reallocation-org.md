## What this is

An economic model of organizational structure under AI-mediated task compression, treating AI as capital that reduces per-link coordination costs toward positive floors. The work studies wage dispersion and span-of-control dynamics when coordination floors are positive versus zero, holding managerial pools and team quality constant.

## What I took from it

The paper operationalizes L-006 (Coordination Cost Conservation) by formalizing what happens when a protocol layer (AI-mediated task handling) compresses coordination costs: the model predicts that *under positive-floor conditions*, long-run wage dispersion becomes floor-determined rather than execution-compression-determined. This is a tractable instantiation of cost conservation — compression at one layer (task execution) is offset by redistribution pressure at another (managerial hierarchy).

However, the work does not engage with L-003 (Formalization Ratchet) meaningfully. It assumes organizational structure is exogenously fixed and coordination floors are parametric. It does not model how stress, conflict, or scaling pressure might *drive* the formalization of those floors in the first place, nor how informal coordination norms might be replaced under adoption pressure. The paper is bottom-up mechanistic but lacks the top-down institutional pathway. It also does not address what happens when floors reach zero or become *negative* (i.e., when coordination cost genuinely vanishes), which is the regime fork promised in the title but apparently not delivered in the abstract.

## Research connections

- **L-006:** Direct instantiation of cost conservation across organizational layer transitions; confirms that compression at one layer (execution) redistributes pressure, not eliminates it.
- **L-003:** Mentioned in triage but not substantially developed; the work does not model how formalization of coordination floors emerges under stress.
- **seed-553:** Coordination infrastructure formalization as legibility-without-cost-reduction — the floor structure may increase measurability without reducing total organizational friction.
- **seed-467:** Coordination structure elasticity under difficulty variance — unclear whether the model's floors are invariant to operational stress or regime shift.

## Seed

**Seed title:** Coordination Floor Regime Nonmonotonicity Under Automation
**Seed type:** question
**Seed text:** When AI compresses task execution costs toward a positive coordination floor, does the organizational equilibrium bifurcate between (1) persistent hierarchy with floor-determined wage dispersion, and (2) regime shift toward flat or fluid structures when floors approach zero? In particular: does the transition zone exhibit nonmonotonic stability — are there automation levels at which coordination *increases* before compression resumes? The mechanism would generalize to any protocol system where cost compression is bounded by an irreducible verification or decision layer.
