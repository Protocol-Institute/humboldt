# Idea: Protocol definitions should establish feature control frames (analogous to GD&T)

**Source:** Discord #Interpretive tolerance as protocol slack (by 4umd)
**Date read:** 2026-07-24
**Connected to:** L-001, H-001
**Escalation:** store-only
**Escalation rationale:** Operationalizes an existing hypothesis mechanic (tolerance formalization under scaling pressure) without introducing new systemic dynamics; useful refinement but doesn't yet warrant independent candidate status.

## What this is

Proposes formalizing protocol tolerance via explicit feature control frames—analogous to geometric dimensioning & tolerancing (GD&T) in manufacturing—that define allowable payload/sequence deviations and boundary enforcement rules.

## What I took from it

This idea takes the intuition behind H-001 (informal slack → explicit boundaries under scaling) and gives it a concrete operational shape: rather than *discovering* where protocols break, *design* tolerance zones into their definitions from the start. This is a useful operationalization move—it shifts from reactive formalization to proactive specification.

The connection to GD&T is particularly clarifying: GD&T doesn't eliminate deviation; it *bounds and communicates* it. Applying this pattern to protocols suggests that the question isn't "how do we prevent all variation?" but "what variation is safe, and how do we make that legible?" This refinement sharpens the formalization ratchet hypothesis by showing *how* informality gets institutionalized rather than just *that* it does.
