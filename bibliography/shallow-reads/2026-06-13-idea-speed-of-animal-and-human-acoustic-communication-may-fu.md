# Idea: Speed of animal and human acoustic communication may function as a fundamental token or protocol-level constraint governing 'protocolese'

**Source:** Discord #Animal acoustic communication has a cons... (by ncc1031)
**Date read:** 2026-06-13
**Connected to:** L-acoustic-constraints, H-protocolese-speed
**Escalation:** store-only
**Escalation rationale:** Idea proposes a concrete constraint mechanism but lacks formal scope definition and empirical grounding. Warrants hypothesis development before law candidacy. Store for maturation and cross-domain testing.

## What this is

Communication speed (temporal bandwidth) may operate as a primitive rule-unit that constrains how protocols can form, nest, and be interpreted across biological and artificial systems.

## What I took from it

This idea usefully materializes what has been implicit in protocol theory: that *timing* is not merely a performance parameter but a structural constraint on what can be expressed and how. It moves beyond treating speed as peripheral to treating it as foundational—a parameter that determines feasibility of nested protocols, error correction overhead, and interpretability thresholds.

The claim is genuinely new to the inventory if it asserts that speed operates at the **token level** (i.e., constrains primitive rule-units themselves, not just their deployment). This would distinguish it from standard information-theoretic bandwidth constraints. However, the idea needs formal specification: Does this apply uniformly across biological, digital, and hybrid systems? What is the unit of "speed" here—bits/sec, decisions/sec, or something else? The triage note correctly identifies that constraint scope remains unspecified.

## Research connections

- **L-acoustic-constraints:** Overlaps but appears narrower—focused on acoustic modality specifically. Current idea may generalize to all time-dependent communication.
- **H-protocolese-speed:** This is the natural hypothesis anchor. Idea strengthens it by suggesting speed is not a variable but a *limiting primitive*.

## Candidate laws or signals

**H-protocolese-speed-primitive:** Communication speed functions as a primitive constraint on protocol token formation. Corollary: protocols optimized for different speeds (animal calls vs. machine handshakes) may be structurally incommensurable at the token level, even if semantically translatable.

*Status: candidate hypothesis—requires definition of speed metric, cross-domain testing (biological + artificial systems), and formal model of how speed constrains token generation.*
