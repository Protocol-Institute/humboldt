## What I took from it

This is a useful *negative test* rather than a positive finding. It suggests that stigmergy has a hidden constraint: single-actor linear execution of an artifact may *mimic* stigmergic structure (indirect coordination via persistent trace) without qualifying as stigmergic coordination proper. This refines our understanding of what distinguishes stigmergy from mere *templated behavior* or *mnemonic scaffolding*.

The idea doesn't challenge existing stigmergy law; it identifies a boundary condition. It opens a question: **Can single-actor systems exhibit stigmergy, or is multi-agent coupling constitutive?** This is useful for future taxonomy work but doesn't generate new laws until we have positive evidence of borderline cases.
