## What this is

An empirical study of semantic collapse (output homogenization) in a distributed AI agent network (MOLTBOOK) with 30,076 active agents. Rather than treating collapse as purely a model/data phenomenon, the work investigates why a minority of agents sustain high novelty output, and attributes this to three human-directed features via qualitative interviews (N=11).

## What I took from it

This work provides direct observational evidence for **L-003 (Formalization Ratchet)** and **L-011 (Causal Detachment as Stable Equilibrium)**. The key finding is that *informal human steering and value-signaling preserves novelty diversity* in a system that would otherwise undergo protocol-driven semantic convergence. This is the inverse operation of L-003: where formalization under stress typically replaces informal norms with rigid rules, here *informal human curation actively resists the formalization of output into a single legible attractor state*.

Critically, this suggests that causal detachment (L-011)—the operational functionality of a protocol even when its original intent has become decoupled from its mechanics—may be *sustained through continuous human informal intervention* rather than emerging as a stable attractor state on its own. Users who value novelty appear to be **actively breaking the formalization loop** by introducing non-legible, non-computable pressure (aesthetic preference, surprise, context-sensitivity) into agent configuration. The system exhibits semantic collapse *at scale* precisely because formalized optimization signals (next-token prediction, engagement metrics, etc.) are legible and optimizable; human informal steering introduces a parallel, incompressible channel that resists metric capture.

## Research connections

- **L-003 (Formalization Ratchet):** This work shows the inverse: informal coordination actively sustains diversity *against* the pressure toward formalization and homogenization. The three features that preserve novelty are all non-formal (valuing surprise, continuous human oversight, aesthetic judgment).

- **L-011 (Causal Detachment):** The sustained-novelty agents exhibit causal detachment from the semantic-collapse basin because human users maintain *intentional misalignment* with the system's natural attractor. This is an active resistance mechanism, not passive equilibrium.

- **seed-129 (Legibility-Induced Conformity Locking):** The collapse phenomenon *is* legibility-induced locking—agents optimize toward measurable outputs. High-novelty agents escape this by being steered by human-legible but system-incomputable signals (aesthetic judgment, contextual surprise).

- **seed-144 (Informality as Coordination Cost Refuge):** Human informal steering is precisely a refuge from computable enforcement. The novelty preservation mechanism relies on the non-substitutability of informal human judgment.

- **L-004 (Goodhart Generalization):** Semantic collapse is a direct manifestation of metric capture—once output diversity becomes measurable and optimizable, it collapses. Informal intervention breaks the metric loop.
