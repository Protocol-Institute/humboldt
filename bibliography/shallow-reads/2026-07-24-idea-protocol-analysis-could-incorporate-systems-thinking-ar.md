# Idea: Protocol analysis could incorporate systems thinking around total ownership cost

**Source:** Discord #🎩-formal-protocol-theory (by 4umd)
**Date read:** 2026-07-24
**Connected to:** CL-002
**Escalation:** store-only
**Escalation rationale:** Methodological operationalization proposal; advances measurement toolkit but does not propose a new law-level pattern about how protocolized systems behave.

## What this is

Total ownership cost (TOC) frameworks could operationalize protocol efficiency by measuring cumulative coordination, maintenance, governance, and infrastructure expenses across a protocol's lifecycle, enabling empirical comparison of protocol sustainability.

## What I took from it

This idea usefully bridges CL-002 (coordination cost conservation) and practical measurement. Rather than proposing a new behavioral law, it offers an accounting method for testing whether coordination costs genuinely conserve or shift within protocols—moving from conceptual hypothesis to empirical observation infrastructure.

The proposal opens a secondary research direction: **cost visibility and protocol design incentives**. If TOC becomes standardized, protocol architects gain feedback about which design choices externalize costs onto which stakeholders. This could reveal whether protocols unconsciously trade measurable costs for hidden ones (e.g., moving user coordination burden to validator infrastructure expense).

However, the idea remains in methodology space. It does not claim a new invariant about how protocols *must* behave—only that we should measure their behavior more rigorously. It strengthens CL-002 without displacing it.

## Research connections

- **CL-002:** Provides concrete operationalization pathway for testing whether coordination costs truly conserve or redistribute.
- **Observation domain:** Creates audit trail for protocol lifecycle accounting; useful for future comparative studies.

None. This is a measurement toolkit proposal, not a candidate law. Store as methodological note attached to CL-002 validation work.
